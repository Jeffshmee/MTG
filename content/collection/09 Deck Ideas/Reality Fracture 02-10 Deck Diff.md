# Reality Fracture 02/10 Deck Diff

Every nonbasic, nontoken card from [[06 Browse/Booster Hauls/(5) Booster Haul 02-10-2026|Booster Haul 02/10/2026]] that is legal in that deck. Highest Combined first. Nothing here is a cut, and nothing has been sleeved.

Combined = 0.4×General + 0.6×Deck-Specific, rounded, from `commander-deckbuilding` and that deck's skill. Urza's General is how the card plays in a white-blue artifact deck, and Deck-Specific is Urza's {6} and Simulacrum Synthesizer. A name that already had a score in that deck's `07 Assets` keeps it.

Empower Jace puts loyalty on a Jace token with “−1: Surveil 1” and “−3: Draw a card.” If you do not have that token, empower creates it. It does not add loyalty to [**`mtg:The Theorist, Jace Beleren`**](https://scryfall.com/card/fra/43). Ways and “each planeswalker” effects do hit him. Behold a Jace counts him on the battlefield or revealed from your hand. The Kykar add is the planeswalker. The empower cards are the follow-ups.

Basics from the box, not ranked: Plains ×2, Island ×4, Swamp ×3, Mountain ×2, Forest ×4. Tokens stay out of these tables.
