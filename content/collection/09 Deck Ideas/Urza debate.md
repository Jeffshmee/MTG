## The argument

The fight was extra seats against the saved counts. Early game, mana ramp, replication, and protection each wanted a higher number. Tutors wanted the same 7. Recursion wanted the same 7 with one name changed. A gap of more than 5 Combined was treated as a swap, not a new seat. A gap of 5 was only a review. The proposal kept every job on its saved count. After the votes, nobody objected.

Early game objected to paying for Master of Etherium or Propaganda with Disenchant or Banishing Light, and objected to dropping Supreme Verdict, Liberator, Esper Sentinel, or Spellskite. The four swaps stood. Day of Judgment is Not Interested, so Supreme Verdict takes that seat at the same 88. Liberator replaces Mechan Assembler. Esper Sentinel replaces Quick Study. Spellskite replaces Cryoshatter. The two extra slots were refused. Propaganda was conceded: it ties Ghostly Prison, and it is exactly 5 over Banishing Light. Master was not dropped with it. Master 83 replaces Disenchant 71, a gap of 12, so that is a swap inside the 15, not a seventeenth card. Early game had refused that cut. The gap rule settled it, and early game did not object after the vote. Steel Hellkite stayed out. It was exactly 5 over Disenchant and only 4 over Banishing Light. Chief of the Foundry stays. Ghostly Prison and Banishing Light stay. Swords to Plowshares stays an order. Zurgo keeps that copy.

Mana ramp objected to cutting a basic, to falling under 51, and to losing Uthros, Smothering Tithe, Arc Reactor, Metalworker, or Semblance Anvil. Those five came in, and the count did not rise. Uthros replaces Wayfarer's Bauble, not an Island or a Plains. Not Interested removes Expedition Map, Talisman of Progress, and Gilded Lotus. Smothering Tithe replaces Gilded Lotus. Semblance Anvil replaces Hedron Archive. Arc Reactor and Metalworker refill the two Not Interested seats. The three-card push to 54 was conceded: Etherium Sculptor, then Cloud Key, then The Mightstone and Weakstone. Sculptor is exactly 5 over Foundry Inspector, a review. Azorius Signet and Inspiring Statuary stay out. Rings of Brighthearth stays in replication with Basalt Monolith. No other job gave up a card to pay for Uthros.

Replication objected to cutting the Moonlit package to fund Extravagant Replication, and to funding Uthros from this job. Steel Overseer is Not Interested and leaves. Echocasting Symposium 78 leaves because Anointed Procession and Phyrexian Metamorph, both 88, beat it by 10. That holds the job at 12. Extravagant Replication was the refused thirteenth. Panharmonicon and Starfield Vocalist do not beat a kept card by more than 5. Their Why notes only order second copies. Both stay out.

Simulacrum tutors did not ask for a seat. They objected to spending Inventors' Fair on Uthros, and to replacing Whir of Invention with Transmute Artifact. Transmute Artifact and Intuition are Not Interested. Tezzeret the Seeker stays a review, within 5 of Oswald Fiddlebender, Trophy Mage, and Inventors' Fair. The seven stay.

Flare of Fortitude is 1 over Blacksmith's Skill, so it stays out. Teferi's Protection is exactly 5 over Loran's Escape and 2 over Clever Concealment, so both stay. The job holds at 8.

Recursion objected to any cut except Buried Ruin, and to giving their seat to Uthros. Dance of the Manse 89 replaces Buried Ruin 81, a gap of 8, and takes only that seat. Academy Ruins, Refurbish, Emry, Hanna, Junk Diver, and Scrap Trawler stay. Argivian Find is exactly 5 over Buried Ruin, a review. The second-copy Why notes do not take a seat.
