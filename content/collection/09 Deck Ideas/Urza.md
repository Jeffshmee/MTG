# Urza

Deck theory for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Urza, Prince of Kroog|Urza, Prince of Kroog]] with [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Simulacrum Synthesizer|Simulacrum Synthesizer]] as the key card. Linked from [[01 - Commander Ranking#68. Urza, Prince of Kroog|rank 68]]. The ranking's sleeve and buy tables are a different 99 (artifact soldiers). This file is the Synthesizer plan.

Nothing here is sleeved. Ayli, Kykar, Arahbo, Maralen, and Zurgo stay 100. Urza, Prince of Kroog and Simulacrum Synthesizer stay in this plan. Every other card is negotiable. The six menus are allowed to be larger than 100.

**General** is how the card plays in a white-blue artifact Commander deck. **Deck-Specific** is how well it does the job of that menu for Urza's activated {6} and for Synthesizer. **Combined** = 0.4×General + 0.6×Deck-Specific, rounded. Within 5 Combined of another card in the same menu, the tick is a review, not an automatic swap.

<span class="urza-pip urza-pip-order" title="Order"></span> Order. <span class="urza-pip urza-pip-box" title="Box"></span> Box, owned and free to use. Where an owned copy is already in one of the five decks, the row is still an Order. That deck keeps its copy.

## How it plays

Hold a counter, tutor Synthesizer, and cast it once you can also cast something else. The something else has to be an artifact of mana value 3 or more, or the Synthesizer sits there and does nothing. Those same cards are the blockers and the removal until the copies start. Urza's {6} is an activated ability. It is not a trigger. Training Grounds and Heartstone are what make that {6} cheaper. The mana table is what pays it more than once.

Moonlit Meditation goes on Synthesizer, never on Urza. The first time you would create one or more tokens each turn, you may instead create that many copies of the enchanted Synthesizer. Declining also turns the choice off until next turn. Myr Battlesphere is the clean batch: stack the four Myr on top of the Construct trigger. Moonlit turns the four Myr into four Synthesizers. They enter together. The original sees all four, and each new one sees the other three, which is 16 Construct triggers, and the Construct from the original spell is still waiting under them. Mondrak doubles after that. Panharmonicon doubles the Construct trigger. Anointed Procession is not in this 100. An upkeep copy from Extravagant Replication or Mechanized Production is the first token of that turn, so cast those after Moonlit has already fired. Mechanized Production wins at eight artifacts named Synthesizer. Constructs have a different name, so they do not count.

The Ooze goes in, on that same Moonlit line, and the Mutagens buff the Constructs without being sacrificed. Each Construct is a 0/0 with "This token gets +1/+1 for each artifact you control." That checks the current number, so it changes whenever an artifact enters or leaves. A Mutagen is an artifact token. While it sits there, every Construct is one point larger. Its sacrifice is a second effect: pay {1}, tap it, and sacrifice it to put one +1/+1 counter on one creature, as a sorcery. The Ooze's leave ability counts those counters, not the Construct's power. Steel Overseer is what puts a counter on each artifact creature. Grow one creature with Overseer, let it leave, and stack that Ooze trigger on top of any others so Moonlit catches the big batch. Moonlit can instead turn the Mutagen event into that many Synthesizer copies, and then those Mutagens are not on the battlefield to be counted. The Ooze is mana value 2, and a Mutagen is mana value 0, so neither triggers Synthesizer. Do not exile your own Synthesizer with the tap. Tap The Ooze after Moonlit has already fired, or that single Mutagen spends the turn.

One Robot from Mechan Assembler, one Thopter from Sai, and one upkeep copy are the same trap. They spend Moonlit on a single token if they are first. Let the big batch happen, then make the small ones.

Basalt Monolith and Rings of Brighthearth pay the {6} as many times as you like. Tap the Monolith for {C}{C}{C}, pay {3} to untap it, and pay {2} so Rings copies that untap. Tap it again. You need {2} from another source to start, then the loop is infinite colorless, and the {6} is generic. Training Grounds cuts {2} and Heartstone cuts {1}. Together the {6} costs {3}, and the cost cannot go below {1}. Urza, Lord High Artificer taps any artifact for {U}. Unwinding Clock untaps your artifacts on each opponent's turn, and the {6} can be activated then. Guidelight Optimizer's {U} is allowed on an activated ability, so it pays this one. It is not allowed on a counterspell.

Constructs are mana value 0. They do not chain Synthesizer. Each one gets +1/+1 for each artifact you control, including every Mutagen and every other Construct, and that number updates. A token copy of Synthesizer keeps mana value 3, so every other copy makes a Construct when it enters. Urza's +2/+2 hits artifact creatures. The original noncreature Synthesizer does not get it. A Soldier copy does.
