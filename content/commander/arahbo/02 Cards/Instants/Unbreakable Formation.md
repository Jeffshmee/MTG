## Oracle Text

Creatures you control gain [[03 Effects/Indestructible|indestructible]] until end of turn.
Addendum — If you cast this spell during your main phase, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on each of those creatures and they gain [[03 Effects/Vigilance|vigilance]] until end of turn.
