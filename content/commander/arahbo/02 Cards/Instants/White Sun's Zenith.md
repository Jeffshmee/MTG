## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Create Token|Create X 2/2 white Cat creature tokens]]. Shuffle White Sun's Zenith into its owner's library.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Instants/White Sun's Zenith|White Sun's Zenith]] makes X 2/2 white Cat tokens and shuffles back. Instant. Those tokens are Cats for Banner / Regal / First Fang's anthem, but they are tokens — First Fang does not ETB-make extras. The shuffle-back is why you cast it as a dump, not as a one-shot.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast at the end of their turn if you can, or in combat after blockers. X = leftover white.
- Banner and Regal should already be in play so the 2/2s are real. Empty-board Zenith is a pile of 2/2s that die to a wrath.
- Does not trigger First Fang. Do not cast it as if it were Prideful Parent.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Tokens / anthem**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Token Cats do not trigger First Fang. Three white pips plus X — not a turn-3 play.
