## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]] is the sleeved general until Roar arrives. 2/2 Cat. Other Cats get +1/+1. Whenever he or another nontoken Cat you control enters, create a 1/1 white Cat. This pile is a go-wide Cat ETB pile. There is **no Eminence** on him. When Roar is in the command zone, First Fang stays in the 99.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play him after one Cat if you can — the ETB is a second token.
- Turn 2 after [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sacred Cat|Sacred Cat]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Savannah Lions|Savannah Lions]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Prowling Caracal|Prowling Caracal]].
- His own tokens are tokens — they do not ETB-make more Cats.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Nontoken Cats**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sacred Cat|Sacred Cat]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Savannah Lions|Savannah Lions]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Prideful Parent|Prideful Parent]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cat Collector|Cat Collector]]
- **Anthem**: [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]]
- **Landfall Cats**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Token Cats do not trigger his ETB. Retreat Cat Beasts are tokens.
- He is not Eminence. Do not play him as if Roar were in the zone.
