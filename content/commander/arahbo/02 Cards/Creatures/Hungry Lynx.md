## Oracle Text

Cats you control have protection from Rats. (They can't be blocked, targeted, or dealt damage by Rats.)
At the [[03 Effects/End Step|beginning of your end step]], target opponent creates a 1/1 black Rat creature token with deathtouch.
Whenever a Rat dies, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on each Cat you control.
