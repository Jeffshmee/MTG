## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Prideful Parent|Prideful Parent]] is a nontoken Cat that ETB-makes a 1/1 Cat. Two bodies; First Fang sees the Parent.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after First Fang if you can — Parent ETB is a First Fang token plus the 1/1.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
