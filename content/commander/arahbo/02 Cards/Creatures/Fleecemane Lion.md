## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Fleecemane Lion|Fleecemane Lion]] is a 3/3 Cat at {G}{W} that monstrosity 1 for {3}{G}{W}: hexproof and indestructible. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]] makes a 1/1 off the nontoken ETB. The monstrous body is the Cat you point Eminence at once Roar arrives; until then it is a protected attacker under First Fang / Banner.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play after First Fang if you can — nontoken Cat ETB is a 1/1.
- Monstrosity after it has attacked once, or in response to removal. Five mana is a lot on this curve — do not hold the Lion in hand waiting for the five.
- Hexproof/indestructible does not save the token First Fang made.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Cat engine**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]]
- **Protect**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]] *(owned extra)*, [[02 Cards/Instants/Unbreakable Formation|Unbreakable Formation]] *(owned extra)*

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Monstrosity is once. Do not pay it twice. Roar is still ordered — there is no Eminence pump on this pile.
