## Oracle Text

Ward {2}
Whenever Sovereign Okinec Ahau attacks, for each creature you control with power greater than that creature's base power, put a number of [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counters]] on that creature equal to the difference.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Sovereign Okinec Ahau|Sovereign Okinec Ahau]] is a 3/4 Cat Noble with ward {2}. Whenever it attacks, each creature you control with power above base gets that many +1/+1 counters. First Fang, Banner, and Regal all raise power above base — that is the trigger. Blank 3/1s (Caracal, Swiftclaw) do not get counters unless something already pumped them.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack only when Banner / First Fang / Regal is already raising power. Empty-board Okinec is a 3/4.
- First Fang makes a token off the nontoken ETB. The token has base 1/1; Banner makes it 2/2 and Okinec will count that.
- Do not chump it. Ward {{2}} is the save until Heroic is sleeved.
