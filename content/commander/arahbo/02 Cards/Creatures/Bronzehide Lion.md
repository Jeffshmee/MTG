## Oracle Text

{G}{W}: This creature gains [[03 Effects/Indestructible|indestructible]] until end of turn.
When this creature dies, return it to the battlefield. It's an Aura enchantment with enchant creature you control and "{G}{W}: Enchanted creature gains indestructible until end of turn," and it loses all other abilities.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Bronzehide Lion|Bronzehide Lion]] is a 3/3 Cat that can buy indestructible for {G}{W}, and when it dies it returns as an Aura that grants the same shield. First Fang sees the creature ETB, not the Aura. The second life is protection for another Cat (First Fang, Regal, Fleecemane).

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play after First Fang. Pay {{G}}{{W}} only if a kill spell is on the stack.
- When it dies, attach the Aura to First Fang or a Banner-pumped Cat, not a 1/1 token.
- Helping Hand does not return the Aura — it is not a creature card in the yard once it came back.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Cat / protect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Fleecemane Lion|Fleecemane Lion]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Helping Hand|Helping Hand]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The Aura is not a Cat. First Fang, Banner, and Regal do not see it.
