## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/ETB|When this creature enters, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a Food token]]]]. (It's an artifact with "{2}, {T}, Sacrifice this token: You [[03 Effects/Gain Life|gain 3 life]].")
Whenever you gain life for the first time during each of your turns, create a 1/1 white Cat creature token.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Cat Collector|Cat Collector]] ETB-makes a Food, then the first life gain each of your turns makes a 1/1 Cat. Not a blink target. Food is {2}, sac, gain 3 — that life is the Cat.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Crack Food on your turn if you have not gained yet. Sacred Cat / Sunstriker / Regal also turn it on.
