## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]] makes two 1/1 Cats and gives Cats +1/+1 and lifelink. Turns [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]] on.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Turn 4 if you can. The two tokens plus the anthem is the combat step.
- Lifelink on First Fang tokens feeds Pridemate and Collector.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Go-wide**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cat Collector|Cat Collector]]
