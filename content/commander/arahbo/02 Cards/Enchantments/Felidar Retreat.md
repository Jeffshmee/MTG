• [[03 Effects/Create Token|Create a 2/2 white Cat Beast creature token]].

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]] is landfall: a 2/2 Cat Beast or +1/+1 counters and vigilance on the team. The Cat Beast is a token — First Fang does not see it as a nontoken Cat.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it before a land drop. Default is the 2/2 until Banner / Regal make counters better.
- Evolving Wilds / a basic after it is a free Cat.
