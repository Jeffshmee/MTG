## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Lands/Selesnya Sanctuary|Selesnya Sanctuary]] enters tapped, bounces a land, taps for {G}{W}. The bounce can replay [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]] landfall. Not a turn-2 land.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Bounce a basic you already used, or Evolving Wilds if you still need the crack. Retreat sees the land returning next turn.
- Do not bounce your only untapped source on the turn you need First Fang.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes
