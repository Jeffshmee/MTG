## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Lands/Secluded Courtyard|Secluded Courtyard]] names a creature type. Untapped {C}, or one colour to cast a creature of that type. Name **Cat**. That mana casts First Fang, Regal, the Lions — not Banner, not Ajani, not Claws Out.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Name Cat as it enters. Use the coloured tap for a Cat spell, the colourless tap for Banner or a land drop.
- Does not cast Roar's attack trigger once he arrives — that is an ability, and the mana restriction is creature spells or creature abilities of the chosen type. Cat abilities are legal.
