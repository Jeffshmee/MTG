## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Sorceries/Split Up|Split Up]] is a wrath that hits tapped or untapped. Your tokens die. Use it when their board is the problem and yours can rebuild off First Fang.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Do not fire it into your own wide board unless you are dead anyway.
- Choose the mode that hits their team. If they are tapped-out attackers, destroy tapped; if they passed with blockers up, destroy untapped.
- Helping Hand gets First Fang back if he died. Token Cats stay dead.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Rebuild**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Helping Hand|Helping Hand]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Tokens die. First Fang does not come back by himself.
