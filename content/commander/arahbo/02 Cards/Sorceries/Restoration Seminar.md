## Oracle Text

Return target nonland permanent card from your graveyard to the battlefield.
Paradigm (Then [[03 Effects/Exile|exile this spell]]. After you first resolve a spell with this name, you may cast a copy of it from exile [[03 Effects/Cast Without Paying|without paying its mana cost at the beginning of each of your first main phases]].)

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Sorceries/Restoration Seminar|Restoration Seminar]] returns any nonland permanent from the yard to the battlefield, then Paradigm exiles it and lets you copy it for free at each first main. Priority: First Fang, Banner, Regal, Retreat. Not a 1/1 Cat.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after a wipe. Default return is First Fang (rebuild tokens) or Banner (anthem).
- The free copy next main is a second return — hold a second target in the yard if you can.
- Helping Hand is the cheap creature-only twin. Seminar hits Banner and Retreat.
