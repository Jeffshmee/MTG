## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]] is the anthem in this pile, not a Signet. Name **Cat**. Other Cats get +1/+1, and it taps for one mana of any colour. Ramp in this pile is still lands — the Banner is the lord.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play after a Cat or two. Name Cat, always.
- Stacks with First Fang's +1/+1 and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]]'s +1/+1 / lifelink.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]]
- **Cats**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]]
- **Sleeve next**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Mirari's Wake|Mirari's Wake]] *(owned extra)*, [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Intangible Virtue|Intangible Virtue]] *(owned extra)*, [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Banner of Kinship|Banner of Kinship]] *(owned extra)*
