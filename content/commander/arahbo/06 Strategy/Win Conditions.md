## 1. Eminence pump (primary once Roar arrives)

**How it wins:** Roar sits in the command zone. Each combat, another Cat gets +3/+3. A wide board of 1/1s becomes 4/4s. If he is cast, `{1}{G}{W}` on attack gives trample and +X/+X.

**Pending:** [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]] is 🟡 Ordered. Do not sleeve a proxy.

**Live bodies waiting for it:** First Fang tokens, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]] Cat Beasts, the one-drops.

Until he arrives this plan does not exist. Play plan 2.

## How to choose a plan in-game

- **Roar is still in the mail** → plan 2. First Fang + Banner + Regal. Tunnel the biggest Cat.
- **Roar is in the command zone** → plan 1 in the background of plan 2. You do not have to cast him.
- **Pridemate is 6/6 and they cannot chump** → plan 3 for that player. Do not wait for it to be 15/15 if combat is already lethal.
