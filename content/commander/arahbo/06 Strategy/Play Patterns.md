# Play Patterns

How this **owned 48** is supposed to play. The vault commander is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, Roar of the World|Arahbo, Roar of the World]] — still 🟡 Ordered. Until that copy is in hand, the sleeved general is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang|Arahbo, the First Fang]]. Every line is judged by whether it is a **Cat**, makes a Cat, or pumps Cats.

See also [[06 Strategy/Win Conditions|Win Conditions]], [[06 Strategy/Playtest Notes|Playtest Notes]], and [[01 Deck/Current Deck|Current Deck]].

**Status of this draft:** written against the **48** sleeved 2026-09-10. Target is 100. Lords, extra token makers, and green ramp are still 🟢 owned extras on [[01 Deck/Sideboard|Sideboard]]. Fleecemane, Bronzehide, Okinec, Helpful Hunter, and Temple Garden are live. Do not sleeve a proxy for Roar. Do not take a last copy out of Maralen, Zurgo, or Kykar.

---

## The engine (two generals)

**Until Roar arrives — First Fang**

First Fang is a 2/2 Cat. When it or another nontoken Cat you control enters, create a 1/1 white Cat. Other Cats get +1/+1. This pile is a go-wide Cat ETB pile. There is **no Eminence**.

**Once Roar is in the command zone**

Eminence pumps another Cat +3/+3 at the beginning of combat whether Roar is cast or not. The attack trigger (`{1}{G}{W}`: trample and +X/+X, X = that Cat's power) needs him on the battlefield. First Fang stays in the 99 as the token engine; he is not the commander.

[[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]] — name Cat. It is the anthem in this pile. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat|Felidar Retreat]] is the landfall: a 2/2 Cat Beast or counters + vigilance.

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Pridemate]] goes tall off life. Live life: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]] (Cats get lifelink), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sacred Cat|Sacred Cat]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Sunstriker|Sunstriker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cat Collector|Cat Collector]] (Food, then a Cat the first time you gain life each turn).

## Opening and curve

**Keep** a hand that makes `{G}` or `{W}` and plays a Cat on 1–2.

- Untapped: [[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]], Plains, Forest. [[02 Cards/Lands/Temple of Plenty|Temple of Plenty]] scries; it is a third land. [[02 Cards/Lands/Selesnya Guildgate|Guildgate]] and [[02 Cards/Lands/Blossoming Sands|Blossoming Sands]] are tapped — not the second land.
- [[02 Cards/Lands/Secret Tunnel|Secret Tunnel]] is colourless. Do not count it as `{G}` or `{W}`. The `{4}` ability makes two Cats unblockable after Eminence (or after Banner) has pumped them.
- Turn 1: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sacred Cat|Sacred Cat]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Savannah Lions|Savannah Lions]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Prowling Caracal|Prowling Caracal]].
- Turn 2: First Fang if you already have a Cat, or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Prideful Parent|Prideful Parent]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Oreskos Swiftclaw|Oreskos]] / Pridemate / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dawnwing Marshal|Dawnwing Marshal]].
- Turn 3: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cat Collector|Cat Collector]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Felidar Savior|Felidar Savior]], Banner, Retreat if a land is coming.
- Turn 4: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal|Regal Caracal]] (two lifelink Cats + anthem). [[02 Cards/Planeswalkers/Ajani, Caller of the Pride|Ajani]] if the board can use +1.

**Mulligan** hands with no white Cat and no `{W}`, or two tapped duals and no one-drop.

Ramp in this pile is **lands only**. [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Helping Hand|Helping Hand]] is the cheap recur, not ramp. [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Cultivate|Cultivate]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Three Visits|Three Visits]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Lore|Nature's Lore]] are still on SB — sleeve one before the curve feels honest.

## Sequencing this pile

Default: Cat, First Fang, another Cat, Banner or Retreat, Regal.

| Priority | Card | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arahbo, the First Fang\|First Fang]] | Token on every nontoken Cat ETB. Play it after one Cat if you can. |
| 2 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sacred Cat\|Sacred Cat]] / one-drops | Fuel for First Fang. Embalm is a second Cat later. |
| 3 | [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner\|Patchwork Banner]] | Name Cat. The only anthem in the 32. |
| 4 | [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Felidar Retreat\|Felidar Retreat]] | Landfall 2/2 or team counters. Play it before a land drop. |
| 5 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Regal Caracal\|Regal Caracal]] | Two tokens + lifelink on Cats. Turns Pridemate on. |
| 6 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cat Collector\|Cat Collector]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Prideful Parent\|Prideful Parent]] | Extra Cat ETBs. Collector also makes Food. |
| 7 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate\|Pridemate]] | Tall off the lifelink pile. |
| 8 | [[02 Cards/Planeswalkers/Ajani, Caller of the Pride\|Ajani]] | +1 on the biggest Cat. −8 is a pile of 2/2s if you ever get there. |
| 9 | [[02 Cards/Instants/Claws Out\|Claws Out]] | Pump / fight. Convoke off the tokens. |

[[02 Cards/Lands/Secret Tunnel|Secret Tunnel]] after you have two Cats you want to connect.

When Roar arrives: leave him in the command zone for Eminence. Cast him only if you have `{1}{G}{W}` up for the attack trigger the same turn, or if First Fang has died and you need a body.

## Common mistakes

- Counting Secret Tunnel as `{G}` or `{W}`.
- Casting First Fang with no other Cat in hand and no follow-up.
- Sleeving a proxy for Roar. Wait for the Cardmarket copy.
- Taking the last Path of Ancestry / Banner / Three Visits out of Maralen.
- Putting 🟡 Ordered into current-deck before it is in hand.
- Treating this pile as an Eminence deck. Eminence is off until Roar is in the command zone.
