## Deck Role & Rating

Solemn Simulacrum is blinkable ramp at {4}: ETB a basic tapped, death-draw. Blink it with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]] for another land. Physical copy unsleeved from Zurgo SB (TDC 325) — named, so the spare-copy block does not apply.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on four after Ring / Bauble, or as the third ramp spell. Finds Island or Plains tapped.
- Blink it for a second basic. Death-draw is extra, not the reason the slot stays.
