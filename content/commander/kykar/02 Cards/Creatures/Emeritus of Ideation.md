## Deck Role & Rating

Emeritus of Ideation is a {3}{U}{U} 5/5 flyer with ward {2} that **enters prepared**. While prepared you may cast Ancestral Recall ({U}, draw three). That instant is a [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar]] trigger. Blink it with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]] and it enters prepared again. Attack and exile eight from the graveyard to re-prepare without blinking. Commander-legal: the banned card is standalone Ancestral Recall, not this prepare creature (Scryfall: Legal in Commander). Filled into the owned pile from the 02/09/2026 haul — no cut.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast, then spend {U} on Recall the same turn if you can. Kykar triggers on the instant.
- Blink it after you have spent the first Recall to get a second.
- Attack to re-prepare if the graveyard is fat enough (eight cards).
- Keep a blue source untapped for Recall after the five-mana cast.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]
- **Blink (live)**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Charming Prince|Charming Prince]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Five mana and {U}{U}. The 21-land pile can stumble on the Recall the same turn.
- Recall unprepares it; you need a blink or the attack trigger (exile eight from the graveyard) for another copy.
- Ancestral Recall as a standalone spell is banned; this prepare creature is legal.
