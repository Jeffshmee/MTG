## Deck Role & Rating

Justice, Vance Astrovik is bounce at {2}{U}: tokens that leave cease to exist, and a creature that leaves and returns is a new object with no counters. Point it at the engine (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord), not one 1/1. It is a noncreature spell, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Sleeved 29/09/2026. MSH 61 foil over Mocking Sprite FDN 159. Maralen keeps FDN 159. The to-do link said FDN 744.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes
