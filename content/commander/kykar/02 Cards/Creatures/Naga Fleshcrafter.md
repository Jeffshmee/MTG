You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature on the battlefield.
Renew — {2}{U}, [[03 Effects/Exile|Exile this card from your graveyard: Put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on target nonlegendary creature you control]]. Each other creature you control becomes a copy of that creature until end of turn. Activate only as a sorcery.

## Deck Role & Rating

Naga enters as a copy of any creature on the battlefield — opponents included, unlike [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glasspool Mimic|Glasspool Mimic]]. Blink it to copy something else. Renew from the yard is a one-shot clone overlay. Live in the 70.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
