## Deck Role & Rating

Selfless Spirit is a Spirit two-drop that trades itself so the rest of the board lives through a wrath. Dawn's Truce is the other protection; this one is a body Kykar can also make a Spirit token beside. In the owned 98. Mark trade 03/09.

## Play Patterns & Lines
