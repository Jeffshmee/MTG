## Deck Role & Rating

Glasspool enters as a copy of a creature you control. Copy Channeler or Hunter, then blink the Mimic to copy something else. Back face is a tapped Island if you need the land. Live in the 65.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
