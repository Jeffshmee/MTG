## Deck Role & Rating

Skycoach is the instant blink in the 65. Flash body, enters prepared; All Aboard exiles a non-Pilot you control and returns it immediately — combat-speed ETB, not Kykar's end-step delay. Blink Channeler, Hunter, or Cloudblazer on their attack. The prepared copy is a second All Aboard later. Not a Pilot, so he cannot All Aboard himself.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after an ETB creature is in play. Empty-board blink is a miss.
- Instant-speed (this card, Skycoach, Kitten) on their attack; Kykar/Thassa wait until end of step.
- Priority targets: Channeler (bounce the engine), Hunter / Cloudblazer (draw), Banishing Light (new exile).

## Anti-synergies / Notes
