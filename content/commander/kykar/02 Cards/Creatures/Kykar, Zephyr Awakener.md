## Deck Role & Rating

Kykar is the engine: every noncreature spell lets you delayed-blink another creature until the next end step, or make a 1/1 flying Spirit. The blink lane is the plan — replay [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]]. He cannot blink himself. Instant blink ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]]'s All Aboard, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Displacer Kitten|Displacer Kitten]] once bought) is the combat trick; his own blink is late. Spirit mode is backup if nothing is worth blinking.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Curve him on 4 off a rock or a dual. The turn he enters, have a cantrip (Opt) so you blink Channeler / Hunter the same turn or make a Spirit.
- Default mode is blink, not Spirit, while an ETB creature is in play. Spirit only if the board is empty of blink targets.
- His blink returns at the next end step — not in time for blocks. Skycoach All Aboard or Kitten (buy) for combat.
- He cannot blink himself. Dawn's Truce is the live save; Boots is buy-rank 26 (Greaves locked).
- Combo turn: he is optional. Deadeye + Drake does not need him. Hold a counter for the interaction window.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Your own tokens that leave (Kykar Spirit, Channeler Bird) cease. Do not blink a token expecting it back.
- Kykar's blink is delayed. Do not block with the blinked creature; it is gone until end of turn.
