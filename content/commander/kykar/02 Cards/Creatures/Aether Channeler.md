## Deck Role & Rating

Channeler is the best bounce body in the 65. Modal ETB: bounce any other nonland, draw, or a 1/1 Bird. Default is bounce the token engine; draw if the board is empty. Blink him with Kykar, Thassa, or Skycoach. Dump piece once Deadeye or Flicker + Drake is live — infinite bounce or draw.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
