## Deck Role & Rating

Harbinger of the Tides is bounce at {U}{U}: tokens that leave cease to exist, and a creature that leaves and returns is a new object with no counters. Point it at the engine (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord), not one 1/1. It is a noncreature spell, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Owned. ETB bounce a tapped creature. Flash for {2} more.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
