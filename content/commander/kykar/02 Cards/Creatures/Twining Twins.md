## Oracle Text

Flying, vigilance, ward {1}

Swift Spiral {1}{W}: Exile target nontoken creature. Return it to the battlefield under its owner's control at the beginning of the next end step.

## Deck Role & Rating

WOE 296. Swift Spiral is Cloudshift at {1}{W}, delayed to the next end step. The 4/4 has no ETB, so blinking the body does not replay a trigger.

## Play Patterns & Lines

Cast Swift Spiral to blink a bounce creature, then cast the 4/4 later. Both spells are casts. Only the Adventure triggers Kykar.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]

- **Instant blink**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Cloudshift|Cloudshift]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Ephemerate|Ephemerate]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes
