## Deck Role & Rating

Sygg is unblockable. ETB or transform into Wisdom grants combat-damage draw until end of turn; blink retriggers the grant. The back face is protection from each colour. Combat-first, not bounce. Live in the 70.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
