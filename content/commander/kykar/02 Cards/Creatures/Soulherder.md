## Oracle Text

Whenever a creature is exiled from the battlefield, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on this creature.
At the [[03 Effects/End Step|beginning of your end step]], you may [[03 Effects/Exile|[[03 Effects/Blink|exile another target creature you control, then return that card to the battlefield under its owner's control]]]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Soulherder blinks a creature you control ({1}{W}{U}). That is instant or delayed ETB replay — hit [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]] so the bounce or draw happens again. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]'s own blink waits until end of step; this card is how you catch combat. End-step blink plus a +1/+1 counter. Extra Kykar-like blink without a spell. It is not in the owned 65 yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after an ETB creature is in play. Empty-board blink is a miss.
- Instant-speed (this card, Skycoach, Kitten) on their attack; Kykar/Thassa wait until end of step.
- Priority targets: Channeler (bounce the engine), Hunter / Cloudblazer (draw), Banishing Light (new exile).
- Not in the owned 65. Skycoach All Aboard is the live instant blink.
