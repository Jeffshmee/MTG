## Deck Role & Rating

Kitesail's ETB turns one artifact or creature per player into a Treasure until it leaves. Tokens they own cease if you later bounce the Treasure. Blink it to steal a new set. Ward {1} keeps it around. Live in the 65.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
