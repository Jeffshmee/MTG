## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/High Fae Trickster|High Fae Trickster]] is how this list casts at instant speed. Flash on a 4/2 flyer, and **every spell** may be cast as though it had flash — [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Ponder|Ponder]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Farewell|Farewell]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Sol Ring|Sol Ring]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Peregrine Drake|Peregrine Drake]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]]. That is the combat-trick window Kykar's own blink does not have (his is delayed to the next end step). [[02 Cards/Creatures/Displacer Kitten|Displacer Kitten]] still blinks on each noncreature; Trickster lets you *cast* the noncreature on their turn. Cut [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Tocasia's Welcome|Tocasia's Welcome]] for this slot.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold {3}{U}. Flash Trickster on their turn, then Channeler / Deputy / Drake / Ephemerate / Ghostly Flicker before they rebuild.
- With Trickster in play, Ponder and Stock Up are instants. Sol Ring on their end step is a Kykar trigger they did not play around.
- Deadeye + Drake still needs {1}{U} up. Trickster does not pay for the loop — it lets you assemble it at instant speed.
- Empty hand is a 4/2 flyer. Do not lead Trickster on your turn 4 if you have no follow-up.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- {3}{U} to hold. A tapped-out Trickster is a 4/2 that dies to two damage.
- Sorcery-speed only cards (Renew on Naga, some land drops) stay sorceries for *them*; you still cannot play a land on their turn.
- Does not give Kykar flash on his delayed blink — the creature still returns at end step.
