## Deck Role & Rating

Day of Judgment is a wrath at {2}{W}{W}. Tokens die; counters on real creatures die with the bodies. Bounce [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] or a payoff first if you have the mana. Owned wrath. Tokens die. Real creatures with counters die too.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
