## Deck Role & Rating

Seven-mana mass creature bounce, then an optional wheel, then exile itself. Tokens cease. Kykar still triggers. Heavier than River's Rebuke; sleeved from the pile. Live in the 70.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
- Mass bounce hits you too (Flood of Tears) unless it says target player (River's Rebuke). Bounce four of yours on Tears to cheat a permanent in.
