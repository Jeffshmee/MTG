## Deck Role & Rating

Boomerang Basics is a {U} Lesson. Noncreature, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Bounce any nonland — token engines, planeswalkers, and auras Unsummon cannot touch. If you bounce your own [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], you also draw. Sorcery speed: it does not catch combat. It replaced Opt as the one-mana spell that still feeds Kykar and answers a board.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Bounce your own ETB when you need the card more than the body.
- Main phase only. Combat bounce is [[02 Cards/Instants/Unsummon|Unsummon]] or [[02 Cards/Instants/Into the Flood Maw|Flood Maw]].

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]
- **Replay**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]]
- **Mass bounce (live)**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/River's Rebuke|River's Rebuke]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Flood of Tears|Flood of Tears]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Aetherize|Aetherize]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Sorcery. Cannot bounce in response to an attack.
- Drawing requires bouncing a permanent you control — do not bounce their engine expecting a card.
