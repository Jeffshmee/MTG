## Deck Role & Rating

River's Rebuke is bounce at {4}{U}{U}: tokens that leave cease to exist, and a creature that leaves and returns is a new object with no counters. Point it at the engine (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord), not one 1/1. It is a noncreature spell, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Owned. One player's nonlands all bounce. Tokens cease; every counter on that board resets.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
- Mass bounce hits you too (Flood of Tears) unless it says target player (River's Rebuke). Bounce four of yours on Tears to cheat a permanent in.
