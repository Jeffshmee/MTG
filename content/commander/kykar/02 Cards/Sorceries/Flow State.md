## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Look at Top|Look at the top three cards of your library]]. Put one of them into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in any order. If there is an instant card and a sorcery card in your graveyard, instead put two of them into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in any order.
