## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Bounce|Return all nonland permanents to their owners' hands]]. If you return four or more nontoken permanents you control this way, you may put a permanent card from your hand onto the battlefield.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Flood of Tears is bounce at {4}{U}{U}: tokens that leave cease to exist, and a creature that leaves and returns is a new object with no counters. Point it at the engine (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord), not one 1/1. It is a noncreature spell, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Owned. Bounce all nonlands. If you bounce four of yours, cheat a permanent in.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
- Mass bounce hits you too (Flood of Tears) unless it says target player (River's Rebuke). Bounce four of yours on Tears to cheat a permanent in.
