## Deck Role & Rating

Tranquil Cove is a mana source for this Azorius pile. Owned. Gain-1 tapped dual. The live base is Island ×8 / Plains ×6 plus [[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]]; duals are almost all on the buy list ([[02 Cards/Lands/Hallowed Fountain|Hallowed Fountain]], Floodfarm Verge). Do not treat this as a spell that triggers [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]].

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sequence it so Kykar is {W}{U} on time. Live base is 8 Island / 6 Plains plus Tower.
- Tapped duals are keepable while the buy list still holds Fountain / Otawara / Verge. Do not flood on Guildgate plus Cove plus Temple.
