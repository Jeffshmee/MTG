## Deck Role & Rating

Meticulous Archive is a mana source for this Azorius pile. Surveil dual. The live base is Island ×8 / Plains ×6 plus [[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]]; duals are almost all on the buy list ([[02 Cards/Lands/Hallowed Fountain|Hallowed Fountain]], Floodfarm Verge).
