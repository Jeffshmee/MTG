## Deck Role & Rating

Essence Flux blinks a creature you control ({U}). That is instant or delayed ETB replay — hit [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]] so the bounce or draw happens again. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]'s own blink waits until end of step; this card is how you catch combat. Instant blink a creature you control. Puts a +1/+1 counter on Spirits/Illusions. It is not in the owned 65 yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after an ETB creature is in play. Empty-board blink is a miss.
- Instant-speed (this card, Skycoach, Kitten) on their attack; Kykar/Thassa wait until end of step.
- Priority targets: Channeler (bounce the engine), Hunter / Cloudblazer (draw), Banishing Light (new exile).
- Not in the owned 65. Skycoach All Aboard is the live instant blink.
