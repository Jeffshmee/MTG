## Deck Role & Rating

Stroke of Midnight ({2}{W}). Owned. Exile a nonland, they get a 1/1. Answers engines and counter piles on one body. Judge it by whether it triggers [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] or is worth blinking.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
