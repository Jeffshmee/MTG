## Deck Role & Rating

Cloudshift blinks a creature you control ({W}). That is instant or delayed ETB replay — hit Aether Channeler, Helpful Hunter, or Cloudblazer so the bounce or draw happens again. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]'s own blink waits until end of step; this card is how you catch combat. Sleeved 29/09/2026. A25 7 over Daydream SOS 9. Instant blink. Daydream was the sorcery blink. Lands stayed 26.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after an ETB creature is in play. Empty-board blink is a miss.
- Instant-speed (this card, Skycoach, Kitten) on their attack; Kykar/Thassa wait until end of step.
- Priority targets: Channeler (bounce the engine), Hunter / Cloudblazer (draw), Banishing Light (new exile).

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]
- **Plan**: Blink spells

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Kykar's blink is delayed. Do not block with the blinked creature; it is gone until end of turn.
