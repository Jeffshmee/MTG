## Deck Role & Rating

Run Away Together is bounce at {1}{U}: tokens that leave cease to exist, and a creature that leaves and returns is a new object with no counters. Point it at the engine (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord), not one 1/1. It is a noncreature spell, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Owned. Bounces one of yours too. Weak wipe. It is not in the owned 65 yet. Owned extra — sitting on the sideboard, not a default 65 include.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
- Not in the owned 65. Unsummon / Roil / Flood Maw are the live spot bounce.
