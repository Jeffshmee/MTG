## Oracle Text

Gift a tapped Fish (You may promise an opponent a gift as you cast this spell. If you do, they [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a tapped 1/1 blue Fish creature token before its other effects]].)
[[03 Effects/Bounce|Return target creature an opponent controls to its owner's hand]]. If the gift was promised, instead return target nonland permanent an opponent controls to its owner's hand.

## Deck Role & Rating

Into the Flood Maw is bounce at {U}: tokens that leave cease to exist, and a creature that leaves and returns is a new object with no counters. Point it at the engine (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord), not one 1/1. It is a noncreature spell, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] triggers. Owned. Bounce a creature. Do not gift the Fish against a token deck.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Point it at the token maker or the counter lord, not a 1/1.
- Tokens that bounce cease. Real creatures come back as new objects — counters gone.
- Noncreature: Kykar triggers. Cantrip bounce (Repeal, Roil kicked) is also a card.
