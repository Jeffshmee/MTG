## Oracle Text

Enchant creature
Enchanted creature loses all abilities and is a green and white Citizen creature with base power and toughness 1/1 named Legitimate Businessperson. (It loses all other colors, card types, creature types, and names.)

## Deck Role & Rating

Witness Protection ({U}). Owned. Turns a threat into a 1/1 Citizen and strips abilities/counters as a type change overlay — still bounce the engine if they grow it. Judge it by whether it triggers [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] or is worth blinking.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on curve if it is a noncreature (Kykar trigger) or an ETB worth blinking.
- Blink targets in the 65: Channeler, Hunter, Cloudblazer, Charming Prince, Overseer, Kitesail, Banishing Light.
