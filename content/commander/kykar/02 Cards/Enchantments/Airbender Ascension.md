## Oracle Text

When this enchantment enters, airbend up to one target creature.
Whenever a creature you control enters, put a quest counter on this enchantment.
At the beginning of your end step, if this enchantment has four or more quest counters on it, exile up to one target creature you control, then return it to the battlefield under its owner's control.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Airbender Ascension is a cheap enchantment (Kykar trigger) that airbends on ETB — a token that is exiled that way ceases to exist — then counts your creature ETBs. At four quest counters it is a second end-step blink next to [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]] and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Teleportation Circle|Teleportation Circle]]. Sleeved 04/09. In the owned 98.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it to airbend their engine token-maker, or to start the quest count.
- Kykar Spirits, Channeler, Hunter, and blinks all put counters on it.
- Once at four, blink Channeler / Hunter / Loran each end step.

## Key Synergies
