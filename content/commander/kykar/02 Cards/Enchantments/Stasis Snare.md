## Deck Role & Rating

Flash exile a creature until Stasis Snare leaves. Noncreature, so Kykar triggers. Blink it for a new target; tokens they lose do not return. Sleeved FDN 581 from Box. Stormplain Detainment is the Banishing Light slot (any nonland); this one is creature-only and can come down at instant speed.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold it for an attacker, or cast it on curve as a Kykar trigger.
- Blink it: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] (end step), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]] (end step), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]] (instant All Aboard).
- Creature-only. Stormplain still covers noncreature permanents.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]
- **Blink (live)**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Charming Prince|Charming Prince]]
- **Same job**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Stormplain Detainment|Stormplain Detainment]] (any nonland, no flash)

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does not hit planeswalkers, artifacts, or enchantments — that is Stormplain's job.
- If this leaves, the creature returns. Do not bounce your own Snare unless you want a new exile.
