## Oracle Text

If an artifact or creature entering causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers [[03 Effects/Double Trigger|an additional time]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

The ETB doubler this blink list is built around. Blink [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Solemn Simulacrum|Solemn Simulacrum]] and they enter twice. Maralen has the only copy — **buy another**; do not unsleeve that 100. Buy-rank 9. Wishlist until it is in hand. Casting it is a Kykar trigger. Kykar cannot blink it (artifact, not a creature).

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on four after Kykar or a payload creature is in play.
- Does **not** double enchantment ETBs ([[02 Cards/Enchantments/Stormplain Detainment|Stormplain Detainment]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Stasis Snare|Stasis Snare]]). [[02 Cards/Creatures/Starfield Vocalist|Starfield Vocalist]] does.
- Stacks with Vocalist: one Channeler blink is three bounces.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]
- **Blink (live)**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa, Deep-Dwelling]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Charming Prince|Charming Prince]]
- **ETB payoffs (live)**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- In the owned 88.
