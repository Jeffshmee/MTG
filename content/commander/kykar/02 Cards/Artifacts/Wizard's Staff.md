## Deck Role & Rating

Hang it on [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]] for {1}. He cannot blink himself, so it stays on. Every Opt / Unsummon / rock then fires him twice: two delayed blinks (two ETBs) or blink plus a Spirit. That is extra ETBs via extra Kykar triggers, not Panharmonicon. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Starfield Vocalist|Starfield Vocalist]] still doubles the ETB of the creature that entered. Buy-rank 11. Wishlist — not in hand.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it ({1}{U} is a Kykar trigger), then equip Kykar for {1}.
- Do not put it on Channeler / Hunter. The next blink drops it.
- With Kitten later: instant blinks can restack the same body on the second trigger. Kykar's delayed blink cannot — Channeler is in exile until end step.
