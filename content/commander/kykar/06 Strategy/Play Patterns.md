# Play Patterns

How this **owned 72** is supposed to play. Commander is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kykar, Zephyr Awakener|Kykar, Zephyr Awakener]]. Every line is judged by whether it is a **noncreature spell** (Kykar trigger) or an **ETB worth blinking** (bounce, draw, exile).

See also [[06 Strategy/Win Conditions|Win Conditions]] and [[01 Deck/Current Deck|Current Deck]].

**Status of this draft:** written against the **72** sleeved (art scan plus Stasis Snare and Solemn Simulacrum). Target is 100. Combo pieces, Kitten, Ephemerate, Aetherize, Vocalist, Reflector Mage, and **Arcane Signet** (buy) are **wishlist**. Duals are almost all on the buy list.

---

## The engine

Kykar has two modes on every noncreature spell:

1. **Delayed blink** — exile another creature you control; it returns at the beginning of the **next end step**.
2. **Spirit** — a 1/1 white Spirit with flying.

Default is blink while [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aether Channeler|Aether Channeler]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cloudblazer|Cloudblazer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Charming Prince|Charming Prince]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kitesail Larcenist|Kitesail Larcenist]] is in play. Spirit only if the board is empty of blink targets.

He **cannot blink himself**. Instant combat blink is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Skycoach Conductor|Skycoach Conductor]]'s All Aboard (live) and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Displacer Kitten|Displacer Kitten]] / [[02 Cards/Instants/Ephemerate|Ephemerate]] (buy). [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thassa, Deep-Dwelling|Thassa]] is a second end-step blink without a spell.

Tokens that leave cease to exist. A creature that leaves and returns is a new object — **every counter on it is gone**. Point bounce at the **engine**, not a 1/1.

## Opening and curve

**Keep** a hand that can make {W}{U} and cast Kykar on 4 (sooner with [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Sol Ring|Sol Ring]]).

- Untapped: [[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]], Island, Plains. Tapped duals ([[02 Cards/Lands/Azorius Guildgate|Guildgate]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Temple of Enlightenment|Temple]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Tranquil Cove|Cove]]) are keepable as the third land, not the second.
- Turn 1–2: [[02 Cards/Instants/Opt|Opt]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Helpful Hunter|Helpful Hunter]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Charming Prince|Charming Prince]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Spectral Sailor|Spectral Sailor]].
- Turn 3: Channeler, Skycoach, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Harbinger of the Tides|Harbinger of the Tides]], a rock.
- Turn 4: Kykar. Have a cantrip so you blink the same turn.

**Mulligan** hands with no blue, or two tapped duals and no rock.

Ramp is **3** (Ring, Bauble, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Solemn Simulacrum|Solemn Simulacrum]]). [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Arcane Signet|Arcane Signet]] is **wishlist** — buy a copy; do not unsleeve Zurgo or Maralen. Short of 8–12 until Azorius Signet / Talisman arrive. Do not cut Ring.

## Bounce — engine, not a 1/1

Friends make tokens **and** +1/+1 counters.

- Token leaves → ceases. [[02 Cards/Instants/Unsummon|Unsummon]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Into the Roil|Into the Roil]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Into the Flood Maw|Flood Maw]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bigfin Bouncer|Bigfin]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Harbinger of the Tides|Harbinger]] (tapped creature), Channeler.
- Creature leaves and returns → new object, no counters. Same spells.
- Spot the **engine** (Adeline, Lathril, a counter lord).
- Mass: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/River's Rebuke|River's Rebuke]] (one player), [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Flood of Tears|Flood of Tears]] (everyone; cheat a permanent in if you bounce four of yours). [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Day of Judgment|Day of Judgment]] if they rebuilt.
- **Aetherize** is not in hand (buy-rank 3). That is the instant attack wipe.

Do not gift the Fish on Flood Maw against a token deck.

## Combo sequencing (pending)

None of the combo pieces are in the 72. When they arrive:

1. **Two-card:** [[02 Cards/Creatures/Deadeye Navigator|Deadeye Navigator]] soulbond [[02 Cards/Creatures/Peregrine Drake|Peregrine Drake]]. {1}{U} flicker Drake, untap five, net mana, repeat.
2. **Three-card:** [[02 Cards/Instants/Ghostly Flicker|Ghostly Flicker]] + [[02 Cards/Creatures/Archaeomancer|Archaeomancer]] + Drake. Flicker both; Archaeomancer returns Flicker; Drake pays.
3. **Dump:** infinite Channeler bounce, or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mulldrifter|Mulldrifter]] draw.

You still have to draw both, cast both, and not get stopped. Hold [[02 Cards/Instants/An Offer You Can't Refuse|Offer]] or [[02 Cards/Instants/Lofty Denial|Lofty Denial]]. Kykar is optional on the combo turn.

Felidar Guardian / Saheeli stays **off**.

## Protection

Live: [[02 Cards/Instants/Dawn's Truce|Dawn's Truce]] (gift for hexproof; gift+indestructible through a wipe). Greaves is locked in the other 100s. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Swiftfoot Boots|Swiftfoot Boots]] is buy-rank 26.

Do not chump Kykar. Recast him if he dies.

## Common mistakes

- Blinking a token (Spirit, Channeler Bird) — it ceases.
- Blocking with the creature Kykar just blinked — it is gone until end of step.
- Bouncing one 1/1 instead of the engine.
- Gifting Flood Maw a Fish to a token player.
- Sitting on Day of Judgment while River's Rebuke would reset one player without touching you.
- Casting Kykar with no follow-up cantrip and no ETB creature.
- Treating Deadeye + Drake as “bad” — the 100 builds for it once those cards are in hand.
