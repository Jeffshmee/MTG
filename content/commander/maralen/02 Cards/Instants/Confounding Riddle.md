## Deck Role & Rating

Confounding Riddle is the modal dig that also taxes. Mode one looks at four, puts one in hand, and bins the rest — that is how you load [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]], and [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]], and how you snag an Elf off a land-heavy top. Mode two is a {4} tax next to live [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Spellstutter Sprite|Spellstutter Sprite]], and [[02 Cards/Instants/Assassin's Trophy|Assassin's Trophy]]. Not tribal. Owned (in hand). Sitting on the sideboard until a main-deck slot is confirmed. [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Germination Practicum|Germination Practicum]] took slot 13; this still needs a named cut.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Dig when the yard is empty and Findbroker / Regrower / Loyalist / Pennant need a permanent, or when the top four include the Elf you actually need this turn.
- Hold the {4} tax on the Maralen or [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] turn the same way you hold Negate. They can pay — stop a tap-out spell, not a 2-mana rock from a player with lands up.
- Instant. End-step look after they pass, or the stack tax if the other mode is live.
- Do not mill the last Wave or a second legend you would rather have in hand.
