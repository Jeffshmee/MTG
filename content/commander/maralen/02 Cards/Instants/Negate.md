## Deck Role & Rating

Cheap noncreature counter — the wipe, the extra turn, or the exile-based removal that would take [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]]. It does not hit creature spells. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Spellstutter Sprite|Spellstutter Sprite]] and [[02 Cards/Instants/Assassin's Trophy|Assassin's Trophy]] now share the suite; until [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]], and [[02 Cards/Instants/Counterspell|Counterspell]] arrive, this is still the cheap noncreature hold-up.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold {1}{U} on the turn you cast Maralen or [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]].
- Counter the wrath or the exile spell, not their ramp.
- Creature removal that is a creature (a Rec Sage, a pack-rat eater) walks. So does a creature commander pointed at her in combat.
- After the pending saves arrive, this stays the cheap noncreature answer.

## Key Synergies

- **What you are protecting**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]
- **Pending better protection**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Counterspell|Counterspell]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Misses every creature spell.
- One copy. After it is gone, Maralen is naked.
- Two mana on the Wave turn is a real tax.
