## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Draw a Card|Draw a card]] if an opponent has cast a blue or black spell this turn. Spells you control can't be countered this turn. You and permanents you control gain [[03 Effects/Hexproof|hexproof]] from blue and from black until end of turn.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Veil of Summer is cheap protection against blue and black — the colours that bounce, counter, and kill Maralen. [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]] already covers wipes. This is the cheaper suit vs UB. Not an Elf or Faerie. Suggested cut from the 100: [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]] (Spellstutter plus Intervention already cover a lot of that job).

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast in response to a blue or black point-spell at Maralen.
- Cast on their turn after they have already played a U/B spell to also draw.
- Not in the 100 until a named cut is confirmed.
