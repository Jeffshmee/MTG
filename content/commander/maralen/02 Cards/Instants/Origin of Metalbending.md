## Oracle Text

Choose one —
• [[03 Effects/Destroy|Destroy]] target artifact or enchantment.
• Put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on target creature you control. It gains [[03 Effects/Indestructible|indestructible]] until end of turn. (Damage and effects that say "[[03 Effects/Destroy|destroy]]" don't [[03 Effects/Destroy|destroy]] it.)

## Deck Role & Rating

Two-mana instant with two jobs: destroy an artifact or enchantment, or put a +1/+1 counter on one of your creatures and give it indestructible until end of turn. The save is how this list keeps [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] through a destroy-based wipe or spot removal. The pump is how [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] connects and how a 3-power Elf becomes a [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]] draw.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold it for the destroy effect pointed at Maralen. Indestructible does not stop exile, bounce, or a shrink-to-zero.
- Pump Lathril through a blocker so the combat-damage trigger makes tokens. Those tokens mill if she is already out.
- Destroy mode when [[02 Cards/Instants/Heritage Reclamation|Heritage Reclamation]] is gone and a lock piece is still up.
- Do not spend the save on a dork if Maralen is also in the blast.

## Key Synergies

- **Save target**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Pump to connect / to 4 power**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]]
- **Other artifact/enchantment answers**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Heritage Reclamation|Heritage Reclamation]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Reclamation Sage|Reclamation Sage]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Indestructible loses to exile, bounce, sacrifice, and 0 toughness.
- Cannot counter a spell. That is [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]].
- One mode. You do not get the save and the Naturalize.
