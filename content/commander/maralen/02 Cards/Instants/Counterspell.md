## Deck Role & Rating

Wishlist hard counter. Counter any spell — including the creature spells [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]] misses. Cleaner protection for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] against a removal creature or a commander coming down. Wishlist only — not purchased, not in the owned list.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold `{U}{U}` for the wipe, the extra turn, or the creature that would eat Maralen.
- Better than Negate when the table is creature-heavy. Worse when you only have one Island.
- After it arrives it sits next to Negate: Negate on the cheap noncreature, this on anything.

## Key Synergies

- **What you are protecting**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]
- **Owned until this arrives**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet. Not purchased.
- `{U}{U}` is a real cost on a green-heavy manabase.
- Still one-for-one. Does not save a board the way [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]] does.
