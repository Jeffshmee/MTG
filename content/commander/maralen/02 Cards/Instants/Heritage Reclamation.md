## Deck Role & Rating

Cheap instant that destroys an artifact, destroys an enchantment, or exiles a graveyard card and draws. The owned list's main answer to the lock pieces that stop Maralen's mill or the steal (grave hate, extra-cost stax, tax auras). Grave mode is a cantrip when the board is clean.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Default: blow up the artifact or enchantment that shuts off the yard, taxes every Elf, or blanks the Wave.
- Grave mode when nothing needs answering — "up to one" means you can exile nothing and still draw, or eat their recursion piece.
- Hold it through the Wave turn if they have a piece that would exile the dump.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Reclamation Sage|Reclamation Sage]] is the creature version. Cast this first if you need to keep Sage as an Elf ETB after Maralen.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does not hit creatures, planeswalkers, or lands. A problem attacker walks.
- Drawing off the grave mode does not replace a body for Maralen.
