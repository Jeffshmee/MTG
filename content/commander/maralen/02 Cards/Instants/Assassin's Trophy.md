## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Destroy|Destroy]] target permanent an opponent controls. Its controller may search their library for a basic land card, put it onto the battlefield, then shuffle.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Destroy any permanent an opponent controls. The answer this list has for problem lands and planeswalkers — the things [[02 Cards/Instants/Heritage Reclamation|Heritage Reclamation]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Reclamation Sage|Reclamation Sage]] miss. They may search up a basic and put it into play. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Save it for the permanent nothing else answers: a utility land, a walker, or a creature that has hexproof from green.
- Do not Trophy a random artifact if Heritage is in hand. They get a land for that.
- After Maralen is down, still spend this on the lock piece, not on a chump attacker.

## Key Synergies

- **What the owned list already answers**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Heritage Reclamation|Heritage Reclamation]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Reclamation Sage|Reclamation Sage]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Origin of Metalbending|Origin of Metalbending]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- They ramp a basic. Bad against landfall and extra-land decks.
- Instant, but still one-for-one plus they got a land.
