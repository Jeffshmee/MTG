## Oracle Text

Elves you control have "{T}: [[03 Effects/Add Mana|Add]] {B}."
+1: Put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on up to one target Elf. [[03 Effects/Untap|Untap]] it. It gains [[03 Effects/Deathtouch|deathtouch]] until end of turn.
0: [[03 Effects/Create Token|Create a 1/1 green Elf Warrior creature token]].
−6: You get an emblem with "Whenever you cast an Elf spell, it gains [[03 Effects/Haste|haste]] until end of turn and you [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw two cards]]."

## Deck Role & Rating

Tyvar is the black-from-Elves planeswalker. The static ability is the reason he is on the wishlist: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sign in Blood|Sign in Blood]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]] are the {B}{B} spells this 100 still pinches on. The 0 loyalty makes an Elf Warrior every turn, which mills if [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] is in play. Plus-one is a pump on an Elf you already tapped for {B}, not the main mode. Ultimate is real but not the plan. He is not an Elf himself — he does not mill on ETB. Not in the owned list yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Loyalty 0 the turn he enters if Maralen is down (token mill). Plus-one if you needed the extra {B} from an Elf you already tapped.
- Do not minus-six unless the table is already dead to the emblem. The static and the token are the card.
- {2}{G}{G} — Archdruid or Chorus should already be making extra green. Do not cast him off two Forests and pass.
- He dies to creature combat the way walkers do. Keep a blocker.

## Key Synergies

- **Spend the black**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sign in Blood|Sign in Blood]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Assassin's Trophy|Assassin's Trophy]]
- **Token mill**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]
- **Green to cast him**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Priest of Titania|Priest of Titania]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet.
- Not an Elf. Maralen does not mill when he enters.
- Faeries do not get "{T}: Add {B}."
- Double green fights the same pip as Circle / Priest / Bloom on the Ordered pile.
