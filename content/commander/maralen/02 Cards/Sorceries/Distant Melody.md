## Deck Role & Rating

Name Elf. Draw a card for each permanent you control of that type. The post-token / post-Wave refill this list is missing — [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sign in Blood|Sign in Blood]] is two cards, this is the rest of the hand. Purchased; it is not in the owned list until it is in hand.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Name Elf. Cast after [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], or [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] so the number is real.
- [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Firdoch Core|Firdoch Core]] counts (changeling permanent). [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Morcant's Eyes|Morcant's Eyes]] does too (kindred Elf).
- Do not name Faerie on this Elf-majority pile.
- Four-mana sorcery. Tap out only when the board is wide and they do not have the wipe.
