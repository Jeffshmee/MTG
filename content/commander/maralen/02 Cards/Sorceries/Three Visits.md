## Deck Role & Rating

Wishlist ramp. Search for a Forest card and put it onto the battlefield untapped. Same job as [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Lore|Nature's Lore]]: [[02 Cards/Lands/Overgrown Tomb|Overgrown Tomb]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Breeding Pool|Breeding Pool]], or a basic Forest, ready to tap. Wishlist only — not purchased, not in the owned list.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Fetch Tomb or Breeding Pool if it is still in the 99. Otherwise a Forest.
- Untapped. Cast a dork or hold [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]] after it.
- Does not find Island or [[02 Cards/Lands/Watery Grave|Watery Grave]]. That is [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Farseek|Farseek]].

## Key Synergies

- **Untapped Forests**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Overgrown Tomb|Overgrown Tomb]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Breeding Pool|Breeding Pool]], Forest
- **Same card, other name**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Lore|Nature's Lore]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet. Not purchased.
- Cannot fetch Island or Watery Grave.
- [[02 Cards/Lands/Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth|Yavimaya]] does not turn Island into a Forest *card* in the library.
