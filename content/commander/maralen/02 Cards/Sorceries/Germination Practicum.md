## Oracle Text

Put two [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counters]] on each creature you control.
Paradigm (Then [[03 Effects/Exile|exile]] this spell. After you first resolve a spell with this name, you may cast a copy of it from exile [[03 Effects/Cast Without Paying|without paying]] its mana cost at the beginning of each of your first main phases.)

## Deck Role & Rating

Put two +1/+1 counters on each creature you control, then Paradigm exiles it and copies it for free at the beginning of each of your first main phases. Wide pump so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] connects and so the team becomes [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]] draws. It is not a mill engine — no tokens, no ETBs, no extra Maralen triggers. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it after the board is wide (Lathril tokens, Imperious tokens). Two counters turn 1/1s into 3/3s; with a lord they hit 4 for Uprising.
- The Paradigm copy every first main is the card. Treat the first cast as setup, not a one-shot anthem.
- Pump Lathril before combat so the damage trigger makes more tokens.
- Do not sequence this as if it mills. Play an Elf that turn if Maralen is down.

## Key Synergies

- **Connect / draw**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]]
- **Lords on top of the counters**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Five-mana sorcery that does nothing if you have no creatures.
- Not Elf/Faerie ETBs. Does not feed Maralen except by helping combat.
- Paradigm is only first main. They can wipe before it repeats.
