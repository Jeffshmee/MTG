## Deck Role & Rating

Flash ping closer: whenever another Faerie you control enters, each opponent loses 1 life. She needs tokens from Bearer or pending Alela to matter; the 100 is still Elf-heavy, so she is a 2/2 flyer until Bearer is making tokens. Flash ETB still mills if Maralen is up. She is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Flash after Maralen so her own ETB mills. Later Faeries ping the table.
- Best after Bearer or Alela is already making tokens. A Wave that hits Faeries also drains.
- Recur with Findbroker or Regrower. Loyalist cannot.
- She is not a pump lord. The only payoff is the drain.
