## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/ETB|When this creature enters]], [[03 Effects/Mill|mill three cards]]. You may put a land card [[03 Effects/Recursion|from your graveyard on top of your library]]. (To mill a card, put the top card of your library into your graveyard.)

## Deck Role & Rating

Glowspore Shaman mills you three, then can put a land from your graveyard on top of your library. That is yard setup for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]], plus a guaranteed land off the next draw. It is not opponent mill — Maralen still has to do that when the Shaman enters. Owned cut — [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]] took the slot. Table note: it underperformed; the list has better fixing now.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen so you mill an opponent and yourself. Put a land on top if you need to hit a drop; leave it if you already milled [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] and want to draw a spell.
- Filling the yard enables Findbroker / Regrower immediately, or Loyalist if an Elf died.
- Do not put [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] or [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] on top — those want to stay in hand or be cast. Lands only.
- A land already in the yard (from a prior mill or a fetch) can be stacked even if this mill missed lands.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Yard payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]]
- **What you want in the yard**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- This mills you, not them. Hitting Wave or Rhythm into the yard is a real cost — Rhythm can still harmonize, Wave cannot.
- 3/1 trades down. Do not send it in unless you need a death for morbid.

- Owned cut. Sitting on the sideboard.
