## Deck Role & Rating

Highspire Artisan is a 0/3 reach Elf with Fabricate 1. Always take the +1/+1 counter. The Servo is not an Elf or a Faerie — it does not mill and it does not count for the steal. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] the Artisan itself mills and counts; the extra is reach on a 1/4. That is Wave/count fodder, not a token engine. Fabricate Servo is not an Elf; that is why it left. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Wilderness Reclamation|Wilderness Reclamation]] took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen, put the counter on Highspire, leave it back as a 1/4 reach blocker.
- Never make the Servo unless you specifically need an extra body to chump and you have already accepted it will not trigger Maralen.
- Wave hitting this is a 3-drop Elf. Do not Rhythm for it.
- Recur it only if you need another reach body. Loyalist is enough.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Anthems**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]
- **Wave fodder**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The Servo is colorless and typeless for this deck. Making it is a missed mill and a missed steal pip.
- 0 power before the counter. It never attacks and never helps Lathril connect.
