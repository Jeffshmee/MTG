## Oracle Text

Bewitching Whispers — [[03 Effects/ETB|When Shessra enters]], target creature blocks this turn if able.
Whispers of the Grave — At the beginning of your end step, if a creature died this turn, [[03 Effects/Pain Land|you may pay 2 life]]. If you do, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Shessra is a Human Elf, not a token engine. The ETB forces a creature to block this turn — that is how you punch [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] through or make their best body trade. The end-step morbid draw needs a death that turn (combat, or blight kills from [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]]). After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] she still mills and counts. Four mana for a 1/3 is the tax.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen, point Bewitching Whispers at their only flyer or at the creature that would block Lathril, then attack.
- Time it on a blight turn. Morcant plus an Elf ETB can kill a 1-toughness creature and turn morbid on.
- Pay 2 life at end step if something died and you need the card. Skip the pay if you are already low.
- She is not a Rhythm target. X=4 is Lathril or Morcant.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Force-block for a connect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]]
- **Deaths that turn morbid on**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thornweald Archer|Thornweald Archer]]
- **Other morbid**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Wardens of the Cycle|Wardens of the Cycle]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- No death this turn = no draw. A quiet end step is a 1/3 for four.
- Legendary 1/3. She does not make tokens and she does not ramp Wave.
