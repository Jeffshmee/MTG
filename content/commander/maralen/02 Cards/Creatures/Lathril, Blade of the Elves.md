## Deck Role & Rating

Lathril is the owned token engine. Combat damage makes that many Elf Warriors; each token is an ETB mill and +1 steal count, and they pay [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] for [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]. Tutor her with [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] when you need bodies — not when you need blight. The tap-ten drain is a backup close, not the main plan. First-priority recursion target next to Morcant.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- After Maralen, connect. Lords ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], Archdruid, Banner, Loyalist) make the hit larger and the tokens relevant in combat.
- [[02 Cards/Lands/Rogue's Passage|Rogue's Passage]] or Menace plus a cleared board is how she gets through. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamer Gifter|Glamer Gifter]] can turn her into a 4/4 for the turn.
- Tokens this turn, Wave next turn. Do not tap ten the same turn you need Archdruid and the team for X.
- If she dies after a good connect, Findbroker / Regrower / Loyalist / Pennant her back. The tokens stay.

## Key Synergies

- **Tutor / find**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] (X=4), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Eclipsed Elf|Eclipsed Elf]]
- **Replay**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]]
- **Make the connect matter**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]]
- **Wave**: tokens are Elves for Archdruid's ritual

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- She does nothing if she cannot connect. Fog, pillowfort, and deathtouch walls shut her off.
- Tap-ten is ten Elves and her tap. That is a Wave turn's mana. Pick one.
- Not the card to tutor into a creature-heavy table — that is Morcant.
