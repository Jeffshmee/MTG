## Deck Role & Rating

Faerie lord plus death-draw: other Faeries get +1/+1, and when another Faerie you control dies you draw a card and lose 1 life. Both halves need a Faerie critical mass. Bearer is live for tokens; until those exist and pending Alela arrives, he is a 2/3 flying deathtoucher and a Sultai body for Bloom Tender. He is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Do not lead on him. Wait until two or more other Faeries exist.
- Death-draw is best with chump tokens from Bearer or Alela, not with your only Gifter.
- Deathtouch flyer is a blocker, not a closer.
- Recur with Findbroker or Regrower. Loyalist cannot.
