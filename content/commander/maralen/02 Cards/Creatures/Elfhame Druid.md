## Deck Role & Rating

Elfhame Druid is a 0/2 that taps for {G}. The second ability — {T}: add {G}{G} only for kicked spells — is dead in this list. There is almost no kicker. Treat it as a two-mana dork with no power. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] it still mills and counts, and it is an Elf for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]. That is the whole card.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on 2 to help hit Maralen on 4, or on 2 after a turn-1 dork to hit her on 3.
- After Maralen, play it for the mill. Tap for {G} toward Perfect's token or to top up Wave if you are short of generic.
- Never hold up the kick mana. You will not cast a kicked spell.
- It cannot attack. Leave it back as a 0/2 chump when you empty the rest of the team into Wave.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The {G}{G} kick clause does not pay Wave, Rhythm, Maralen, or stolen spells.
- 0 power means it never connects, never pumps Lathril's damage, and is a blank on [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]].
