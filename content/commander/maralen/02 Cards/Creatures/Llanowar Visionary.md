## Deck Role & Rating

Llanowar Visionary is an owned cut. Sitting on the sideboard. The job was the clean three-drop after [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]]: mill 2, draw a card, then tap for {G}. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thranduil, Sindarin Liege|Thranduil, Sindarin Liege]] took this slot — lord plus landfall Elf tokens over the cantrip dork. The body is still an Elf for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] if it ever comes back.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Maralen on 3, Visionary on 4 (or the reverse if you hit four lands first). The draw replaces itself and often finds Rhythm or another Elf.
- Tap it for green the turn after, not the turn it enters, unless you need the mana to steal or to make a Perfect token.
- A Wave that hits Visionary is excellent — you draw as the pile resolves and get another dork.
- Recur it when you need cards. Findbroker / Regrower on Visionary is a real line.
