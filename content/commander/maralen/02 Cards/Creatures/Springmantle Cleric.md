## Oracle Text

This creature enters with a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on it for each color of mana spent to cast it.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Springmantle Cleric is a five-mana Elf that enters with a +1/+1 counter per color spent. Off `{G}` it is a 3/4. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] it mills and counts. That is the whole card: a late Wave/count body with a slightly bigger frame. Do not build the turn around spending three colors to make it a 5/6.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen when you have five mana and nothing better. Take the mill and the 3/4.
- If [[02 Cards/Creatures/Llanowar Loamspeaker|Llanowar Loamspeaker]] or Rosethorn can feed a second color, you may as well — one extra counter is free toughness.
- Wave at X=5 puts this onto the battlefield — it was not cast, so it enters as a 2/3 with no counters. Still take it.
- Never Rhythm X=5 for this. X=4 is Lathril or Morcant.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Color filters if you bother**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Llanowar Loamspeaker|Llanowar Loamspeaker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Rosethorn Acolyte|Rosethorn Acolyte]]
- **Wave fodder**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]
- **Anthems**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Five mana for a 3/4 is the worst rate in the creature suite. It does not draw, ramp, or make tokens.
- Wave puts it onto the battlefield uncast, so it enters as a 2/3. The rainbow 5/6 only happens if you hard-cast it with three colors.

- Owned cut. Sitting on the sideboard.
