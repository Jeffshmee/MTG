## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/ETB|When this creature enters]], [[03 Effects/Look at Top|look at the top]] five cards of your library. You may put a land card from among them onto the battlefield tapped. Put the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Elvish Rejuvenator is land ramp that happens to be an Elf. The ETB looks at five and puts a land onto the battlefield tapped — extra land in play is extra permanents for [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] to hit and extra mana to pay a bigger X. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] the body mills and counts. Prefer a basic Forest or a color you are short, then treat the 1/1 as Archdruid fuel.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen so you mill an opponent and still find a land. The land entering does not mill — only the Elf does.
- Grab [[02 Cards/Lands/Forest|Forest]] if you need Archdruid/Wave green, [[02 Cards/Lands/Island|Island]] if Maralen is stranded, [[02 Cards/Lands/Swamp|Swamp]] if you are holding [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]].
- A hit land raises Wave's density. Cast Rejuvenator the turn before you tap Archdruid if you can.
- Recur it with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]] only when you still need lands. Rhythm would rather find Archdruid or Lathril.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Wave setup**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]
- **Color you might hit**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Forest|Forest]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Island|Island]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Swamp|Swamp]]
- **Replay the ETB**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The land comes in tapped. This does not help you cast Maralen the same turn.
- Top five with no land is a 1/1 for three. The list is land-heavy, but a spell-flood miss happens.
