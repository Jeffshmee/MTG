## Deck Role & Rating

Lluwen is a two-mana Elf that mills you four, then can stack a creature or land from those cards. That is the job: set up the next draw and dump payoffs into the yard for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]]. The Worm mode is not a win. Worm tokens are not Elves or Faeries — they do not mill for Maralen and they do not count for the steal. Use him as a yard-setup Elf.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen. Mill four, stack [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] or a land if you need it next, or leave a milled [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]] in the yard for recursion.
- Stack Wave or Rhythm only if you have nothing else — you would rather draw a creature and recur from the yard.
- Ignore the Worm ability unless the game is already over and you need chump blockers. Do not spend the turn and the land-discard on Worms.
- He is legendary. A second copy is just the ETB again if you somehow see one.

## Key Synergies

- **Yard payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]]
- **Stack or dump**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Worm tokens do not trigger Maralen and do not raise the steal number. That mode is off-plan.
- Self-mill can hit [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]. Do not stack a land over a Wave you still need to draw.
