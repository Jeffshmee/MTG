Vivid — {T}: For each color among permanents you control, [[03 Effects/Add Mana|add]] one mana of that color.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Bloom Tender is a two-mana Elf that taps for colors, not for creature count. Once Maralen or another multicolor permanent is out, Vivid hits Sultai — {G}{U}{B} from one dork — which is how you cast her and pay mixed steal costs. It is not a Wave ritual; five Elves still only add as many colors as you actually have. It is Ordered and sits on the sideboard — not in the owned list yet. Locked cut when it arrives: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Wardens of the Cycle|Wardens of the Cycle]]. Do not cut [[02 Cards/Creatures/Rosethorn Acolyte|Rosethorn Acolyte]] for it.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Turn-2 dork. The tap is one green until a blue, black, or gold permanent is in play.
- Maralen is Sultai, so after she lands the tap is three colors. Owned gold already lights it: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]].
- Do not tap it expecting Archdruid math. Use it to fix, then let Priest, Archdruid, or pending Circle fund X.
- Recur the fixer with Findbroker or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]]; [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]] can also return it.
