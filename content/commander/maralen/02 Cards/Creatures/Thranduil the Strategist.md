## Deck Role & Rating

Thranduil is a five-mana Elf token engine that also turns every other Elf into a GU dork. The tokens are Elves, so they mill and count for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]]. The landfall trigger wants extra land drops: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Springbloom Druid|Springbloom Druid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Rejuvenator|Elvish Rejuvenator]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth|Yavimaya]]. He is legendary and shares the five-mana Elf slot with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] — Lathril is the combat close; Thranduil is the landfall mill + blue mana. Not in the owned list yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast him after Maralen. The next land (or Springbloom) is an Elf ETB mill.
- Sequence Springbloom / Rejuvenator the same turn if you can. One extra land = one extra mill.
- Tap Elves for {U} when Maralen or a Faerie is in hand and the Forests are already online.
- Do not tap him for the team ability — other Elves get it; he does not give it to himself.

## Key Synergies

- **Extra lands**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Springbloom Druid|Springbloom Druid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Rejuvenator|Elvish Rejuvenator]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth|Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth]]
- **Use the tokens**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]
- **Other five-mana Elf**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet.
- Legendary. A second copy from Wave is a legend-rule loss.
- Lands that already entered this turn before he resolved do not trigger. He has to be in play first.
- Tokens are Elves, not Faeries.
