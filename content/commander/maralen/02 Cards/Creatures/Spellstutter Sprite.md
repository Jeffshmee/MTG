## Deck Role & Rating

Flash counter that stops a spell with mana value ≤ the number of Faeries you control, including itself. The 100 is still Elf-heavy — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamer Gifter|Glamer Gifter]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamermite|Glamermite]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mocking Sprite|Mocking Sprite]], and Maralen — so Sprite often only tags 1–2 MV until Bearer tokens and pending Alela raise the count. Even a failed or tiny counter is still a flash Faerie ETB that mills if she is up. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold {1}{U} after Maralen. Count Faeries before you flash, and include Sprite.
- Stop cheap interaction aimed at her. Do not try to counter a 5-drop on a two-Faerie board.
- Gets real once [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bitterbloom Bearer|Bitterbloom Bearer]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Alela, Cunning Conqueror|Alela, Cunning Conqueror]] is making tokens.
- Recur with Findbroker or Regrower for another ETB counter. Loyalist cannot.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Weak while the 100 is Elf-majority. X is often 1 or 2 until Bearer tokens exist.
- Cannot counter high-MV commanders or their finishers.
- The counter is a one-shot ETB. A 1/1 leftover is not a second counter.
