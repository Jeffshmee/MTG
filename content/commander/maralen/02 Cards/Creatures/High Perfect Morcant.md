## Deck Role & Rating

Morcant is the attrition legend. Every Elf ETB (including his own) makes each opponent blight 1, and tapping three Elves proliferates those −1/−1s. Tutor him with [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] when the table has a creature board you need to shrink — not when you need tokens. He is an Elf, so he mills and counts, and he is a first-priority recursion target for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Regrower]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Loyalist]].

## Play Patterns & Lines

- After Maralen: his ETB mills *and* blights. Then dump Elves (tokens from Lathril / Imperious, flash Gifter) so each body is a mill and a blight.
- Proliferate as a sorcery once three Elves can tap. Stack blight on their best creature, then push it.
- Do **not** tap the team on the same turn you need them for Archdruid → Wave.
- If he dies, get him back before you replay Lathril. The blight plan needs him more than a second token engine.

## Key Synergies

- **Tutor / find**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] (X=4), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Eclipsed Elf|Eclipsed Elf]]
- **Replay**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]]
- **Elf ETBs to blight**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dwynen's Elite|Dwynen's Elite]]
- **Double ETBs**: [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Panharmonicon|Panharmonicon]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does nothing to a table with empty boards or indestructible / blight-proof commanders.
- Proliferate is sorcery-speed and taps three Elves. It fights Wave and Lathril's tap-ten.
- Faerie ETBs do **not** blight. Only Elves.
