## Deck Role & Rating

Best repeating Faerie engine in the 100: flash it in, then every upkeep you lose 1 life and make a 1/1 flying Faerie. Each token is a Faerie ETB — mills 2 if Maralen is up and raises the steal count. That is the critical mass Spellstutter, Tegwyll, and Obyra are waiting on, and pending Scion later. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Flash end of turn after Maralen so the first token mills on your upkeep.
- Feed [[02 Cards/Creatures/Spellstutter Sprite|Spellstutter Sprite]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor|Tegwyll]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Obyra, Dreaming Duelist|Obyra]], and pending [[02 Cards/Creatures/Scion of Oona|Scion of Oona]].
- Recur with Findbroker or Regrower if it dies. Loyalist cannot — Bearer is not an Elf.
- The life loss is a clock. Do not run it out at 4 life.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- 1 life a turn. Stacks with other drains and with Tegwyll's death trigger.
- Tokens are not Elves. They do not grow Marwyn, pay Priest or Archdruid, or trigger Warmaster.
- Needs Maralen already in play to turn tokens into mills.
