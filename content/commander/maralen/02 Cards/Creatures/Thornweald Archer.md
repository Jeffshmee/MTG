## Deck Role & Rating

Thornweald Archer is the two-mana reach-and-deathtouch Elf. Flyers eat [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] in the air; this is the owned answer that is also an Elf. After she is down it mills and counts. Leave it back. The 2/1 will die to any damage, but deathtouch means the bird dies with it.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen for the mill, then do not attack unless you need a death for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Wardens of the Cycle|Wardens]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Shessra, Death's Whisper|Shessra]].
- Block the first flyer that is pointed at Maralen or Archdruid. That is the card.
- Under Perfect it is a 3/2 deathtouch — still a blocker first.
- Recur it with Loyalist if the table is still flying. Do not Rhythm for a 2/1.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander to protect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Morbid payoffs off the trade**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Wardens of the Cycle|Wardens of the Cycle]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Shessra, Death's Whisper|Shessra, Death's Whisper]]
- **Backup reach**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Tajuru Snarecaster|Tajuru Snarecaster]]
- **Anthems**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- 2/1 dies to any ping, blight, or a 1-power first strike. You get one block.
- Reach does nothing on offense. Sending it at a planeswalker just to "use it" is how Maralen dies the next turn.
