## Deck Role & Rating

Maralen is the engine and the primary win. Every Elf or Faerie ETB mills two; once a turn you steal an exiled spell if its mana value is ≤ your Elf + Faerie count. Every other card is judged by whether it is an Elf or Faerie, whether it makes more of those, or whether it puts her on the battlefield on time and keeps her there. Live protection is [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Spellstutter Sprite|Spellstutter Sprite]], and [[02 Cards/Instants/Assassin's Trophy|Assassin's Trophy]] until Greaves / Heroic / Scion arrive.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Curve her on 3–5 off dorks. The turn she enters, have a follow-up Elf or Faerie so you mill twice and the steal number is already >1.
- After she is down: tokens (Lathril, Imperious, Dwynen's Elite, Warmaster, Bearer) beat solo dorks. Flash Faeries (Gifter, Glamermite, Sprite, Trickster, Obyra) mill on an opponent's turn.
- Steal the cheapest *useful* spell, not the biggest. A 2-mana removal or ramp from their pile is better than missing because you were greedy.
- Wave turn: she must already be in play so every Elf/Faerie the Wave hits mills. Do not Wave her into play unless she is stuck in the command zone.
- Recursion does not return commanders the way it returns Morcant. If she dies, recast her. Spend Findbroker on the 99.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- A pile of dorks with her in the command zone is not a deck.
- Non-Elf, non-Faerie tokens (Lluwen Worms) do not mill and do not count.
- She is legendary. A second copy from Wave or steal is a legend-rule loss unless you need the ETB.
