## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/ETB|When this creature enters]], if you control another Elf, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a 1/1 green Elf Warrior creature token]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Dwynen's Elite is two Elves for {1}{G} if you already control an Elf. That is two Maralen mills, +2 steal count, and two bodies for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]] blight. Sequence it after a dork or after Maralen herself — she is an Elf Faerie, so she turns the token on. The token is an Elf Warrior, so it mills when it enters.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Turn 1 dork, turn 2 Elite. Both the 2/2 and the 1/1 token ETB; if Maralen is already down that is four cards exiled from an opponent.
- If the board is empty, Elite makes no token. Play a one-drop first, or cast it after Maralen — she is an Elf and satisfies the clause.
- After [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], both ETBs blight. That is the two-mana blight burst.
- The token is Wave fuel and a tap for Archdruid. It is also a legal Lathril-team attacker once lords are out.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Elves that turn the token on**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Llanowar Elves|Llanowar Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Mystic|Elvish Mystic]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Fyndhorn Elves|Fyndhorn Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arbor Elf|Arbor Elf]]
- **Payoffs for two ETBs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Virulent Emissary|Virulent Emissary]]
- **Go-wide**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Empty board = a vanilla 2/2 for two. Maralen counts; a lone Faerie that is not also an Elf does not.
- The token is a 1/1. Without [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]] or Banner it does not win combat; it exists to mill and count.
