## Deck Role & Rating

Kujar Seedsculptor is a two-mana Elf that drops a +1/+1 counter on ETB. The counter is the line: put it on [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] so a connect makes an extra token, or on [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] so he lives through a shock. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] Kujar mills and counts. It does not make mana — do not treat it as a dork.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- With Lathril in play (2/3), Kujar makes her 3/4. A connect is three Elf tokens, each of which mills if Maralen is up.
- With only Maralen, put the counter on her (4/5 becomes 5/6) so she is harder to ping, or on Kujar itself if nothing else matters.
- After Archdruid, the extra toughness is more valuable than the extra power — keep the ritualist alive.
- Recur Kujar only if Lathril is in play and you want another counter. Rhythm would rather find Lathril herself.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Pump targets**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]]
- **Token payoffs after a bigger Lathril**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Virulent Emissary|Virulent Emissary]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- No mana ability. A t2 Kujar instead of Loamspeaker/Cowl delays Maralen.
- The counter is one shot. Without Lathril or a lord in play you spent two mana on a 2/3.

- Owned cut. Sitting on the sideboard.
