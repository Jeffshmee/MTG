## Deck Role & Rating

Marwyn is a slow ritual: she enters as a 1/1 and only grows when another Elf you control enters. Once she is large she taps for her power and can fund [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] the same way [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] does, but she needs a turn of Elf ETBs first. Protect her — a 1/1 with counters is a lightning rod, and recursion returns her as a 1/1. She is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast her, then dump Elves or activate [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]] / connect with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] before you tap.
- Hold the tap for Wave (X equals power minus 3) once she is 5+. Early taps just recast small stuff.
- Faerie ETBs do not put counters on her. Bearer and pending Alela tokens are dead growth.
- If she dies, Findbroker or Regrower get the permanent back, Loyalist gets the Elf — all as a 1/1. Rebuild the counters.

## Key Synergies

- **Elf tokens**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Warmaster|Elvish Warmaster]]
- **Wave**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]
- **Tutor**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] (X=3)
- **Recursion**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Only other Elves grow her. Faerie tokens do nothing.
- Counters fall off on death. Recursion does not save the ritual size.
- Slower than Archdruid or Priest, who are live the turn they see a wide board.
