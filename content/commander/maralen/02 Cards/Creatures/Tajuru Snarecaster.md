## Deck Role & Rating

Tajuru Snarecaster is Wave/count fodder with reach. The 1/4 sits under Maralen and eats a small flyer that would otherwise snipe her. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] it mills and counts. Reach is the extra — it is not [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thornweald Archer|Thornweald Archer]], so it will not kill a 5-power bird without a lord.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen for the mill, then leave it back. The 4 toughness lives through most blight and through a 2-power flyer.
- Under Perfect or Archdruid it can actually trade up. Without them, chump the 2/2 faerie and move on.
- Wave hitting a 3-drop Elf with reach is fine. Do not Rhythm for this.
- Pair with Thornweald if the table is flying-heavy: Tajuru soaks, Thornweald kills.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Anthems that make reach real**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]]
- **Better flyer answer**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thornweald Archer|Thornweald Archer]]
- **Wave fodder**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- 1 power. It does not attack, it does not connect for Lathril, and it does not turn on Uprising.
- Reach without deathtouch loses to any flyer bigger than the current toughness.

- Owned cut. Sitting on the sideboard.
