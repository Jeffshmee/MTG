## Deck Role & Rating

Reclamation Sage is the artifact/enchantment answer that is also an Elf. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] it mills, counts, and can eat a Rest in Peace, a Grave Pact, or the rock that is keeping someone ahead. It is a primary recursion target: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]] replay the ETB. Hold it when you expect a problem permanent; dump it when you just need the mill.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- After Maralen, point the ETB at the permanent that actually stops you — grave hate, a pillowfort enchantment, or a mana rock — then take the mill.
- If nothing needs answering, you may still cast it for the body. The destroy is optional.
- Recur it the turn they replay the same enchantment. That is the card's second life.
- Do not Rhythm X=3 for Sage if Archdruid or Perfect is still in the deck. Recursion is how you see the ETB twice.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Replay the ETB**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]]
- **Owned answers in the same lane**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Heritage Reclamation|Heritage Reclamation]]
- **Wave hits it as a 3-drop Elf**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- 2/1 dies to any removal and to blight. You get one ETB unless you recur it.
- It does not hit creatures. Shessra / Trophy / blight have to do that job.
