## Deck Role & Rating

Cheap Faerie that untaps two lands on ETB — a double-spell or a refund after tapping out, not a token maker. Cycle it for {2} when you do not need the body; recursion rebuys the untap. Live in the 100. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lluwen, Imperfect Naturalist|Lluwen]] is the owned cut.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after tapping two lands so you can recast something the same turn — Maralen, a steal, or Wave leftover.
- Untaps lands only. It will not untap Archdruid, Priest, or Marwyn.
- After Maralen the ETB still mills even if you only wanted the mana.
- Cycle when the board does not need another 1/1.
- Recur with Findbroker or Regrower to do the refund again. Loyalist cannot.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not an Elf. Loyalist cannot return it.
