## Deck Role & Rating

Glamermite is the three-mana flash Faerie that taps or untaps a creature on ETB. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] that is an instant-speed mill and +1 steal count. The real line is untapping [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] after the first ritual so you can float a second pile of green into [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], or tapping the blocker that would eat Lathril. Hold it; do not run it out as a 2/2 flyer.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Wave turn: tap Archdruid for {G} per Elf, flash Glamermite, untap Archdruid, tap again. That is the owned double-ritual.
- Combat: tap their only blocker so Lathril or Maralen connects, or tap the flyer that is pointed at Maralen.
- Their end step, with Maralen up: flash, mill 2, raise the count, steal on your turn.
- Untap Perfect to make a second token the same turn if you have the {G}.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Double ritual**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]
- **Tap for a connect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]]
- **Other flash**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Scion of Oona|Scion of Oona]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Three mana at instant speed. Holding it forever and never using the tap/untap is how the card becomes a blank 2/2.
- Untapping Archdruid does nothing if you already spent the green and have no Wave (or no X left to grow).
