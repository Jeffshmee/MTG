## Deck Role & Rating

Wardens of the Cycle is a four-mana Elf that, at your end step, draws (and loses 1) or gains 2 life if a creature died this turn. The death can be combat or a blight kill from [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]]. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] it mills and counts. Default mode is the card; take the life only when you are dead to the next swing. It is not a token engine.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen on a turn you will actually get a death: attack with deathtouch, chump, or blight with Morcant.
- End step: draw almost always. The 3/4 body is also a real attacker under lords.
- Same-turn pairing with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Shessra, Death's Whisper|Shessra]] is two morbid payoffs off one death.
- Do not tap it into a suicide attack just to turn morbid on unless the draw matters more than the 3/4.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Deaths on demand**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thornweald Archer|Thornweald Archer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Virulent Emissary|Virulent Emissary]]
- **Other morbid**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Shessra, Death's Whisper|Shessra, Death's Whisper]]
- **Wave fodder**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- No death that turn = no trigger. A tapped-out, no-combat turn is a vanilla 3/4 for four.
- The draw costs a life. Stacking it every turn while blight is also picking off your 1/1s adds up.
