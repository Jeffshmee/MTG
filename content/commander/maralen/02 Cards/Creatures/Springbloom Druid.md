## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/ETB|When this creature enters]], you may [[03 Effects/Sacrifice|sacrifice]] a land. If you do, [[03 Effects/Tutor|search your library]] for up to two basic land cards, put them onto the battlefield tapped, then shuffle.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Springbloom Druid is the net-plus-one land Elf: sacrifice one land, put two basics onto the battlefield tapped. That is more lands in play for [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] to count as permanents and more colored sources for Maralen / Morcant. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] the Druid itself mills and counts. Always take the sacrifice unless you are land-screwed to one untapped source you still need this turn.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen. Sac a tapped [[02 Cards/Lands/Forest|Forest]] or a gate, fetch the two colors you are missing (usually Island + Swamp, or Forest + the hole).
- Two new lands raise the permanent count for Wave. Sequence Springbloom the turn before you ritual if the board is already wide.
- The sacrificed land can be a tapped dual or a basic Forest — still net plus one.
- Do not sac your only blue source the turn you need [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]] up.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Wave setup**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]
- **Basics it finds**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Forest|Forest]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Island|Island]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Swamp|Swamp]]
- **Replay the ramp ETB**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Both lands enter tapped. Same-turn Maralen or Wave still needs mana you already had.
- Refusing the sacrifice leaves a 1/1 for three. Taking it when you have one land strands you.
