## Deck Role & Rating

Virulent Emissary is the one-mana deathtouch Elf, not a dork. It does not tap for mana. What it does is pad life on every later creature ETB — which is the entire Wave turn — and sit as a 1/1 that trades with anything that tries to eat a lord. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] it still mills and counts. Play it early so the life trigger is online before the flood.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Turn 1 Emissary, then develop dorks and Maralen. Every token from [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] is +1 life.
- On a [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], each permanent creature that hits nets a life. That is the reason it stays over a fourth 1/1 dork.
- Block the first attacker that would snipe Maralen or Archdruid. Deathtouch makes the 1/1 real.
- Do not tap it for anything — it has no mana ability. It is a body and a trigger.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander / count**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **ETB flood**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dwynen's Elite|Dwynen's Elite]]
- **Lords it can trade to protect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not ramp. A t1 Emissary instead of Llanowar/Mystic/Fyndhorn puts Maralen a turn later.
- Life gain does not mill opponents and does not raise the steal number — only the body does.
