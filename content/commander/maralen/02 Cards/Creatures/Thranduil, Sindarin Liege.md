## Deck Role & Rating

Thranduil is the Elf lord that also prints Elves. The anthem is the same job as [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]'s static; the landfall tokens mill and raise Maralen's steal number without tapping him. Silvan Rally mills four and puts up to two lands into hand — that is how you hit landfall the same game and how you find the {B} for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]]. Cast the Adventure when you need lands or mill; cast the creature after [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] so the next land is another mill. This is **not** [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thranduil the Strategist|Thranduil the Strategist]] (still wishlist). He took [[02 Cards/Creatures/Llanowar Visionary|Llanowar Visionary]]'s slot. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamermite|Glamermite]] stays.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Adventure first if you are short on lands or want the mill. The Adventure is not a creature and does not mill via Maralen.
- Creature after Maralen. The next land ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Springbloom Druid|Springbloom]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Farseek|Farseek]], a basic, [[02 Cards/Lands/Wastewood Verge|Wastewood Verge]]) is a 1/1 Elf and a mill.
- [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] X=4 finds him; X=3 is still Perfect. Recur him with Findbroker / Loyalist / Pennant when you want the factory back.
- Do not Wave him in as a 2/3 if Maralen is still in the command zone — you get the lord and later tokens, but you miss the mill on his ETB until she is down.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Creature cost is {2}{G/U}{G/U} — two hybrid pips. Forests plus [[02 Cards/Lands/Breeding Pool|Breeding Pool]] cover it; do not keep him in a no-blue, no-green-fix hand.
- Landfall tokens are 1/1 Elves, not Elf Warriors. Banner still pumps if you named Elf.
- The Adventure does not trigger Maralen. If you never cast the creature you only milled four.
- Legendary. A second copy from Wave is a legend-rule loss unless you need the ETB.
- Not [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thranduil the Strategist|Thranduil the Strategist]].
