## Deck Role & Rating

Once per turn, another Elf ETB makes a 1/1 Elf Warrior — cheaper than [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], no tap, no anthem until you pay {5}{G}{G}. The token is an Elf, so it mills if Maralen is up and counts for steal, Archdruid, and Priest. It overlaps Imperious — you want one token engine early, not both. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast after Maralen, then play one Elf a turn so you get the extra mill each time.
- A Wave of many Elves is still one Warmaster trigger. Do not count on it to double a pile.
- The pump is a combat closer after Archdruid or pending Circle, not a main-phase plan.
- Recur with Findbroker, Regrower, or Loyalist.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Once each turn. Wave and multi-spell turns are inefficient.
- Faeries do not trigger it.
