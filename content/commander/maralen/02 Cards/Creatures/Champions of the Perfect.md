## Oracle Text

As an additional cost to cast this spell, behold an Elf and [[03 Effects/Exile|exile]] it. ([[03 Effects/Exile|Exile]] an Elf you control or an Elf card from your hand.)
Whenever you cast a creature spell, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]].
When this creature leaves the battlefield, [[03 Effects/Recursion|return the exiled card]] to its owner's hand.

## Deck Role & Rating

6/6 Elf that draws on every creature spell, with an extra cost: behold an Elf and exile it. When Champions leaves the battlefield, that Elf returns to hand — soft protection and a third recursion pattern next to Findbroker/Regrower (any permanent) and Loyalist (Elves only). Behold a dork you can recast, not [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|Morcant]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], unless you want that legend in hand later. It is Ordered and sits on the sideboard — not in the owned list yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Behold a 1-drop so the board stays wide and you get the dork back when Champions leaves.
- Draw like [[02 Cards/Creatures/Beast Whisperer|Beast Whisperer]] — cast creatures after it lands. Token activations do not draw.
- Behold Morcant or Lathril on purpose only when you want them in hand after a chump, bounce, or death.
- Recur Champions itself with Findbroker or Regrower to exile a new Elf and restart the draw.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- You must exile an Elf from the battlefield or your hand. No Elf, no cast.
- Beholding your only ritual or lord shrinks the board for a 6/6.
- Token ETBs are not creature spells.
- Not in the owned list yet.
