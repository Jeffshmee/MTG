## Deck Role & Rating

Glamer Gifter is the two-mana flash Faerie that, on ETB, makes another creature a 4/4 with every creature type until end of turn. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] the flash mill happens on their turn, and the steal count ticks up before you take a spell. The type gift can turn a non-Elf, non-Faerie into something that counts this turn, or it can make [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] a 4/4 so a connect is four tokens. The pump is one turn; the Faerie stays. Owned cut — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Scion of Oona|Scion of Oona]] took the slot.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold it up after Maralen. Flash on their end step: mill 2, +1 count, then steal on your turn if the number is high enough.
- Point the ETB at Lathril and attack. Base 4/4 plus menace is four Warrior tokens if she connects.
- Point it at a leftover non-type body (a Worm, a Servo, an animated land) only if you need that one extra count this turn.
- A 4/4 also turns on [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]] for the draw if Uprising is in play.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The 4/4 and the extra types last until end of turn. It does not permanently make a Worm into an Elf.
- 1/2 flyer dies to any ping. Flash it after they are tapped out if you can.

- Owned cut. Sitting on the sideboard.
