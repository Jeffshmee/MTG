## Deck Role & Rating

Leaf-Crowned Visionary is the cheap Elf lord and paid-draw engine. Other Elves get +1/+1, stacking with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]], and [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]]. Casting an Elf — including [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] — lets you pay {G} to draw. That is narrower than [[02 Cards/Creatures/Beast Whisperer|Beast Whisperer]] (any creature, free, four mana) and cheaper. It took [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]]'s slot. It mills and counts; the anthem and the draws are why it is in the pile.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- After a one-mana dork, not instead of one. {G}{G} on turn 2 instead of Priest / Elite / Loamspeaker delays Maralen.
- Pay the {G} when you have extra green and an Elf spell. Skip it the turn you need that pip for Maralen or a ritual.
- Cast it, then dump the Elf hand. [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] does not draw — Wave does not cast those Elves.
- [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] X=2 finds this when you want a lord-plus-draw now; X=3 is still Perfect or Archdruid.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Tokens are not spells. Perfect activations, Lathril combat, and Warmaster tokens do not draw.
- Faerie spells do not trigger it. Beast Whisperer still covers those.
- 1/1. Dies to most removal, and the anthem leaves with it.
- Not a dork. Do not keep a two-land {G}{G} hand that is only this and no path to {U} or {B}.
