## Deck Role & Rating

Speaker is the creature-tutor Elf. The ETB mills for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] and, if you pitch, finds a creature — not a land, not an enchantment. Priority: Maralen if she is in the library, then [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] depending on whether you need mana or the close. The {1}, {T} untap is a second use of [[02 Cards/Creatures/Priest of Titania|Priest of Titania]] or [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], not a reason to play him without the tutor. Not in the owned list yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Enter after Maralen so the ETB mills. Pitch a land or a dead extra dork, not the card you needed this turn.
- Tutor tree: Maralen (if missing) → Archdruid (cast her this turn) → Lathril / Imperious (board is already wide) → Findbroker (something died).
- Untap Priest, Archdruid, Marwyn, or Banner in the same turn you tapped them for the Speaker + Maralen line.
- Do not discard [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]]; Harmonize wants it in the yard from the library, not from hand this way.
