## Deck Role & Rating

Incubator is the Elf-cost rock. Name **Elf** (the owned majority, not Faerie) so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] is three mana, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] is two, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] is three. Faeries stay full price; that is the trade for an Elf-lane list. Not in the owned list yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it before Maralen if you can. Three mana + {B}{G}{U} is the whole point.
- Name Elf as it enters. Do not name Faerie unless the board is already Faerie-heavy and Maralen is out.
- After it is down, dump [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] and the cheap Elves; the {2} off each creature is the Wave's extra names.
- Do not tap it for mana. It does not make mana.

## Key Synergies

- **Payoffs that get cheaper**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]
- **Name Elf with**: [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Kindred Discovery|Kindred Discovery]]
- **Wave**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet.
- Faerie spells (Sprite, Trickster, Obyra) do not get the discount if you named Elf.
- Does nothing the turn you need a rock that taps. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Sol Ring|Sol Ring]] / [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Arcane Signet|Arcane Signet]] are still the Ordered rocks.
