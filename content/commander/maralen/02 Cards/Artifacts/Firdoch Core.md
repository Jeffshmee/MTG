## Deck Role & Rating

Changeling rock. It is every creature type, so it is an Elf *and* a Faerie for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]]'s mill trigger and steal count even while it is not a creature. {T}: any colour toward Maralen. {4}: becomes a 4/4 until end of turn — extra body, extra [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]] draw if you pay it.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it as ramp. After Maralen is down, the ETB mills (it is an Elf/Faerie permanent) and it stays in the count.
- Animate when you need a 4-power creature for Uprising or a blocker that actually fights.
- [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]] named Elf pumps it only while it is a creature. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]] named Elf sees the ETB as a permanent.
- [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Distant Melody|Distant Melody]] named Elf counts it, because Melody counts permanents, not just creatures.

## Key Synergies

- **Counts as both**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]]
- **4-power when animated**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]]
- **Pending count draw**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Distant Melody|Distant Melody]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not a creature until you pay {4}. [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]] cannot find it, and it does not attack on its own.
- Animate lasts one turn.
- Three mana is slow if you already have dorks untapped.
