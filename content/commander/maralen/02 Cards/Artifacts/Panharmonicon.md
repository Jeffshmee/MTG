## Oracle Text

If an artifact or creature entering causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers [[03 Effects/Double Trigger|an additional time]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Panharmonicon is the ETB doubler in the 100. It doubles a triggered ability of a permanent you control when an artifact or creature enters — so Maralen mills four per Elf/Faerie, Findbroker / Regrower / Eclipsed Elf / Reclamation Sage / Visionary / Springbloom / Dwynen's Elite all fire twice, and Morcant blights twice. It is the first card Findbroker should return once it is in the yard. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it after Maralen. The next Elf is four cards, not two.
- Sequence ETB Elves after it: Sage, Visionary, Eclipsed, Regrower, Findbroker, Dwynen's Elite (two tokens if you already have an Elf).
- Recursion target #1 if it dies. Banner and Uprising wait.

## Key Synergies

- **The reason it exists**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Double ETBs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Eclipsed Elf|Eclipsed Elf]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Reclamation Sage|Reclamation Sage]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]]
- **Replay it**: Findbroker, Regrower

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does not double attack triggers, Lathril connects, or Archdruid's tap.
- Four mana that does nothing the turn you play it unless something enters after it.
