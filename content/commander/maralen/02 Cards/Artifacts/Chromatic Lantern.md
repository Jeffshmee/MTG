## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Artifacts/Chromatic Lantern|Chromatic Lantern]] at {3} makes every land a Sultai source and taps for any colour. That is the green-heavy Maralen-cast problem — but it is the *late* version. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Rosethorn Acolyte|Rosethorn Acolyte]] already pays {U} or {B} off Forests on turn 1–2. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Urborg Elf|Urborg Elf]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maraleaf Pixie|Maraleaf Pixie]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elves of Deep Shadow|Elves of Deep Shadow]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bloom Tender|Bloom Tender]], and [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Arcane Signet|Arcane Signet]] cover the same pips cheaper. Not an Elf or Faerie, so it does not mill. 🟢 Owned extra — named cut required.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it only if the board is already Forests and you still cannot pay {U}{B} for Maralen next turn.
- After Maralen is down this is just a rock. Sequence an Elf ETB over tapping it if you need the mill.
- Do not keep a one-land hand hoping this fixes it — you need three mana first.
