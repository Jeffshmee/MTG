## Deck Role & Rating

Haste and shroud on the equipped creature. Equip `{0}`. Slap it on [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] the turn she enters. Live in the 100. Shroud means *you* cannot target her either: [[02 Cards/Instants/Origin of Metalbending|Origin of Metalbending]]'s save, [[02 Cards/Lands/Rogue's Passage|Rogue's Passage]].

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Equip Maralen immediately so the wipe and the spot removal bounce off.
- Haste lets her attack the turn she lands (4 power, flying).
- Move the boots to another creature if you need to target her with Origin or Passage.
- Shroud is only on the equipped creature. [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]] is still the board-wide save.

## Key Synergies

- **Wearer**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Haste also good on**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]]
- **Other protection**: [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Scion of Oona|Scion of Oona]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Shroud stops your own targeting: Origin's +1/+1 / indestructible, Rogue's Passage.
- Does not save the rest of the board. A wrath still kills every Elf that is not wearing the boots.
