## Oracle Text

As this artifact enters, [[03 Effects/Choose Type|choose Elemental, Elf, Faerie, Giant, Goblin, Kithkin, Merfolk, or Treefolk]].
[[03 Effects/ETB|Whenever a permanent you control of the chosen type enters]], [[03 Effects/Gain Life|you gain 1 life]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Pennant is the third recursion outlet. Name Elf: each Elf permanent that enters gains you 1 life, and `{2}`, tap, sacrifice returns an Elf card from the yard to hand. That is how you replay [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Archdruid]] when Findbroker and Regrower are not available. The life is incidental.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Name Elf. Sac it the turn a legend dies if you have `{2}` and no other outlet.
- Do not sac it for a dork. The card is the outlet, not the 1 life.
- After Maralen, Elf tokens (Lathril, Imperious) trigger the life and later can be the reason you have a yard.

## Key Synergies

- **Return these**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Perfect Morcant|High Perfect Morcant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril, Blade of the Elves]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]]
- **Other outlets**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Cannot return Banner, Uprising, or Panharmonicon. Those are not Elves.
- Sac is the card. You get one shot unless Wave or recursion finds another Pennant (there isn't one).
- Naming Faerie only makes sense after Bearer / Bitterblossom arrive and the legends are not the thing dying.
