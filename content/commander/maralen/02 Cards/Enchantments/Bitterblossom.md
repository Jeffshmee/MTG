## Oracle Text

At the beginning of your upkeep, [[03 Effects/Lose Life|you lose 1 life]] and [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a 1/1 black Faerie Rogue creature token]] with [[03 Effects/Flying|flying]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Wishlist repeating Faerie tokens. Each upkeep you lose 1 life and make a 1/1 flying Faerie Rogue. After [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] is down, every token mills two and raises the steal count. Kindred Enchantment — Faerie, so the enchantment itself is a Faerie permanent and mills when it enters. Wishlist only — not purchased, not in the owned list.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Get Maralen down first so each upkeep token is a mill.
- The tokens are Faeries. They count for the steal even if [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]] is named Elf — they just do not get the anthem.
- Kindred Faerie: [[02 Cards/Lands/Secluded Glen|Secluded Glen]] can reveal this from hand to enter untapped.
- Life loss adds up. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]] named Elf does not offset Faerie tokens.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Other Faerie engine**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bitterbloom Bearer|Bitterbloom Bearer]]
- **Pending Faerie engine**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Alela, Cunning Conqueror|Alela]]
- **Reveal for**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Secluded Glen|Secluded Glen]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet. Not purchased.
- One life a turn. This list has almost no lifegain once Pennant is named Elf.
- Tokens miss Banner / Archdruid pumps. They are still mills.
