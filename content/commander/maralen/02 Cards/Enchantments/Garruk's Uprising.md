## Oracle Text

Creatures you control have [[03 Effects/Trample|trample]]. (Each of those creatures can deal excess [[03 Effects/Combat Damage|combat damage]] to the player or planeswalker it's attacking.)

## Deck Role & Rating

Uprising is the owned combat draw engine. Creatures you control have trample, and a creature with power 4 or greater entering draws a card. Lords ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Banner]]) turn ordinary Elves into 4-power ETBs. It is a Findbroker / Regrower target and a Wave hit. It does not mill.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it after a lord is in play so the next Elf is already a draw. Maralen herself is 4/5 — she draws if Uprising is out first.
- Lathril tokens are 1/1s. They do not draw unless a lord has already pumped the team *before* they enter. Sequence the lord first.
- If it dies, get it back. Trample is how Lathril connects through a chump.

## Key Synergies

- **Make 4-power ETBs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morcant's Loyalist|Morcant's Loyalist]]
- **Replay it**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]]
- **Trample for**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does nothing the turn you play it unless a 4-power creature enters after it.
- Token swarms without a lord are trample-only.
- Not an Elf. Wave hits it; Maralen does not trigger off it.
