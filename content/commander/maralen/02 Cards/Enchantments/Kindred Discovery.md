## Deck Role & Rating

Kindred Discovery is the wishlist draw engine. Name Elf: every Elf that enters or attacks draws a card. Token turns (Lathril, Imperious) become empty-hand refills; Wave becomes a new grip. It is a Findbroker / Regrower target the moment it is in the yard. Wishlist — not purchased, not in the owned list.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Name Elf. Name Faerie only after Bearer / Bitterblossom / Alela make that the majority.
- Cast it after Maralen and a token maker. The next Warrior is a card.
- If it dies, get it back ahead of Banner. Draw wins more games than +1/+1 once the board exists.

## Key Synergies

- **Token draw**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dwynen's Elite|Dwynen's Elite]]
- **Replay it**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Regrower|Elvish Regrower]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet. Wishlist, not Ordered.
- Draws you out if you name Elf and then dump a Wave you cannot stop. Play around empty library.
- Does not mill. It refills *you*.
