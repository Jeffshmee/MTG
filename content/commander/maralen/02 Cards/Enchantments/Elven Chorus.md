## Oracle Text

You may [[03 Effects/Look at Top|look at the top card of your library]] any time.
You may cast creature spells from the top of your library.
Creatures you control have "{T}: [[03 Effects/Add Mana|Add]] one mana of any color."

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Chorus is the “team taps for anything + cast Elves off the top” enchantment. The any-colour tap is the land-mix card: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Findbroker]] and [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sign in Blood|Sign in Blood]] stop waiting on Watery Grave, and Maralen’s {U} comes from an Elf instead of an Island. Casting a creature from the top still mills if [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] is in play — it is a real ETB, not a cheat-into-play. You still pay the mana. Not in the owned list yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Resolve it, then tap dorks for {B}{U}{G} and cast the top creature if it is an Elf or Faerie.
- If the top is a land or a noncreature, leave it; you can still look. Do not mill it for no reason.
- Cast Maralen from the top only if the pips are already in pool. Four mana for Chorus first.
- Faeries tap for colours too. Use that on a Trickster / Sprite board.

## Key Synergies

- **Spend any colour**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sign in Blood|Sign in Blood]]
- **Top of library**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lys Alana Informant|Lys Alana Informant]]
- **Wide tap team**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not in the owned list yet.
- Does not cast enchantments or artifacts from the top. Incubator and Kindred stay in hand.
- Tapping the team for mana fights attacking. On a Lathril turn, leave bodies up that you intend to swing.
