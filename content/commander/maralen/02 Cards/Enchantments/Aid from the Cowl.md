## Oracle Text

Revolt — At the beginning of your end step, if a permanent left the battlefield under your control this turn, [[03 Effects/Look at Top|reveal the top card of your library]]. If it's a permanent card, you may [[03 Effects/ETB|put it onto the battlefield]]. Otherwise, you may put it on the bottom of your library.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Five-mana revolt cheat: at the end of turn, if a permanent left the battlefield under your control this turn, reveal the top card and you may put a permanent onto the battlefield. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Springbloom Druid|Springbloom Druid]] and [[02 Cards/Lands/Evolving Wilds|Evolving Wilds]] turn it on; an Elf hitting the battlefield mills if [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] is already up. Not tribal — it does not count and it does not mill on its own, and this list does not leave permanents every turn. Owned (in hand). Sitting on the sideboard until a main-deck slot is confirmed. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Wilderness Reclamation|Wilderness Reclamation]] took Highspire Artisan; this still needs a named cut.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Enable revolt the same turn: Springbloom (sacrifice a land) or Evolving Wilds, then hope the top is a creature or engine.
- A dead dork, a sacrificed token, or a fetch is enough. Do not spend a legend just to turn this on.
- If the cheat is an Elf and Maralen is in play, that ETB mills. A land or a non-Elf engine is still a free permanent.
- Flash Faeries dying on an opponent's turn also count for revolt on your end step.

## Key Synergies

- **Revolt enablers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Springbloom Druid|Springbloom Druid]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Evolving Wilds|Evolving Wilds]]
- **If the cheat is an Elf**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **What you hope to hit**: Maralen, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Patchwork Banner|Patchwork Banner]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Garruk's Uprising|Garruk's Uprising]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Five mana. The revolt clause misses if nothing left this turn.
- Reveals an instant or sorcery and you may only put it on the bottom.
- Not an Elf. Does not mill. Does not raise the steal count.
- Owned (in hand). Sitting on the sideboard until a main-deck slot is confirmed.
