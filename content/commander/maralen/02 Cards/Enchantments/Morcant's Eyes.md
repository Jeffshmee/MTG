## Oracle Text

At the beginning of your upkeep, [[03 Effects/Surveil|surveil 1]].
{4}{G}{G}, [[03 Effects/Sacrifice|Sacrifice]] this enchantment: [[03 Effects/Create Token|Create X 2/2 black and green Elf creature tokens]], where X is the number of Elf cards in your graveyard. Activate only as a sorcery.

## Deck Role & Rating

Kindred Enchantment — Elf. Surveil 1 on your upkeep, then `{4}{G}{G}`, sacrifice: create X 2/2 black and green Elf tokens, where X is the number of Elf cards in your graveyard. Yard-matters with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glowspore Shaman|Glowspore Shaman]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lluwen, Imperfect Naturalist|Lluwen]]. The enchantment itself is an Elf permanent, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] mills when it enters. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Surveil Elves into the yard on purpose if you plan to sac soon. Lands and non-Elves can stay on top.
- Sac after Maralen so each 2/2 mills. Activate only as a sorcery.
- Glowspore mills three; Lluwen mills four. Both feed X. This card in the yard is itself an Elf card.
- [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Distant Melody|Distant Melody]] named Elf counts it (permanent). [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]] named Elf gains life on the ETB.

## Key Synergies

- **Fill the yard**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glowspore Shaman|Glowspore Shaman]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lluwen, Imperfect Naturalist|Lluwen]]
- **The tokens mill**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Count it as an Elf permanent**: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Distant Melody|Distant Melody]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Sac is a sorcery and costs six. Need a real yard or X is 0–1.
- You lose the surveil engine when you cash it.
- Tokens are Elves. They are not Faeries.
