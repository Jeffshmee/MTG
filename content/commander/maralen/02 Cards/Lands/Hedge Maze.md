## Deck Role & Rating

Hedge Maze is a mana source for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]'s Elves plus Faeries list. UG surveil shock. Untapped dual that also looks at the top. Basics plus [[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]] are the live untapped core; named duals on the sideboard fill as they arrive.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Enters tapped and surveils 1. Sequence it after an untapped source.
- Forest Island, so [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Farseek|Farseek]] can find it.
