## Deck Role & Rating

Wastewood Verge is the untapped black source this list was missing. {G} is always on; {B} turns on as soon as a Forest or Swamp is in play, which this 100 already has in bulk ([[02 Cards/Lands/Forest|Forest]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Swamp|Swamp]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Overgrown Tomb|Overgrown Tomb]]). That is how you pay [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]]'s {B}{B} without shocking. It replaced one FDN 280 Forest. It is **not** a Forest or a Swamp itself — [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Farseek|Farseek]] cannot find it, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arbor Elf|Arbor Elf]] cannot untap it unless [[02 Cards/Lands/Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth|Yavimaya]] is making every land a Forest.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it untapped. Default tap is {G} toward Maralen or a dork; tap {B} when Findbroker, Trophy, or Maralen's {B} is the pip you are short.
- Keep at least one Forest or Swamp in play before you need the {B}. A Verge as your only land does not make black.
- Sequence with Yavimaya so Arbor Elf can untap it; that is extra {G} or {B} from one dork.
- Do not fetch it. Farseek still wants Tomb / Pool / Grave.

## Key Synergies

- **{B}{B}**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Golgari Findbroker|Golgari Findbroker]], [[02 Cards/Instants/Assassin's Trophy|Assassin's Trophy]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]]
- **Turns it into a Forest**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth|Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth]] (then [[02 Cards/Creatures/Arbor Elf|Arbor Elf]] untaps it)
- **Other BG**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Overgrown Tomb|Overgrown Tomb]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not a Forest or Swamp type. Farseek misses it. Arbor Elf misses it without Yavimaya.
- First land of the game: {G} only. Do not keep a hand that needs {B} off Verge alone.
- Replaced one Forest. Green is still saturated; do not cut another Forest for a second dual unless named.
