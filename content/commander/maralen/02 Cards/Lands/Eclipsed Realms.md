## Deck Role & Rating

Type-chooser land. Name Elf. {T}: {C}, or any colour that can only be spent to cast a spell of the chosen type or activate an ability of a source of the chosen type. That pays for Elf creature spells — including [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] — and for Elf abilities ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]] tap, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]] tap-ten, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]] token). Broader than [[02 Cards/Lands/Secluded Courtyard|Secluded Courtyard]], which is creature-only.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Name Elf. Always, on this owned list.
- Use the coloured mana on Maralen, Archdruid, Lathril, or an Elf dork. Not on [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]], or [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]].
- The colourless tap is still a land. Do not strand yourself if the next spell is not an Elf.

## Key Synergies

- **Pays for**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Imperious Perfect|Imperious Perfect]]
- **Sister land (creature-only)**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Secluded Courtyard|Secluded Courtyard]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Naming Faerie on this Elf-majority pile is a mistake.
- Coloured mana cannot pay Wave, Rhythm, Banner, or Negate.
- Kindred Elf spells ([[02 Cards/Enchantments/Morcant's Eyes|Morcant's Eyes]]) can use it; Courtyard cannot.
