## Deck Role & Rating

Tapped Sultai fixer. `{T}`: one mana of any colour in your commander's identity. When that mana is spent to cast a creature spell that shares a type with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]], scry 1. Commander is Elf Faerie, so Elf *or* Faerie creature spells scry. There is no type to name. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Comes in tapped. Do not play it on the turn you need to cast Maralen.
- Spend it on Elf creature spells (the majority) or the flash Faeries to scry toward Wave / the next legend.
- Spending it on [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]] or [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]] is still legal. You just miss the scry.

## Key Synergies

- **Scry when you cast**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamer Gifter|Glamer Gifter]]
- **Commander**: Maralen (Elf and Faerie — both types scry)

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Enters tapped. Slows the Maralen turn.
- No type-chooser. You do not "name Elf" here.
