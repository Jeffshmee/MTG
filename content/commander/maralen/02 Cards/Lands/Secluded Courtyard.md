## Deck Role & Rating

Type-chooser land. Name Elf. `{T}`: `{C}`, or any colour that can only be spent to cast a *creature* spell of the chosen type or activate an ability of a *creature* source of the chosen type. Pays for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen]] and the Elf dorks. Tighter than [[02 Cards/Lands/Eclipsed Realms|Eclipsed Realms]] — no noncreature Elf spells, no noncreature Elf sources.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Name Elf. Use the coloured mana on Maralen, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]], or a one-drop.
- Archdruid's tap ability is a creature source, so Courtyard can help pay to activate it if you ever need coloured mana there — you usually do not.
- Do not try to pay [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Genesis Wave|Genesis Wave]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Nature's Rhythm|Nature's Rhythm]], Banner, or [[02 Cards/Instants/Negate|Negate]] with the coloured tap.

## Key Synergies

- **Pays for**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maralen, Fae Ascendant|Maralen, Fae Ascendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elvish Archdruid|Elvish Archdruid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Lathril, Blade of the Elves|Lathril]]
- **Sister land (any Elf spell/ability)**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Eclipsed Realms|Eclipsed Realms]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Naming Faerie on this owned list is wrong.
- Creature spells only. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Morcant's Eyes|Morcant's Eyes]] (kindred enchantment) cannot use this mana.
- Colourless tap still exists. Use it when the next spell is not an Elf creature.
