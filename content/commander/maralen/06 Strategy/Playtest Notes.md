# Playtest Notes

Table observations for this list. **Read this page before recommending an add or a cut.** A Combined score does not override a note that a card wins games, sits in hand, or opens a hole.

See also [[06 Strategy/Play Patterns|Play Patterns]] and [[06 Strategy/Win Conditions|Win Conditions]].

---

> [!info] Rosethorn Acolyte stays — Maralen-cast fix
> Do not cut Rosethorn for Bloom Tender. The Adventure is any-color mana with no gold permanent required. That is how green-heavy hands actually cast Maralen.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Keep [[02 Cards/Creatures/Rosethorn Acolyte|Rosethorn Acolyte]] in the 100. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bloom Tender|Bloom Tender]] is Vivid: without a blue or black permanent it taps for one green. Rosethorn’s Adventure pays {U} or {B} off Forests on turn 1–2. Same reason not to spend [[02 Cards/Creatures/Llanowar Loamspeaker|Llanowar Loamspeaker]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Maraleaf Pixie|Maraleaf Pixie]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elves of Deep Shadow|Elves of Deep Shadow]] on Bloom.
> > **Reinforce:** Bloom’s locked cut is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Wardens of the Cycle|Wardens of the Cycle]] (75) — four-mana morbid draw, not a fixer. Mastermind now covers draw.
> > **Overcome:** three mana for the creature side is slow if Loamspeaker is already out.

---

> [!info] Wilderness Reclamation is the flash-turn engine
> Play a full hand, untap at end step, then flash Faeries on their turn with no leftover-mana tax. Cornerstone — do not cut it. Combined 72 underweights this line.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Keep [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Wilderness Reclamation|Wilderness Reclamation]] in the 100. The score reads as “not tribal extra land mana.” The actual job is empty-the-hand on your turn, then mill and steal on *their* turn with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamermite|Glamermite]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Scion of Oona|Scion of Oona]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Spellstutter Sprite|Spellstutter Sprite]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/High Fae Trickster|High Fae Trickster]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mocking Sprite|Mocking Sprite]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Faerie Mastermind|Faerie Mastermind]]. Leyline makes Wave / Maralen flash after the untap.
> > **Reinforce:** never offer it as a cut for Circle, Bloom, Mastermind, Sol Ring, or Aid. Score-only ranking does not apply.
> > **Overcome:** four mana, not an Elf. A Reclamation with no flash in hand is just extra land mana.

---

> [!info] Mocking Sprite stays
> Instant and sorcery cost reduction is too useful to cut. Do not swap it for Faerie Mastermind.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Keep [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mocking Sprite|Mocking Sprite]] in the 100. The discount is for spells you pay for: Wave, Rhythm, Negate, Heritage, Trophy. Stolen spells are already free; Sprite is for the rest of the hand, including the flash package after Reclamation untaps.
> > **Reinforce:** Mastermind’s slot is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Illusion Spinners|Illusion Spinners]], not Sprite.
> > **Overcome:** 2/1. If they kill it in response, Wave costs full price.

---

> [!info] Shessra forces a block into deathtouch
> Shessra is too useful to cut. Play her to force a big card to block a deathtouch creature for cheap removal.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Keep [[02 Cards/Creatures/Shessra, Death's Whisper|Shessra, Death's Whisper]] in the 100. The Bewitching Whispers ETB makes a creature block this turn if able — point a deathtouch body ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Thornweald Archer|Thornweald Archer]], a blighted leftover, or a stolen deathtoucher) at their best creature. That is a removal line, not just conditional draw.
> > **Reinforce:** do not offer her as a cut for a pending add. Do not cut Thornweald while this line is live.
> > **Overcome:** she is still a 1/3 Human Elf with no mill of her own. If the table has no creatures worth forcing, she is only the morbid draw.

---

> [!info] Dawn-Blessed Pennant stays
> Life on ETB and recursion — too useful to cut for Lightning Greaves.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Dawn-Blessed Pennant|Dawn-Blessed Pennant]] is a named-type recursion outlet (name Elf) plus life on every Elf/Faerie ETB. Combined 80 is lower than Greaves (86), but the table value is unique: Findbroker/Regrower return any permanent, Loyalist returns an Elf on death, Pennant returns a named type from the yard on a sacrifice.
> > **Reinforce:** do not cut Pennant for a rock or for Greaves. Recursion priority on Play Patterns already lists it.
> > **Committed 2026-08-21:** Greaves entered over [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glowspore Shaman|Glowspore Shaman]], not Pennant.

---

> [!info] Opulent Palace is the three-colour gap filler
> Even though it enters tapped, it fixes all three identities. Too good a gap filler for Maralen to cut for Breeding Pool.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Keep [[02 Cards/Lands/Opulent Palace|Opulent Palace]]. It is the only tapped land that makes {B}, {G}, *and* {U} without a type restriction. Command Tower and shocks cover two-colour pairs; Palace covers the awkward Maralen pip when the shocks are the wrong two.
> > **Reinforce:** do not cut it land-for-land for a shock. Breeding Pool replaced a Forest instead.
> > **Overcome:** it enters tapped. Do not keep a one-land hand that is only Palace.

---

> [!info] Firdoch Core stays — skip Sol Ring
> Firdoch has changeling, so it triggers Maralen. Prefer leaving Sol Ring out of the 100 rather than cutting the changeling rock.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Firdoch Core|Firdoch Core]] is every creature type. Banner, Pennant, Eclipsed Realms, Secluded Courtyard, and Maralen all count it. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Sol Ring|Sol Ring]] is faster colourless mana and does not mill.
> > **Reinforce:** do not cut Firdoch for Sol Ring. Sol Ring stays owned on the sideboard until a different named cut (or stays out).
> > **Committed 2026-08-21:** Sol Ring was left out of the 100 on purpose.

---

> [!info] Glowspore Shaman underperformed
> Not the best in playtesting. Better land-fixing options now. Cut for Lightning Greaves.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glowspore Shaman|Glowspore Shaman]] mills you three and can stack a land. Self-mill for Findbroker is real, but the card sat as a 3/1 that did not fix {U} or {B} the way the new duals and Deep Shadow do.
> > **Reinforce:** do not put it back while Watery Grave, Breeding Pool, Elves of Deep Shadow, and Farseek are in the 100.
> > **Committed 2026-08-21:** owned cut. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]] took the slot.

---

> [!info] Glamer Gifter is fine — Scion is better
> Gifter is ok, but Scion of Oona is a better swap.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Creatures/Glamer Gifter|Glamer Gifter]] is a two-mana flash Faerie with a one-turn 4/4 type-gift. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Scion of Oona|Scion of Oona]] is a flash Faerie lord that gives other Faeries (including Maralen) shroud. Combined 82 vs 88.
> > **Reinforce:** Gifter's pump is one turn and it does not protect Maralen. Scion does.
> > **Committed 2026-08-21:** Gifter is an owned cut. Scion is in the 100.

---

> [!info] Greenwood Sentinel lost the Heroic slot
> Like the body, low cost, and vigilance — but Heroic Intervention is the board-wide save, not a second copy of Origin of Metalbending.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Do not cut [[02 Cards/Instants/Origin of Metalbending|Origin of Metalbending]] for [[02 Cards/Instants/Heroic Intervention|Heroic Intervention]]. Origin is removal or a protected body; Heroic is hexproof and indestructible on the whole board. Those are different jobs.
> > [[02 Cards/Creatures/Greenwood Sentinel|Greenwood Sentinel]] is honest 2/2 vigilance filler (Combined 68). The user liked the body; the board-wide save still outranks it.
> > **Committed 2026-08-21:** Greenwood is the owned cut for Heroic.
