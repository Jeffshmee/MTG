# Adventure

A spell type on the back of some creatures. You cast the Adventure, then exile the card and may cast the creature later from exile.

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Rosethorn Acolyte|Rosethorn Acolyte]] is a mana Adventure; [[02 Cards/Creatures/Thranduil, Sindarin Liege|Thranduil, Sindarin Liege]] mills four and finds lands. The Adventure is not a creature — it does not mill via Maralen.
