# Win Conditions

How this deck actually closes a table. All three plans run through [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]] and a stream of tokens.

Play order and sequencing live on [[06 Strategy/Play Patterns|Play Patterns]]. Table observations live on [[06 Strategy/Playtest Notes|Playtest Notes]]. Card scores live on the individual pages and in [[01 Deck/Current Deck|Current Deck]].

**Draft note:** primary and secondary plans below are live in the current 100. Isshin, Teysa, Packbeasts, Bone-Cairn, Anim Pakal, Belladonna, Windcrag, Sacred Foundry, [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Mardu Ascendancy|Mardu Ascendancy]], and [[02 Cards/Lands/Path of Ancestry|Path of Ancestry]] are in. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kambal, Profiteering Mayor|Kambal]] is a sideboard sleeve vs token decks — see [[06 Strategy/Play Patterns|Play Patterns]].

## 1. Combat — go wide

Overwhelm with tokens plus anthems, extra combat, and evasion.

**How it wins:** Mobilize and attack-token makers flood the board. Combat damage (and commander damage from Zurgo) knocks players out. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] turns the same tokens into 4/4 Angels.

**Live in the 100**

- Token engines: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Loyal Apprentice|Loyal Apprentice]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Mardu Ascendancy|Mardu Ascendancy]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Heroic Reinforcements|Heroic Reinforcements]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Lingering Souls|Lingering Souls]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Tempt with Vengeance|Tempt with Vengeance]]
- Pump / extra combat: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ogre Battledriver|Ogre Battledriver]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Castle Embereth|Castle Embereth]]
- Attack doublers: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]]
- Closer: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] (playtest: real win-con once the token stream is on)

This is the default plan. If you have Zurgo and any second token maker, keep attacking until the table is dead or someone wipes.

## 2. Drain — tokens enter and leave

Life totals melt without needing to connect.

**How it wins:** each token ETB and each death (or leave) taxes the table. A single Zurgo attack is already 1 damage from Tremors and 1 life loss per opponent from Bastion when the Mobilize token dies — times three opponents.

**Live in the 100**

- ETB damage: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]]
- Death drain: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Boggart Mischief|Boggart Mischief]] (Goblins), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] (ETB gain + death drain)
- Attack-or-death split: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] (leave), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]] (dies)
- Burn on a wide board: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] token-count damage; [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Within Range|Within Range]] on attack

- Death doubler: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] — doubles death drains (and Zurgo, when the token **dies**)

Stack Bastion + Tremors + Zurgo + Elas first. That package alone wins medium-length games. Teysa makes it a primary close.

## 3. Extra combat / doubled triggers

Same creatures, twice (or three times) the Mobilize and attack tokens.

**How it wins:** one extra combat retriggers Zurgo, Adeline, Hero, Packbeasts, Anim Pakal, Caesar. Isshin / Windcrag make each of those triggers fire twice *inside* a combat.

**Live in the 100**

- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia, the Warleader]]
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]]
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]] (always name Mardu)

Aurelia + Zurgo + Adeline is already a kill. Isshin + Aurelia is three combats of doubled triggers.

## How to choose a plan in-game

- **You have Zurgo and a clear attack** → plan 1. Add Tremors/Bastion in the background; they will finish whoever combat does not.
- **The table is fogging, pillowforting, or chump-blocking forever** → lean on plan 2. Stop needing to connect.
- **You drew Aurelia / All-Out Assault / Isshin with a live board** → plan 3. Point the extra combat at the player who would otherwise stabilize.
- **Zurgo is stuck in the command zone at 6+ mana** → you can still go wide with Adeline, Hero, Legion Warboss, and spells, but close faster. Recast him the moment you can protect him.

---

## What does *not* win

- Sitting on rocks and waiting. This is not a control deck.
- A single big creature with no tokens. Myr Battlesphere and Sun Titan are support, not the plan.
- Divine Visitation with no token makers left. The enchantment does nothing alone.
