# Playtest Notes

Table observations for this list. **Read this page before recommending an add or a cut.** A Combined score does not override a note that a card wins games, sits in hand, or opens a hole.

See also [[06 Strategy/Play Patterns|Play Patterns]] and [[06 Strategy/Win Conditions|Win Conditions]].

---

> [!info] Goldnight Commander — one-shot closer
> Goldnight Commander has serious one-shot potential when stacking tokens. Already won several games with this on the field.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Treat [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] as a live closer, not a generic anthem. Each creature that enters (Zurgo Mobilize, Adeline Humans, Caesar Soldiers, Hero, Warboss) pumps the whole team until end of turn, so one attack with a token wave can end a player.
> > **Reinforce:** keep him in the 100. Sequence him *before* the wave, not after an empty board. Extra combat ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]) and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] make the same stack lethal. Do not offer him as a cut for a pending add.
> > **Overcome:** he does nothing the turn he resolves if you make no tokens. Hold him when the board is empty; cast him when Zurgo or another engine will attack the same turn.

---

> [!info] Zurgo gets stolen
> Zurgo has been stolen a few times and used by opponents because he synergises well with their decks and the token trigger. May need more protection than just Lightning Greaves — draw rate is too low to rely on finding it. May need more protection in the list, or ways to tutor it.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Mobilize and the token-leave trigger work for *whoever controls Zurgo*. A theft spell turns your commander into their engine. The 100’s only dedicated suit is [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]]; [[02 Cards/Instants/Grand Crescendo|Grand Crescendo]] covers wipes, not theft.
> > **Reinforce:** do not cut Greaves. Prefer haste-and-shroud/hexproof over more raw tokens when discussing upgrades. Recast Zurgo only when you can protect him (Play Patterns already says this).
> > **Overcome / candidates to consider (not in the 100):** a second suit (Swiftfoot Boots), a recapture land (Homeward Path), or a cheap protection instant (Boros Charm, Flawless Maneuver). A tutor that finds Greaves only helps if draw is the real problem — [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] / [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Idol of Oblivion|Idol]] are already the finders. Flag this hole on every protection-light audit. Do not silently add a card.

---

> [!info] Chittering Witch sits in hand
> When Chittering Witch is in hand, four mana to summon one or two tokens (depending on opponent count) is a weak option. Usually cast only if there is nothing better; often sits unplayed.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Creatures/Chittering Witch|Chittering Witch]] sat as the weakest live four-drop: Rats equal to opponents (two in a typical three-player leftover, one heads-up) plus a {1}{B} sac for -2/-2. Caesar occupies that mana slot with a repeatable attack engine.
> > **Reinforce:** her real use was the activated ability — sac a Mobilize token to shrink a blocker — not the ETB. Do not count her as a token engine if she returns.
> > **Committed 2026-08-20:** Scratch named her as the cut for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Belladonna Took|Belladonna Took]]. She is an owned cut on the sideboard.

---

> [!info] Divine Visitation is a win condition
> Divine Visitation has been a win-con card with the amount of tokens this deck makes.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] is plan 1’s closer, not a luxury anthem. Every Mobilize / Adeline / Caesar / spell token becomes a 4/4 Angel.
> > **Reinforce:** keep her. Cast her when at least one token engine is already on board (Play Patterns). Pair with [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Tempt with Vengeance|Tempt with Vengeance]] and extra combat.
> > **Overcome:** after she resolves, [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] no longer kills the tokens (4/4, or 5/3 equipped). Do not Clamp, and do not offer her as a cut.

---

> [!info] Tempt with Vengeance overperformed
> Tempt with Vengeance has had more utility than expected. If one player is clearly ahead and a weaker player takes the offer, the extra Elementals plus Divine Visitation or Ogre Battledriver become a potential one-shot.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Tempt with Vengeance|Tempt with Vengeance]] is a political burst, not a fair X-spell. Point it when one player is the table threat so a third player is willing to make Elementals and feed you.
> > **Reinforce:** keep it. Cast with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ogre Battledriver|Ogre Battledriver]] or [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] already out, or with Goldnight about to see a pile of ETBs. Haste on the Elementals means they attack immediately.
> > **Overcome:** if every opponent is even and nobody takes the offer, you still get X Elementals — cast it anyway when X is large. Do not cut it for a “cleaner” token spell.

---

> [!info] Kambal as a token-matchup sleeve
> Keep Kambal, Profiteering Mayor sleeved on the sideboard. He would be a strong conditional swap against players who create tokens — for example Mark’s Spirit deck, which makes tokens as cards leave the graveyard to exile. The copies enter tapped, so they are blockers by the time those tokens can attack. After playtesting the matchup, a committed cut can be named later.
>
> > [!note]- AI Comments
> > Default 100 stays without [[02 Cards/Creatures/Kambal, Profiteering Mayor|Kambal]] (Combined 74). Sleeve him in pre-game when a token engine is the problem. Ranked take-outs and the haste/attacking-token caveat live on [[06 Strategy/Play Patterns|Play Patterns]] → **Matchup sleeve — Kambal vs token decks**. Default take-out is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sandskitter Outrider|Sandskitter Outrider]]. Do not treat a good Mark game as a standing include until a cut is named.
