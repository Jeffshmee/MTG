## Deck Role & Rating

Prismatic Ending is flexible exile at {X}{W}. With {R}{W}{B} it hits mana value 3. [[02 Cards/Instants/Swords to Plowshares|Swords to Plowshares]] is already the better white exile (instant, any creature). This fights [[02 Cards/Instants/Mortify|Mortify]] for a removal slot — Mortify is instant and hits any-size creature or an enchantment. Suggested cut: Mortify. Do not cut Swords, Goldnight, Divine Visitation, Tempt, or Greaves.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Pay {W} for a 0 or 1. Pay {R}{W}{B} for a 3-drop nonland.
- Cannot hit lands. Sorcery speed.
