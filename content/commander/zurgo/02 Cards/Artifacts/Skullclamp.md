## Deck Role & Rating

Skullclamp is the deck's best piece of card advantage.  
A 1/1 token becomes 2/0 and dies, so you draw two from Clamp and still get Zurgo / Garna / Bastion / Elas on the death. Mobilize tokens that would die at end of turn can be clamped first for extra cards.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Default: equip a 1-toughness token, draw two, then the death payoffs fire.
- On a 2-toughness body it is a +1/-1 pump until you find a 1/1.
- Clamp an attacking Mobilize token: Zurgo draws (left attacking), Garna draws (died attacking), Clamp draws two.
- Equip cost is {1}. Keep one mana up after you make tokens.

## Key Synergies

- **1-toughness tokens**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]]
- **Death payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]]
- **Sac / extra tokens**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] makes 4/4 Angels. Clamp becomes +1/-1 on a 5/3 and no longer kills the token.
- Do not clamp Zurgo. The -1 toughness is a real way to lose the game.
- Repeatable, but each equip costs mana.
