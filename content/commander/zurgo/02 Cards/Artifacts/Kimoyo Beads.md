## Oracle Text

At the beginning of your end step, choose one that hasn't been chosen —  
• AV Bead — [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|Draw a card]].  
• Communication Bead — [[03 Effects/Create Token|Create two 1/1 white Soldier creature tokens]].  
• Prime Bead — You [[03 Effects/Gain Life|gain 3 life]]. [[03 Effects/Exile|Exile]] this artifact, then return it to the battlefield under its owner's control.

## Deck Role & Rating

Kimoyo Beads is a four-mana looping engine: each end step pick a mode that has not been chosen, then Prime Bead flickers it and the cycle starts again. AV Bead is the card; Communication Bead is two Soldiers into Tremors, Elas, and Zurgo (drain, because they enter at end step and cannot attack that turn); Prime Bead is 3 life and a reset. Combined 76 is a tie with Sandskitter / Anafenza / Stormbeacon. **Do not cut for it.** Playtest already had four-drops sitting (Chittering), and the real hole is protection, not another end-step engine. Owned; stay on the sideboard.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- If it entered the 100: default order is **draw → Soldiers → reset**, unless you need the tokens the same turn Tremors or Elas is already out.
- End-step Soldiers do not attack that turn. Zurgo drains if they die before your next combat; attack with them next turn so a later leave draws.
- The flicker is a new object. Modes reset; you do not skip a turn of the engine.
- Four mana. Do not tap out into it on a turn you needed Zurgo or a wipe-answer.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Combined 76 is ±5 of Sandskitter / Anafenza / Stormbeacon. Recommendation: do not cut. Stay on the sideboard.
- End-step tokens miss the combat they entered on. They are slower Mobilize.
- Four mana, no body. Artifact removal (Abrade) answers the whole loop.
