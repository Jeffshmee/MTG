## Deck Role & Rating

Mardu Banner is a three-mana rock that taps for {R}, {W}, or {B} and can pay those three pips plus the tap to sac itself for a card. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Arcane Signet|Arcane Signet]] is cheaper fixing; [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Mardu Monument|Mardu Monument]] is cheaper and dumps three Warriors. The sac-draw is a late flood valve, not an engine. Owned; stay on the sideboard. Not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- If it entered the 100: tap it for Zurgo colours. Only sac it when you are flooding and the card matters more than the rock.
- The sac costs {R}{W}{B} and the tap — you cannot float the Banner’s own mana into the activation the same tap.
- Do not run it next to Signet as a second three-drop rock. The slot is already full.

## Key Synergies

- **Cheaper rocks already in**: [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Sol Ring|Sol Ring]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Arcane Signet|Arcane Signet]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Mardu Monument|Mardu Monument]]
- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Owned; stay in the sideboard. Not a reason to cut Signet or Monument.
- Three mana to cast, three more to draw. Slow.
- Sac is not a creature death. Zurgo, Bastion, and Teysa do not care.
