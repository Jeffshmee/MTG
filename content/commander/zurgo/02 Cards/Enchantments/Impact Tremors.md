## Oracle Text

Whenever a creature you control enters, this enchantment [[03 Effects/Deal Damage|deals 1 damage]] to each opponent.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Impact Tremors is the ETB half of the burn plan.  
Bastion and Elas tax deaths; Tremors taxes the enter. A single Zurgo attack is 1 damage to each opponent on the token ETB, then more drain when it leaves.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it turn 2, Zurgo turn 3, attack. The first token already pings the table.
- Isshin does **not** double Tremors (not an attack-caused trigger on Tremors itself). Isshin does double the token-making triggers, so you still get twice the ETBs and twice the pings.
- Adeline in a four-player game is 3 damage from one attack. Packbeasts is 3. Anim Pakal scales.
- Stacks with Elas lifegain on the same ETBs.

- **Token makers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does not trigger off tokens that are already on the battlefield.
- Creature-only. Treasure, Food, and other noncreature tokens do not ping.
- Teysa does not double this (not a dies trigger).
