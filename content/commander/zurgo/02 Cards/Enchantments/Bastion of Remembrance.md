## Oracle Text

When this enchantment enters, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a 1/1 white Human Soldier creature token]].  
Whenever a creature you control dies, each [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|opponent loses 1 life]] and you [[03 Effects/Gain Life|gain 1 life]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Bastion is the main-deck aristocrat win condition.  
The ETB Soldier is a Clamp target or a Zurgo attacker. Every death after that — Mobilize end step, Skullclamp, combat, wipes — drains each opponent and pads your life.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it before you attack. The first Mobilize wave then drains on the end-step sacrifice.
- Teysa doubles the dies trigger: each death is 2 life lost by each opponent and 2 life gained.
- Stacks with Elas (more drain) and Impact Tremors (ETB damage on the way in).
- The ETB Soldier can attack immediately if you have haste, or sit as Clamp fodder.

## Key Synergies

- **Token deaths**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]]
- **Death doubler**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]]
- **Parallel drain**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]]
- **Forced deaths**: [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Exile-based removal skips the drain (Zurgo still sees the leave).
- Does nothing if your creatures stop dying. Token-stall effects that keep bodies around reduce its rate.
- One copy is enough; it is not legendary.
