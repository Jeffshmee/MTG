## Oracle Text

When this enchantment enters, you may [[03 Effects/Blight|blight]] 1. If you do, create two 1/1 black and red Goblin [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]]. (To [[03 Effects/Blight|blight]] 1, put a -1/-1 counter on a creature you control.)

Whenever a Goblin creature you control dies, each [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|opponent loses 1 life]] and [[03 Effects/Gain Life|you gain 1 life]].
