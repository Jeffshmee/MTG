## Oracle Text

As this enchantment enters, choose Mardu or Jeskai.  
• Mardu — If a creature attacking causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability [[03 Effects/Double Trigger|triggers an additional time]].  
• Jeskai — At the beginning of your upkeep, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a 1/1 red Goblin creature token]]. It gains [[03 Effects/Lifelink|lifelink]] and [[03 Effects/Haste|haste]] until end of turn.

## Deck Role & Rating

Windcrag Siege is Isshin as an enchantment if you name Mardu.  
That is the mode this deck wants: double Zurgo Mobilize, Adeline, Hero, Packbeasts, Anim Pakal, and Caesar. Jeskai is a consolation Goblin and almost never correct here. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Always choose **Mardu** in this list.
- Stacks with Isshin. Two replacement-style doublings mean each attack trigger happens three times.
- Play it the turn before you want to attack, or the same turn if you still have an attack left.
- Jeskai is only a consideration if you cannot attack for several turns. That is not this deck's plan.

- **Same effect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin, Two Heavens as One]]

- **Attack token makers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Mardu Ascendancy|Mardu Ascendancy]]
- **Extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The choice is permanent. A wrong Jeskai lock-in is a brick.
- Does not double death or ETB triggers.
- Enchantment removal (Mortify, etc.) answers it; creature wipes do not.
