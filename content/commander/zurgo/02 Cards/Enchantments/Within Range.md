## Oracle Text

When this enchantment enters, create two 1/1 red Warrior [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]].

Whenever you attack, each [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|opponent loses life]] equal to the number of creatures attacking them.
