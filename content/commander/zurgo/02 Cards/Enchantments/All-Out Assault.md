## Oracle Text

Creatures you control [[03 Effects/Pump Creatures|get +1/+1]] and have [[03 Effects/Deathtouch|deathtouch]].

When this enchantment enters, if it's your main phase, there is an [[03 Effects/Extra Combat|additional combat phase]] after this phase followed by an additional main phase. When you next attack this turn, [[03 Effects/Untap|untap each creature you control]].
