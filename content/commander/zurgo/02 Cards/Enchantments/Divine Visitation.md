## Oracle Text

If one or more [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]] would be created under your control, that many 4/4 white Angel [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]] with [[03 Effects/Flying|flying]] and [[03 Effects/Vigilance|vigilance]] are created instead.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Divine Visitation is the token-upgrade closer.  
It does not make tokens itself. Every token the rest of the deck makes — Mobilize Warriors, Adeline Humans, Hero Soldiers, Anim Pakal Gnomes — enters as a 4/4 Angel with flying and vigilance instead.

Mobilize's delayed sacrifice still happens, so you attack with 4/4 flyers and then they leave, drawing off Zurgo. That is the card's highest line.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Resolve this, then attack with Zurgo / Packbeasts / Adeline. Do not wait a turn if you can help it.
- Extra combat with Aurelia is usually lethal in the air.
- Isshin means twice the Angels from every attack trigger.
- The Angels are still tokens. Bastion, Elas, Garna, and Zurgo all still care when they leave.

## Key Synergies

- **Token makers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]]
- **Attack doublers / extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]]
- **Leave / death payoffs still apply**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] no longer kills the tokens (4/4 becomes 5/3).
- Does nothing if you are not making tokens.
- Replacement is not optional. You cannot make a 1/1 for Clamp while this is out.
