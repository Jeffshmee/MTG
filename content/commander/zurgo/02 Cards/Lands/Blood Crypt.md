As this land enters, you may [[03 Effects/Pain Land|pay 2 life]]. If you don't, it [[03 Effects/Enters Tapped|enters tapped]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Blood Crypt is the untapped BR shock: pay 2 life or it comes in tapped. [[02 Cards/Lands/Canyon Slough|Canyon Slough]] always entered tapped for a cycle; this keeps red-black on curve for Zurgo, Garna, and Caesar. It is a Swamp and a Mountain, so Dragonskull Summit and the other checks see it. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Pay the 2 life on curve turns (Zurgo, Garna, Bone-Cairn). Take the tapped ETB when you already have the colours or are flooding.
- Sequence other tapped duals first; keep this as the life-for-speed source.
- Dragonskull Summit and similar checks count it as both a Swamp and a Mountain.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]]
- **Wants the mana**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]]
- **Checks that see Swamp/Mountain**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Dragonskull Summit|Dragonskull Summit]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Isolated Chapel|Isolated Chapel]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Clifftop Retreat|Clifftop Retreat]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The 2 life is real next to Caesar draw, Skullclamp, and other shocks. Do not pay if the land would sit tapped anyway.
- Not a basic. Monument and fetches that say "basic" miss it.
