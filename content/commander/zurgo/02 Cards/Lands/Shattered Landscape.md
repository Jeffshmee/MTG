## Deck Role & Rating

Shattered Landscape is a main-deck land.  

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Fetch a basic Mountain, Plains, or Swamp when you need the colour. Cycling {R}{W}{B} is the backup if you already have lands.
- Hold it for a turn you are already making or sacrificing tokens so the draw stacks with Zurgo.
- Sequence tapped lands on early turns; keep untapped sources for a turn-3 Zurgo.
