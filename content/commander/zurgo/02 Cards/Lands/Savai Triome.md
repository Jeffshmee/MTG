## Deck Role & Rating

Savai Triome is the tapped Mardu triome: one land for {R}, {W}, or {B}, typed Mountain, Plains, and Swamp, with cycling {3} when you do not need the land. [[02 Cards/Lands/Terramorphic Expanse|Terramorphic Expanse]] found one basic; this is all three colours and a late-game card. Isolated Chapel, Clifftop Retreat, and Dragonskull Summit all see it. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sequence it on an early turn you can afford tapped. Do not keep a two-lander that needs this for turn-3 Zurgo.
- Cycle it in the midgame when you already have the colours and want a card. Hold it for a turn you are already making or sacrificing tokens so the draw stacks with Zurgo.
- Checks count it as Mountain, Plains, and Swamp at once.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Always enters tapped. A second tapped triple next to Nomad Outpost can miss Zurgo on three.
- Not a basic. Monument and fetches that say "basic" miss it.
- Cycling {3} is expensive; do not cycle it off two lands.
