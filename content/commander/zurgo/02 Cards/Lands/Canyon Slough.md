## Deck Role & Rating

Canyon Slough is a tapped BR cycling dual. Blood Crypt took this slot as the untapped shock. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold it for a turn you are already making or sacrificing tokens so the draw stacks with Zurgo.
- Sequence tapped lands on early turns; keep untapped sources for a turn-3 Zurgo.
- Comes in tapped. Do not flood the opener with too many of these if you need Zurgo on three.
