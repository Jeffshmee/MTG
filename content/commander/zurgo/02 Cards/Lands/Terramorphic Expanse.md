## Deck Role & Rating

Terramorphic Expanse is a slow fetch that finds a basic tapped. Savai Triome took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Crack it when you need a basic colour. It enters the found land tapped, so do not fetch if you needed that mana this turn.
