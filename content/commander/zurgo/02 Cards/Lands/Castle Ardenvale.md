## Deck Role & Rating

Castle Ardenvale is a main-deck land.  

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Tokens ETB into [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and leave into [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]].
- Sequence tapped lands on early turns; keep untapped sources for a turn-3 Zurgo.
- Comes in tapped. Do not flood the opener with too many of these if you need Zurgo on three.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]]
- **Attack doublers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]]
- **Token payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]]
- **Wants the mana**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Tapped ETB can miss a turn-3 Zurgo if the rest of the opener is also slow.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] upgrades the tokens; [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] then no longer kills 4/4s.
