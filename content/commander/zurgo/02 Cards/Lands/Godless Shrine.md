## Deck Role & Rating

Godless Shrine is the untapped WB shock: pay 2 life or it comes in tapped. [[02 Cards/Lands/Temple of Silence|Temple of Silence]] always cost a turn for a scry; this keeps a turn-3 Zurgo realistic when the other pip is missing. It is a Plains and a Swamp, so Isolated Chapel and the other checks see it. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Pay the 2 life on curve turns (Zurgo, Isshin, Adeline). Take the tapped ETB when you already have the colours or are flooding.
- Sequence other tapped duals first; keep this as the life-for-speed source.
- Isolated Chapel and similar checks count it as both a Plains and a Swamp.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]]
- **Wants the mana**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]]
- **Checks that see Plains/Swamp**: [[02 Cards/Lands/Isolated Chapel|Isolated Chapel]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Clifftop Retreat|Clifftop Retreat]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Dragonskull Summit|Dragonskull Summit]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The 2 life is real next to Caesar draw, Skullclamp, and other shocks. Do not pay if the land would sit tapped anyway.
- Not a basic. Monument and fetches that say "basic" miss it.
