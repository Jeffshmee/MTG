## Oracle Text

+1: Creatures you control gain [[03 Effects/Deathtouch|deathtouch]] until end of turn. Put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on up to one target [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]] you control.

−2: Until end of turn, if one or more tokens would be created under your control, twice that many of those tokens are created instead.

−6: [[03 Effects/Exile|Exile]] all cards from all graveyards, then create a 1/1 white Spirit [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]] with [[03 Effects/Flying|flying]] for each card exiled this way.
