## Oracle Text

Whenever one or more other creatures die, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]]. This ability triggers only once each turn.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Morbid Opportunist turns the first death each turn into a card, stacked on Zurgo's leave trigger rather than replacing it. The usual death is a Mobilize Warrior at end of turn, so one attack with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Nightblade Brigade|Nightblade Brigade]] is already a draw. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], and [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] let you force that death on an opponent's turn as well. It does not scale — a wipe, a pile of sacs, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]] (once she arrives) still pay exactly one card, because the trigger is "one or more" and only once a turn.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it before you attack with Zurgo. The end-step Mobilize death is this turn's free card.
- On an opponent's turn, sac a leftover Human, Goblin, or Snake to Seer, Yahenni, or Caesar so you still draw off-turn.
- [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] a 1/1 in combat: that death *is* the trigger. Do not expect a second card from the rest of the tokens.
- A wipe still draws one. Do not hold it as if it were [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]].

- **Mobilize deaths**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Nightblade Brigade|Nightblade Brigade]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]]
- **Forced deaths**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]]
- **Parallel payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]]
- **Recursion**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sun Titan|Sun Titan]] (mana value 3)

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Once per turn, period. Ten deaths are still one card.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] does not double this. The trigger is "one or more," not "each creature."
- Does not trigger off itself.
- Exile-based removal skips the death; Zurgo still sees the leave.
