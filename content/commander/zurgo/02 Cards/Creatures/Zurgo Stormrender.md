## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Mobilize|Mobilize]] 1 *(Whenever this creature attacks, create a tapped and attacking 1/1 red Warrior [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]]. Sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step.)*  
Whenever a [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]] you control leaves the battlefield, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]] if it was attacking. Otherwise, each [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|opponent loses 1 life]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Zurgo is the engine and the win: Mobilize makes an attacking Warrior, and whenever a creature token you control leaves, you draw if it was attacking or each opponent loses 1 life if it was not. Every other card in the list is judged by whether it makes those tokens, forces them to leave, or pays off the same loop. Opponents have stolen him — Mobilize and the leave trigger work for whoever controls him, so a theft is them going wide and drawing with your commander. The only protection in the 100 is [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]]; losing him is the worst swing this deck can suffer.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Ideal curve: Turn 3 Zurgo → attack immediately for the first Mobilize token. Keep attacking every turn; sitting him back “to stay safe” is how the deck stops functioning.
- Greaves him the moment it is in play. Do not Clamp him. If a steal is on the stack and Greaves is off, sac him to [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]] rather than let them have Mobilize.
- Extra combat ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]) re-triggers Mobilize on a bigger board. Isshin makes Mobilize 1 into two tokens.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] doubles **dies only**. Mobilize end-step sac is a death → draw two. Exile or bounce → draw once.
- Caesar, Seer, or Clamp an attacking token: the leave still draws, and the aristocrats still fire.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Theft is the nightmare line. Mobilize works for them. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]] is the only protection in the 100.
- Board wipes that hit Zurgo are the next-worst swing. [[02 Cards/Instants/Grand Crescendo|Grand Crescendo]] saves the team; Yahenni only saves Yahenni.
- Cards that prevent tokens from leaving (or that replace deaths with exile you do not control) cut the draw in half — Teysa also shuts off on those leaves.
- He is legendary — only one copy. Greaves shroud also stops your own Swords or Clamp on him.
