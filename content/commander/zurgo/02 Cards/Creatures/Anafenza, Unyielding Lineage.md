## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Flash|Flash]]

[[03 Effects/First Strike|First strike]]

Whenever another nontoken creature you control dies, Anafenza [[03 Effects/Endure|endures 2]]. (Put two [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]]s on it or create a 2/2 white Spirit [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]].)

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Anafenza is a flash first-strike answer that sometimes makes a body, not a token engine. Endure 2 only when another nontoken dies — Mobilize Warriors, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]] Humans, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] Soldiers, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]] Goblins do not count. The commander feed is incidental: take the 2/2 Spirit and that token can later leave for a [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] drain, or draw if it attacks first. This slot is a planned future cut for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]] (not in the 100 yet).

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Flash her in as a first-strike blocker. That is the card. Do not main-phase a 3-mana 2/2 hoping endure "goes off" on a token turn.
- Real endure setups: an opponent kills Zurgo / Adeline / Caesar; you [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] [[02 Cards/Creatures/Loyal Apprentice|Loyal Apprentice]] or another 1-toughness nontoken; you sac a nontoken to Caesar, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aron, Benalia's Ruin|Aron]].
- On endure, take the Spirit when [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] are in play. Take counters when you need Anafenza to win the next fight.
- Teysa (not in the 100 yet) is what this slot wants — she doubles the death half the deck already has. Anafenza does not.

## Key Synergies

- **Flash blocker**: first strike into a creature that would eat Zurgo or a value attacker
- **Nontoken deaths**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Loyal Apprentice|Loyal Apprentice]] (clamp), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aron, Benalia's Ruin|Aron]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]]
- **If you take the Spirit**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Ignores token deaths, which is the deck's main event. Most turns she is a 2/2 with flash.
- The Spirit is a 2/2, so Skullclamp does not kill it. Counters on Anafenza do not make tokens.
- Legendary. Planned cut for Teysa.
