## Deck Role & Rating

Jazal is the go-wide combat closer that is also a Warrior. The body is a 4/4 first strike for {2}{W}{W}; the activated ability is {3}{W}{W} for +X/+X on attacking creatures, where X is how many are attacking. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]]'s Mobilize token, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]] all count. Sequence the activation after the tokens are attacking, before damage. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] pumps on ETB the same turn; Jazal pumps on the attack. He took [[02 Cards/Instants/Deadly Dispute|Deadly Dispute]]'s slot. He does not make tokens — empty board is a 4/4 first strike, which is honest, but the five-mana activation is blank.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on four, attack, activate if you have four or more attackers (X=4 is +4/+4). Small X is a bad use of five mana.
- Extra combat ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]): the pump lasts until end of turn, so the second combat is still pumped and the second Mobilize wave raises X if you activate after that attack too.
- First strike on the 4/4 himself. He can trade up. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mindblade Render|Mindblade Render]] cares that he is a Warrior.
- Do not activate into a wipe. The pump is until end of turn.
