## Deck Role & Rating

Viscera Seer is the free sac outlet: sacrifice a creature, scry 1, no mana. Leftover Mobilize tokens, Adeline Humans, and Caesar Soldiers become Zurgo leaves, [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]] drains, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] pings, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]] cards or burns on demand. Leave a spare body for Caesar to sac on attack; Seer is the instant-speed version for end of combat and for converting the board before a wipe. One mana, turn-1 play, then Zurgo on 3.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sac an attacking token at end of combat, while it is still attacking: Zurgo draws, Garna draws, you scry 1. Do this when you need the card now rather than waiting for the end-step Mobilize trigger.
- Opponent’s wipe on the stack: crack leftover tokens first. The leaves still pay Zurgo / Bastion / Elas / Garna; the scry finds a rebuild.
- Leave a spare token for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] to sac on attack. Use Seer on the ones Caesar does not need.
- Do not sac Zurgo. Do not empty the board before [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Hour of Reckoning|Hour of Reckoning]] — Hour is one-sided *because* of the tokens.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Scry 1 is not a draw. If the top is a land you needed, you still have to take it or bury it.
- Emptying the board into your own [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Hour of Reckoning|Hour of Reckoning]] or [[02 Cards/Instants/Grand Crescendo|Grand Crescendo]] is a miss — those cards want the tokens.
- It is a 1/1. It dies to any ping; you only needed the outlet to stay on the field.
