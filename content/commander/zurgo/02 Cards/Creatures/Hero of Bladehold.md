## Deck Role & Rating

Hero of Bladehold is the attack engine that must swing: two tapped-and-attacking Soldiers plus battle cry. Those Soldiers persist, unlike Mobilize Warriors, so they are [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] fodder, and they are attacking tokens, so they draw off Zurgo when they leave. Battle cry pumps Zurgo, Adeline, Caesar, and every other attacker except Hero herself — including the new Soldiers, depending on trigger order. Isshin makes this four Soldiers and two battle cries; Aurelia is a second pair on the extra combat.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- She must attack. Unlike Adeline, sitting back does nothing. Send her with Zurgo so Mobilize plus two Soldiers is three token ETBs for Tremors / Elas / Belladonna.
- Completes [[02 Cards/Creatures/Belladonna Took|Belladonna]]’s ladder by herself (two tokens; Zurgo’s Mobilize is the third and the pump lands).
- Isshin / Windcrag Siege: two battle cries and four Soldiers from one attack. Extra combat with Aurelia is a second pair and another cry.
- Clamp or Caesar-sac a Soldier after it has attacked: Zurgo draws, Garna draws, and Caesar still gets two modes if you sac on the attack trigger.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Battle cry does not pump Hero herself.
