## Oracle Text

Whenever a creature an opponent controls dies, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on Yahenni.

## Deck Role & Rating

Yahenni is the haste sac outlet that can live through a destroy-based wipe. Sacrifice another creature — a leftover Mobilize token, an Adeline Human, a Caesar Soldier — and it is indestructible until end of turn; that death still pays Zurgo, [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]]. It does **not** give Zurgo indestructible; [[02 Cards/Instants/Grand Crescendo|Grand Crescendo]] is the team-wide wipe save. Whenever an opponent’s creature dies it grows, so Bone-Cairn deathtouch trades and Hour of Reckoning both turn it into a real attacker.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Drop it and attack immediately (haste). Sac a spare token in response to a kill spell or a wipe to keep Yahenni; Zurgo still sees that token leave.
- Opponent’s [[03 Effects/Destroy|destroy]] wipe on the stack: sac leftover tokens into Yahenni. The aristocrats fire, Yahenni lives, Zurgo still dies unless you also have Grand Crescendo.
- Steal on Zurgo with Greaves off him: sac Zurgo to Yahenni rather than let them Mobilize with your commander. He goes to the command zone; that is the grim line, not the default.
- It grows off *their* deaths, not yours. Send deathtouch tokens ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]]) into their blockers, or resolve Hour of Reckoning, and it becomes the post-wipe beater.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Indestructible does not stop exile, bounce, or -X/-X. A steal still takes it if you cannot sac in response.
- It does not protect Zurgo. Greaves is still the only in-list shroud; Grand Crescendo is the only in-list team indestructible.
- It is legendary — only one copy.
