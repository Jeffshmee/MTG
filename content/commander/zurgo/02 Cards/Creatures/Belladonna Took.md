## Oracle Text

Whenever a token you control enters, you [[03 Effects/Gain Life|gain 1 life]] if this is the first time this ability has resolved this turn. If it's the second time, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]]. If it's the third time, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on each creature you control.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Belladonna is a two-mana token-ETB ladder: first token that turn is 1 life, second is a card, third puts a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. A lone Zurgo Mobilize only hits the life step; any second token the same turn — Adeline, Hero, Caesar’s Soldiers, Isshin doubling Mobilize — draws, and a third pumps the team. The counters are permanent, so they stay on Zurgo after the Mobilize tokens die. She is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- She counts **tokens**, not creatures. Non-token ETBs do not advance the ladder.
- Isshin on Zurgo: two Mobilize tokens in one combat = life + card with no other help. Caesar’s two Soldiers, or Hero’s two Soldiers, complete the ladder by themselves; Zurgo’s Mobilize is the third and the pump lands.
- Dalkovan Packbeasts (Mobilize 3) is life + card + pump off one Ox attack.
- The +1/+1 counters stay after the Warriors leave. Tremors and Elas still fire on each of those same ETBs. Fourth and later tokens that turn do nothing extra for her.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- One token a turn is only 1 life. She is a dead card if you cannot go wide the same turn.
- Fourth and later tokens that turn do nothing extra for her. Do not sequence extra waves “for Belladonna.”
- She is legendary — only one copy.
