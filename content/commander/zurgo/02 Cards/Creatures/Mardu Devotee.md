## Deck Role & Rating

Mardu Devotee is a one-mana Human Scout that scries 2 on ETB and filters {1} into {R}, {W}, or {B} once each turn. That is early selection plus a pip for Zurgo, not a token or an attack trigger. Viscera Seer already scries for a creature; the filter is real on a two-lander that is short a colour. Owned; stay on the sideboard. Not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- If it entered the 100: turn 1 Devotee, scry toward a land or Zurgo, then filter on turn 2–3 for the missing pip.
- The filter is once each turn and costs {1}. It does not ramp; it fixes.
- Sac or Clamp it after the scry if you need the death more than the filter.
