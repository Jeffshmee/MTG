## Oracle Text

At the beginning of each end step, create X 1/1 white Spirit [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]] with [[03 Effects/Flying|flying]], where X is the number of tokens you created this turn.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Thalisse is the end-step copier: she makes X flying Spirits equal to the number of tokens you created this turn, not how many are still on the battlefield. Attack first — Zurgo Mobilize, Adeline Humans, Hero Soldiers, Caesar’s pair — so the wave exists, then she reprints it as 1/1 flyers that persist. Those Spirits ping [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and gain life off [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] on the way in; next turn they can attack and feed Zurgo when they leave. Isshin, Aurelia, and Kaya all raise X because they make you create more tokens in the same turn.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack, then pass to end step. Mobilize’s sacrifice and Thalisse both trigger at the beginning of the end step; order them either way — X is a historical count of tokens created this turn, even after the Warriors are gone.
- Zurgo plus Adeline in a four-player game is already four tokens created (one Mobilize, three Humans). Thalisse then makes four Spirits. Hero or Caesar on the same attack is a second full wave in the air.
- Extra combat ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]) adds another Mobilize / Adeline / Hero / Caesar wave before she copies the total.
- Her own Spirits do not count toward that same trigger. They sit around for the next turn’s Clamp, Caesar sac, or attack-and-leave.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- She does nothing on a turn you created zero tokens. Cast her, then attack — do not drop her as a 3/4 and pass.
- Five mana. Do not tap out into her if Zurgo is already dead and the board is empty.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] turns the Spirits into 4/4 Angels; [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] then no longer kills them.
- She is legendary — only one copy.
