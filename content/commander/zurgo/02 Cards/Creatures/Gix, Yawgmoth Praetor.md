## Oracle Text

Whenever a creature deals combat damage to one of your opponents, its controller may [[03 Effects/Lose Life|pay 1 life]]. If they do, they [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]].

{4}{B}{B}{B}, [[03 Effects/Discard|Discard X cards]]: [[03 Effects/Exile|Exile]] the top X cards of target opponent's library. You may play lands and cast spells from among cards exiled this way without paying their mana costs.

## Deck Role & Rating

Gix is a combat-damage draw engine, not a token maker. Each creature that connects may pay 1 life to draw — your 1/1s included — so a wide Zurgo attack can be several cards if you spend the life. That is a different axis from [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]]: Gix pays for connecting even if the token lives; Zurgo pays when the token leaves. The 7-mana exile ability is a brick, and he is global — opponents who connect draw too.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast him the turn you will actually connect with several bodies. A 3-mana 3/3 that does nothing on a stalled board is a bad play.
- Wide attacks: Zurgo + Mobilize + [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]] Goblin + [[02 Cards/Creatures/Loyal Apprentice|Loyal Apprentice]] Thopter can be multiple cards. Budget the life; [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] is how you get it back.
- Token connects (Gix draw), then dies attacking at end step or in combat (Zurgo draw, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]] draw). Do not treat him as a second Zurgo.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] is a second damage step and another round of payments.
- Ignore {4}{B}{B}{B}. You will not have seven mana and a discard hand in the games this deck wants to win.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Opponents draw too. Do not play him into a wide table that will swing back.
- Life payment scales with token count. You can deck yourself or bleed out on your own turn.
- Not a token engine. Does nothing if you never connect. Legendary. The 7-mana ability is not why he is here.
