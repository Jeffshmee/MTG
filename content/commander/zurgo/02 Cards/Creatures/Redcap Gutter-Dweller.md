At the beginning of your upkeep, you may [[03 Effects/Sacrifice|sacrifice]] another creature. If you do, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on this creature and [[03 Effects/Exile|exile]] the top card of your library. You may play that card this turn.

## Deck Role & Rating

Redcap dumps two Rats on ETB and then asks for a sac each upkeep to impulse the top card and grow. The Rats hit [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] on the way in and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] on the way out — draw if they attacked, drain if they did not — but they cannot block, and the upkeep sac is a tax better engines already want that body for. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]] took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- ETB the Rats into Tremors or Elas. Attack with them if Zurgo is attacking so they draw on the way out.
- Upkeep sac a leftover Rat, not a Mobilize engine. Mobilize tokens are already gone by your next upkeep.
- Impulse is this turn only. Play the land or the cheap payoff immediately.
- Do not protect Redcap. Bone-Cairn is the card this slot actually wants.
