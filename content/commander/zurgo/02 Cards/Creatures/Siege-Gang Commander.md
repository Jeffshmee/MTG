## Deck Role & Rating

Siege-Gang is a five-mana burst of three Goblins plus a ping that sacs a Goblin for 2 damage. The Goblins enter into [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], then leave into [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] — draw if they attacked, drain if you ping them idle. The ping is real reach after a wide turn. It sacs Goblins, not Warriors, so Mobilize tokens are the wrong fodder; [[02 Cards/Creatures/Nightblade Brigade|Nightblade]], Neriv's Goblins, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Redcap Gutter-Dweller|Redcap]], and Siege-Gang itself are legal food.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast into Tremors or Elas. Attack with the Goblins plus Zurgo, then ping leftovers after combat if you need the last points.
- Sac a Goblin that did not attack to Zurgo-drain plus 2 damage; sac one that attacked for Zurgo-draw plus 2 damage.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ogre Battledriver|Ogre Battledriver]] before Siege-Gang so the Goblins haste in as 3/1s.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Redoubled Stormsinger|Redoubled Stormsinger]] copies the three Goblins if they entered this turn.
