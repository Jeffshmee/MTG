## Deck Role & Rating

Sandskitter is a four-mana 2/1 with menace that endures 2 — counters on itself or a 2/2 Spirit — and it is not Mobilize. Take the Spirit whenever [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] is around so the token can draw if it attacks or drain if it dies idle; the 4/3 body is the fair mode with no payoffs. Packbeasts is already in (Emeria was the cut). This is leftover four-mana filler, Combined 76.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Almost always make the Spirit. Attack with it if Zurgo is attacking so the leave draws.
- Take the +1/+1 counters only when you need a 4/3 menace and have no token payoffs in play.
- Fine to feed the Spirit (or the 2/1) to [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] or [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] — the card's job is temporary.
- Do not hold it as if it were a Mobilize creature.

## Key Synergies

- **Token payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]]
- **Sac / clamp**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not Mobilize. The 2/1 is a bad card if you skip the Spirit.
- Four mana for endure 2 is behind every real engine in the deck.
- Combined 76. Same band as Kimoyo Beads — that is a tie, not an auto-cut.
