## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Vigilance|Vigilance]]  
Adeline's power is equal to the number of creatures you control.  
Whenever you attack, for each opponent, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a 1/1 white Human creature token]] that's tapped and attacking that player or a planeswalker they control.

## Deck Role & Rating

Adeline is the go-wide engine that does not have to attack. Any attack — Zurgo swinging is enough — makes a 1/1 Human per opponent, tapped and attacking that player, and her power equals the number of creatures you control, so the tokens she prints also turn her into the biggest body on the field. Those Humans persist, unlike Mobilize Warriors, so they are [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] fodder; they are attacking tokens, so they draw off Zurgo when they leave. Vigilance lets her sit back as a 4-toughness blocker while still making the wave.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Swing with Zurgo, leave Adeline back. She still makes the Humans. In a four-player game that is three attacking tokens that do not get sacrificed at end of turn.
- Clamp or Caesar-sac a Human after combat: Zurgo draws (it left attacking), Garna draws, Clamp draws two.
- Extra combat ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]) is a second wave of Humans and another Zurgo cycle on a bigger power.
- Isshin / Windcrag Siege: two Humans per opponent from one attack. Divine Visitation turns each into a 4/4 Angel attacking a different player.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Token count scales with **opponents remaining**. Heads-up she only makes one Human.
- The Humans attack specific players. You cannot pile them all onto one opponent.
- She is legendary — only one copy.
