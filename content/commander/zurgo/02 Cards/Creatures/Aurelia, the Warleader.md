## Deck Role & Rating

Aurelia is the extra-combat closer. The first time she attacks each turn she untaps the team and adds a combat, so Zurgo Mobilize, Adeline, Hero, Caesar, and every other attack engine fire again on a board that just grew. Haste means she does this the turn she resolves — cast her, swing, take the second combat. Six mana is the tax: she is the card you cast when Zurgo and at least one other engine are already in play, not into an empty board. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] is the other extra-combat in the 100; Isshin makes her first-attack trigger fire twice.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast her and attack immediately. The first combat is the one that unlocks the second. Zurgo plus Adeline plus Aurelia is already a kill-sized wave.
- She only extra-combats **the first time she attacks each turn**. A third combat needs All-Out Assault or Isshin doubling this trigger.
- Isshin / Windcrag Siege: her first-attack trigger fires twice — untap twice and two extra combats (three combats total). Later combats that turn do not retrigger her.
- Caesar on the same board sacs and modes twice. Divine Visitation plus the second combat is often lethal in the air.

- **Upgrade**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Six mana. Do not tap out into her if Zurgo is already dead and you have no board.
- She must attack to extra-combat. Fog, tap, or Pacifism shuts her off.
- She is legendary — only one copy.
