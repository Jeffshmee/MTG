## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/First Strike|First strike]]

Whenever this creature attacks, for each [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]] you control that entered this turn, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a tapped and attacking token]] that's a copy of that token. At the beginning of the next end step, [[03 Effects/Sacrifice|sacrifice those tokens]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Redoubled Stormsinger copies each token that entered this turn when it attacks, makes those copies tapped and attacking, then sacs them at end of turn. Attack after [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] tokens have already entered — stack the triggers so Mobilize and Adeline resolve first — and every copy is another attacking leave for Zurgo, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]]. First strike on a 3/3 is incidental. If nothing entered this turn, the attack is just a 3/3.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Declare Zurgo plus Adeline or Hero plus Stormsinger. Put Stormsinger's trigger on the stack first so it resolves last and sees the new tokens.
- First-main [[02 Cards/Creatures/Siege-Gang Commander|Siege-Gang]], [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Tempt with Vengeance|Tempt with Vengeance]], or an [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ophiomancer|Ophiomancer]] Snake, then attack with Stormsinger to copy that wave.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] copies are 4/4 Angels that still sac at end of turn and draw Zurgo.
- Do not attack it on a turn no tokens entered "just to hit."

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Must attack, and only copies tokens that entered *this turn* — last turn's Adeline Humans do not count.
- Copies sac at end of turn even if the original was a lasting token (Adeline Human, Siege-Gang Goblin).
- Three mana of do-nothing if you stumble into combat with an empty wave.
