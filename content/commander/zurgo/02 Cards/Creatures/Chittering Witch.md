## Deck Role & Rating

Chittering Witch is an expensive token burst plus a situational -2/-2 sac outlet, not a core engine. Four mana makes Rats equal to opponents — three in a full table, one heads-up — and playtest has her sitting in hand unless the outlet is actually needed. The named commander line is real but narrow: sac a Mobilize token to shrink a blocker; the token left attacking, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] draws (and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]] draws). [[02 Cards/Creatures/Belladonna Took|Belladonna Took]] took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Default: do not cast her on curve. Hold for a turn where -2/-2 on a blocker or a 2-toughness threat matters, or where four mana has nothing better to do.
- Combat line: attack with Zurgo, then pay {1}{B} and sac the Mobilize token to shrink a blocker. Repeat if you have the mana and more fodder.
- ETB Rats ping [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]]. They do not attack the turn they enter. Zurgo drains if they die that turn; [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] after combat is two cards plus drain.
- Cast her before combat if [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] is out: Rats ETB, the team gets +N/+N, then Zurgo attacks.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] makes the Rats 4/4 Angels. Keep sacing Mobilize 1/1s, not the Angels.

## Key Synergies

- **Combat sac / removal**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]] (Mobilize fodder), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]]
- **Faster outlets (different jobs)**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aron, Benalia's Ruin|Aron]]
- **Rat / leave payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]]
- **Clamp the Rats**: [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Four mana, 2/2, no haste. Heads-up she makes one Rat. Playtest has her sitting in hand; that cut is now committed. Owned cut; not in the 100.
- The outlet costs {1}{B} each time. Mana-hungry next to Seer and Yahenni, and -2/-2 misses 3+ toughness without multiple activations.
- Divine Visitation 4/4s are bad sac fodder and bad clamp targets.
