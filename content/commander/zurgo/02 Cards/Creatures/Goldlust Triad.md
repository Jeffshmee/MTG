## Deck Role & Rating

Goldlust Triad is a five-mana Myriad Dragon; honest filler. The copies are attacking tokens that exile at end of combat — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] draws because they leave while attacking, but [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] miss the leave because exile is not dying. Treasure on combat damage is extra mana, not a plan. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]] took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast her only when you can attack immediately. A 5-mana 4/3 that sits is a missed [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Invention|Angel of Invention]] slot.
- Myriad copies ETB attacking the other opponents: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] pings, Elas gains life, Goldnight pumps, Zurgo draws when they exile at end of combat.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] is a second Myriad wave and more Treasures. First-combat copies have already been exiled.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] replaces the copies with 4/4 Angels; they still exile at end of combat, and Zurgo still draws.
- Do not hold her as a Dragon win condition. Anim Pakal is the card this mana wants.

## Key Synergies

- **Attacking-leave draws**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]]
- **ETB on the copies**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]]
- **Extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]
- **Upgrade**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Myriad exile is not "dies." Garna, Bastion, Elas's death drain, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] all miss the copies leaving.
- Five mana. Heads-up, Myriad makes zero copies. Treasure does not feed the token loop.
- Committed cut for Anim Pakal. Owned cut; not in the 100.
