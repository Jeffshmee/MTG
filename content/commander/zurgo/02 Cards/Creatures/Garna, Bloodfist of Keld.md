## Oracle Text

Whenever another creature you control dies, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]] if it was attacking. Otherwise, Garna [[03 Effects/Deal Damage|deals 1 damage]] to each opponent.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Garna is a second copy of Zurgo’s payoff, except she cares about **dies** rather than any leave, and the non-attack half is 1 damage to each opponent instead of life loss. Mobilize tokens that die attacking are a card from Zurgo and a card from Garna; tokens you sac after combat (or that never attacked) ping the table instead. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] doubles both modes — two cards on an attacking death, 2 damage to each opponent on a non-attacking one. Keep her on the field whenever Zurgo is attacking; the overlapping draw is the reason she sits at the top of the creature list.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack with Zurgo while she is in play. Each Mobilize death is Zurgo draw + Garna draw + Bastion / Elas drain.
- Sac an attacking token with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]] at end of combat: still “was attacking,” so both draw, and Caesar still gets two modes.
- Skullclamp on an attacking 1/1: dies attacking → Zurgo draw + Garna draw + Clamp two cards.
- After combat, leftover Humans and Spirits that never attacked (or that already stopped) ping the table when they die. That is the burn half — useful when the hand is full.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Exile-based removal and bounce skip her (tokens still trigger Zurgo).
- She does not trigger off herself.
- She is legendary — only one copy.
