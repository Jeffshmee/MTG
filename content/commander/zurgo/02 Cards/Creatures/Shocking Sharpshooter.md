## Oracle Text

Whenever another creature you control [[03 Effects/ETB|enters]], this creature [[03 Effects/Deal Damage|deals 1 damage]] to target opponent.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Shocking Sharpshooter is a two-mana ETB ping on a 1/3 with reach: every other creature you control that enters deals 1 to target opponent. Zurgo’s Warrior, Adeline’s Humans, Hero’s Soldiers, Packbeasts’ three, Anim Pakal’s Gnomes, and Caesar’s haste Soldiers all shoot. Unlike [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] it hits one player, so you finish someone instead of spreading. Reach holds the air. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Curve: turn 2 Sharpshooter → turn 3 Zurgo → attack. The Mobilize token is 1 damage on the way in, then a Zurgo draw when it leaves.
- Point each ping at the player you need dead. Do not auto-split when one opponent is in burn range.
- Isshin / Windcrag / Packbeasts / Anim Pakal is the flood that makes this a finisher. Tremors still hits everyone; this is the targeted extra.
- The 1/3 reach is a flyer-blocker the turn it lands. Do not wait for a board to cast it.

## Key Synergies

- **Token floods**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]]
- **Same-cost burn**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]]
- **Attack doublers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]]
- **Extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Hits **one** opponent, not each opponent. Tremors is still the table-wide half.
- Does not trigger off itself. A lonely 1/3 does nothing until another creature enters.
- Removal on this body shuts the pings off; the tokens still feed Zurgo without it.
