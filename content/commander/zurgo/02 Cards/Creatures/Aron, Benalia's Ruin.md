## Deck Role & Rating

Aron is a slow anthem sac outlet: {W}{B}, tap, sacrifice another creature, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]] sac for free; Aron charges mana and a tap to grow the team. The leave still pays [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] — draw if the token was attacking, drain if it was not — plus [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]]. The counters are a permanent pump the go-wide close wants; that is why he is the slow outlet instead of a third Seer.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Do not attack with Aron if you plan to activate. The combat line is: leave him back, after blockers sac an attacking Mobilize token; the team gets +1/+1 before damage and Zurgo draws.
- Second main, sac a leftover Goblin / Human / Rat / Thopter — not a Mobilize token that will die at end step anyway. Those counters are wasted.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] also wants a sac on attack. With two tokens, Caesar takes one on attack and Aron takes one after combat. With one token, Caesar usually gets it.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]]'s until-end-of-turn pumps plus Aron's counters is a real kill turn. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] after the counters land will not kill those bodies.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- {W}{B} plus tap is slow. Dead if you already attacked with him or are tapped out.
- Counters on Mobilize tokens that will be sacrificed at end step do nothing. Counters also turn 1/1s into 2/2s, so Skullclamp misses.
- Legendary. Double white in the casting cost on top of the activate.
