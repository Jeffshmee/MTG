## Deck Role & Rating

Emeria Angel is a landfall Bird maker, not an attack-token engine. Each land drops a 1/1 flyer with no haste, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]]'s leave is drain unless the Bird attacks later and then dies — worse commander feed than Mobilize, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]]. Double white at four mana is the other cost. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]] took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast her only if you will play another land this turn or next, or if you just need a 3/3 flyer. A 4-mana 3/3 that makes nothing on a skipped land drop is a brick.
- [[02 Cards/Lands/Terramorphic Expanse|Terramorphic Expanse]] / [[02 Cards/Lands/Shattered Landscape|Shattered Landscape]] are the extra landfall — sacrifice them after she is in play.
- Attack with the Birds before you [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] or sac them so Zurgo draws instead of drains.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] turns each land drop into a 4/4 Angel. That is the only time she looks like a win condition.
- Extra combat does nothing for her. She does not care whether Zurgo attacks.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Not an attack trigger. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], Adeline, and Hero do not sequence with her.
- Double white. Birds have no haste, so the turn they enter they are only Tremors / Elas / chump.
- Divine Visitation 4/4s cannot be clamped.
