## Oracle Text

Whenever another creature you control enters, that creature [[03 Effects/Pump Creatures|gets +2/+0]] and gains [[03 Effects/Haste|haste]] until end of turn. (It can attack and {T} this turn.)

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Ogre Battledriver gives every creature that enters after it +2/+0 and haste. Mobilize Warriors, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]] Humans, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero]] Soldiers, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] Soldiers all enter attacking — the +2 is extra damage on tokens that were dying anyway, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] still draws when they leave. The playtest kill is [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Tempt with Vengeance|Tempt with Vengeance]] (or any large wave) after Battledriver is already in play; [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] on that wave is 6/4 haste Angels. Cast it before the tokens, not after.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sequence: Battledriver, then Tempt / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Siege-Gang Commander|Siege-Gang]] / a Hero or Adeline attack. Never the reverse.
- With Divine Visitation, the same line is a one-shot. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] on the same board stacks a team anthem on top of the +2 haste.
- Zurgo's Mobilize token is a 3/1 attacker that still draws when it leaves.
- Do not drop it as a 3/3 on an empty board and pass.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does not pump itself.
