## Oracle Text

Whenever you attack with this creature and/or your commander, for each opponent, create a 1/1 red Goblin [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]] that's tapped and attacking that player.

[[03 Effects/Sacrifice|Sacrifice]] this creature: [[03 Effects/Create Token|Creature tokens]] you control gain [[03 Effects/Indestructible|indestructible]] until end of turn.

## Deck Role & Rating

Ainok is a two-mana attack-token engine: whenever you attack with it and/or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]], you make a tapped-and-attacking Goblin for each opponent. Those Goblins persist after combat — unlike Mobilize — so they are leftover attackers, [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] fodder, and extra Zurgo leaves on a later turn. The sac ability is token-only indestructible, a wipe hedge or a combat trick, not protection for Zurgo himself. Attacking Goblins that die draw off Zurgo; Goblins you clamp or sac after combat drain instead.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Leave Ainok back and attack with Zurgo. The trigger still fires; you keep a 2/2 and can sac it later for token indestructible.
- Attacking with both is still one trigger. Do not send Ainok just to "double" the Goblins.
- Goblins ETB into [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] and, if they die in combat, draw off Zurgo and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]]. After combat they are clamp / Caesar / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aron, Benalia's Ruin|Aron]] food and Zurgo drains.
- Sac Ainok after blockers so the Goblins and Mobilize tokens live, or in response to a wrath. It does not save Zurgo, Adeline, or Caesar.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] is a second Ainok trigger and another wave of attacking Goblins.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]] (Ainok need not attack if Zurgo does)
- **Extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]
- **Token payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]]
- **Sac / clamp the Goblins**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aron, Benalia's Ruin|Aron]]
- **Upgrade**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- One trigger whether Ainok, Zurgo, or both attack. Heads-up it makes a single Goblin.
- Sac ability does not make nontokens indestructible. Zurgo still dies to the wrath.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] turns the Goblins into 4/4 Angels; [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] then misses.
