## Oracle Text

Whenever another creature you control enters, creatures you control [[03 Effects/Pump Creatures|get +1/+1]] until end of turn.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Goldnight Commander is the go-wide combat closer: each other creature that enters gives the team +1/+1 until end of turn — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]]'s Mobilize token, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]] Humans, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]] Soldiers, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]] Goblins, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] Soldiers, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Loyal Apprentice|Loyal Apprentice]] Thopters. Playtest: real one-shot potential when those tokens stack, so sequence him before the wave, not onto an empty board as a 4-mana 2/2. He does not make tokens; he turns the ETBs the deck already produces into lethal damage.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast Goldnight, then attack. Beginning-of-combat tokens (Warboss, Apprentice) pump first; attack tokens (Zurgo, Adeline, Hero, Caesar, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ainok Strike Leader|Ainok]]) pump again before damage.
- Empty board: do not spend four mana unless you can follow up this turn (Zurgo already in play, Hero in hand). A lonely Goldnight is a 2/2.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]: pumps last until end of turn, so the first combat's ETBs still apply in the second combat, and the second wave adds more. That is the one-shot.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] still fire on the same ETBs. Goldnight does not replace those payoffs.
- He does not trigger off himself. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] in second main of the go-off turn will not kill the pumped 1/1s; clamp on a later turn after the pump wears off.

## Key Synergies

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Pumps last until end of turn. After the turn you have no board presence from this card.
- Four mana for a 2/2 with no tokens in play is a blank. Does not trigger off himself.
- The go-off turn turns 1/1s into 2/2s or larger, so Skullclamp misses until the pump wears off.
