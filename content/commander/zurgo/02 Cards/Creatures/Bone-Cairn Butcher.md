## Deck Role & Rating

Bone-Cairn Butcher is a Mobilize 2 beater that also gives every attacking token deathtouch — Zurgo’s Warriors, Adeline Humans, Hero Soldiers, Caesar Soldiers, Anim Pakal Gnomes, the lot. Attack with Butcher and Zurgo together and you get three deathtouch tokens that trade up, then leave and feed Zurgo, Bastion, Garna, and Elas. The deathtouch is static and only while they are attacking, so send them into blockers they would never survive; they were dying at end of turn anyway. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack with Butcher and Zurgo together. Zurgo’s token plus two more, all with deathtouch, then all three leave. Yahenni grows off the blockers they kill.
- Isshin / Windcrag Siege turns Mobilize 2 into Mobilize 4. Aurelia is a second Mobilize 2 on the extra combat.
- The deathtouch aura is static. Tokens created by Adeline, Hero, Caesar, or Anim Pakal that attack the same combat also get it — including Caesar’s haste Soldiers.
- Fine to chump their fatties. The tokens were Mobilize fodder; the trade is free value into Garna / Bastion / Elas.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Deathtouch only applies while the tokens are **attacking**. It does not help on blocks.
- Four mana for a non-legendary body — do not keep it in hand over cheaper engines (Elas, Adeline, Zurgo).
- Not legendary. The deck only wants one.
