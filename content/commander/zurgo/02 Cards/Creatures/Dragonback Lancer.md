## Deck Role & Rating

Dragonback Lancer is a four-mana flying Mobilize 1 — the same attack-token job as Zurgo, on a worse body.  
The flying 3/3 is a real attacker, but one Warrior per combat is the smallest Mobilize number in the vault, and Caesar occupies that four-mana slot with sac, draw, extra Soldiers, and burn. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- If it were still in the list: attack every turn it is safe. The Mobilize token feeds [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]] when it leaves.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]] (Mobilize 3) and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]] (Mobilize 2) cover the width this card used to provide. Nightblade Brigade is also an owned cut.
