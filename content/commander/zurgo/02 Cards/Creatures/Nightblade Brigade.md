## Deck Role & Rating

Nightblade Brigade is the three-mana Mobilize 1: deathtouch on a 1/3, plus surveil 1. Every attack makes an attacking Warrior that [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] draws off when it leaves, and the body trades up as a blocker. Surveil bins cheap engines for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sun Titan|Sun Titan]] — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]] — or keeps a land. It is not a finisher; Isshin took this slot. Owned cut; not in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Curve into Zurgo or right after him. Attack whenever the 1/3 will not eat a wipe for free.
- Surveil: bin Elas / Tremors / Seer / Bastion / Skullclamp if Titan is in hand or coming; otherwise keep gas.
- Attack with Zurgo plus Nightblade for two Warriors. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] can sac one for modes and still leave the other to draw.
- Deathtouch blocks. Do not send it into a 6/6 unless you need the Mobilize more than the blocker.
