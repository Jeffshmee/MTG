## Deck Role & Rating

Neriv is a 4/4 flying deathtouch that drops two Goblins on ETB and then impulse-draws off token *name* diversity when it attacks. The Goblins hit [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] on the way in and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] on the way out — draw if they attacked, drain if they did not. The exile pile is only playable on a turn you attacked with a commander, so Zurgo has to swing the same combat; Neriv is not a substitute commander. A mix of names (Warrior, Goblin, Human, Soldier, Snake, Myr) is the engine — a board of identical Mobilize Warriors is a small pile.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack with Zurgo and Neriv together. Zurgo satisfies "attacked with a commander"; Neriv exiles; play the cheap hits after combat.
- Cast it into Tremors or Elas for two Goblin ETBs, then swing.
- Diversify names before the trigger: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ophiomancer|Ophiomancer]] Snake, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]] Humans, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] Soldiers, Zurgo Warriors.
- Flying deathtouch is a real attacker. Point it at the player who cannot chump a flyer.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Legendary — only one copy.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] collapses every new token into "Angel." Neriv's exile count tanks.
- Five mana, and the pile sits if Zurgo did not attack that turn.
- A board of only Warrior tokens is a weak trigger.
