## Oracle Text

If a creature attacking causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability [[03 Effects/Double Trigger|triggers an additional time]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Isshin is the attack-trigger multiplier: Zurgo Mobilize 1 becomes two Warriors, Adeline makes two Humans per opponent, Hero makes four Soldiers, and Caesar sacs and modes twice. He does not need to attack himself — play him, then send the rest of the team. He does **not** double death, ETB, or generic leave triggers; [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] is that half, and together they turn one combat into two token waves and two death payoffs. He is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Curve: Turn 3 Zurgo → Turn 4 Isshin → attack. Mobilize 1 becomes two tokens and two Zurgo leaves at end of turn, plus two Tremors pings and two Elas life.
- He does not need to attack. Keep him back; the doubling is a static replacement on your permanents.
- Stacks with [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]] on Mardu — two replacement effects mean three total resolutions of each attack trigger. Packbeasts becomes nine Warriors.
- Extra combat ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]]) with Isshin on board is two combats of already-doubled triggers. Aurelia’s own first-attack trigger also fires twice (three combats).

## Key Synergies

- **Mobilize engines**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Dalkovan Packbeasts|Dalkovan Packbeasts]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bone-Cairn Butcher|Bone-Cairn Butcher]]
- **Attack token makers**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon|Anim Pakal]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Mardu Ascendancy|Mardu Ascendancy]]
- **Attack modal / extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]]
- **Same effect as an enchantment**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]]
- **Death half (not doubled by Isshin)**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does **not** double death, ETB, or generic leave-the-battlefield triggers. Tremors, Bastion, Elas drain, and Zurgo’s leave are not attack-caused on those permanents.
- Does nothing if you are not attacking.
- He is legendary — only one copy.
