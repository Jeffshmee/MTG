## Deck Role & Rating

Bone Devourer is an owned cut — it is not in the 100. Flash in a flyer that enters with +1/+1 counters equal to creatures that died this turn, then draw X and lose X life when *it* dies. The job was “cash a death-heavy combat for a body and a later draw,” but that is two setup conditions (deaths this turn, then its own death) plus life loss, and it does nothing on a quiet turn. It was cut for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], who taxes every token enter and every token death for two mana with no setup. Honest: too situational for this list.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Flash it after a Mobilize end-step wave or a Clamp/Caesar turn so the ETB counters are real. A 2/2 with zero deaths is a miss.
- Cash it the same turn with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] if you actually want the cards and can pay the life.
- Do not hold it as a 4-mana flyer. If Elas, Bastion, or Garna is the other card in hand, cast those.
- Teysa does not help the ETB. She would double nothing until Bone Devourer itself dies.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Owned cut, not in the 100. Status stays Ordered.
- Zero deaths this turn → a 2/2 flyer for four mana. Do not flash it “just in case.”
- The draw costs life equal to the counters. A huge Devourer you then sac can kill you.
- It does not trigger off tokens entering — only off deaths before it enters, and off its own death.
