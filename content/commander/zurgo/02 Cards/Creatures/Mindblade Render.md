## Oracle Text

Whenever your opponents are dealt combat damage, if any of that damage was dealt by a Warrior, you [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]] and [[03 Effects/Lose Life|you lose 1 life]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Mindblade Render is cheap Warrior combat-damage draw. One card and 1 life whenever opponents take combat damage if any of that damage was from a Warrior — not per Warrior, not per opponent. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] is a Warrior, Mobilize tokens are Warriors, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ainok Strike Leader|Ainok]] is a Warrior — Goblins, Humans, Thopters, and Servos do not turn it on by themselves. That is a different axis from Zurgo's leave draw and from [[02 Cards/Creatures/Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor|Gix]]'s per-creature payments: Mindblade is one shot per combat damage step.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on 2, attack with Zurgo on 3. Zurgo connecting is enough for the card even if every token is blocked.
- If Zurgo is blocked and only [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]] Goblins / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]] Humans / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Loyal Apprentice|Loyal Apprentice]] Thopters connect, Mindblade does nothing. Send at least one Warrior through.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] is a second trigger — two cards, 2 life.
- Stacks with Zurgo's leave draw and with Gix. Do not skip Zurgo's attack to "save" Mindblade; Zurgo connecting is the cleanest on-switch.
- The 1/3 body is a blocker. Do not spend protection on it.

## Key Synergies

- **Warriors that connect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ainok Strike Leader|Ainok Strike Leader]] (Ainok and his Goblins — only Ainok is a Warrior; the Goblins are not)
- **Help the Warrior connect**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Invention|Angel of Invention]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]]
- **Extra combat**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]
- **Parallel draw**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor|Gix]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- One card per combat damage event, not per Warrior and not per opponent. Hitting three players with Warriors is still one draw.
- Non-Warrior tokens (Warboss Goblins, Adeline Humans, Apprentice Thopters, Angel Servos) do not count.
- Combat damage only. [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] burn, and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] do not trigger it. You lose 1 life; tight with Gix payments.
