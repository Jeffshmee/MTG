## Oracle Text

At the beginning of each upkeep, if you control no Snakes, create a 1/1 black Snake [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]] with [[03 Effects/Deathtouch|deathtouch]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Ophiomancer is a three-mana Snake factory: if you control no Snakes at each upkeep — including opponents' — you get a 1/1 deathtouch Snake. That token is fodder for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Chittering Witch|Chittering Witch]], plus clamp food and an ETB/leave for [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]]. Keep eating the Snake so the next upkeep makes another; a leftover Snake shuts the engine off. Attack with it when Zurgo is attacking so the leave draws; sac it idle so Zurgo drains.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Default: sac or clamp the Snake every turn. Opponent-upkeep Snakes are off-turn Tremors / Elas / Zurgo drains.
- Caesar sacs the Snake on attack, takes tokens plus draw, and the next upkeep replaces it.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]]: the token enters as a 4/4 Angel, so you control no Snakes and make another Angel every upkeep.
- Deathtouch Snake is a real blocker on the turns you have to keep it.

## Key Synergies

- **Sac outlets**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Chittering Witch|Chittering Witch]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]]
- **ETB / leave**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morbid Opportunist|Morbid Opportunist]]
- **Upgrade**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] (breaks the "already have a Snake" lock)
- **Recursion**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sun Titan|Sun Titan]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- If you leave the Snake, you make nothing on later upkeeps.
- Without a sac outlet or clamp, it is one deathtouch 1/1.
- Not Mobilize — the Snake only draws Zurgo if it actually attacked.
