## Oracle Text

Whenever you attack, you may [[03 Effects/Sacrifice Creature|sacrifice another creature]]. When you do, choose two —  
• [[03 Effects/Create Token|Create two 1/1 red and white Soldier creature tokens]] with [[03 Effects/Haste|haste]] that are tapped and attacking.  
• You [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]] and [[03 Effects/Lose Life|you lose 1 life]].  
• Caesar [[03 Effects/Deal Damage|deals damage]] equal to the number of [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]] you control to target opponent.

## Deck Role & Rating

Caesar is the four-mana modal attack engine: a repeatable sac outlet, two attacking haste Soldiers, a card, or token-count burn — choose two each combat.  
Sacrificing an attacking Mobilize token (Zurgo, Nightblade, later Packbeasts) draws off Zurgo because it left while attacking, then still pays two Caesar modes. Default is **tokens + draw**; switch to **tokens + burn** once Impact Tremors / Elas / Adeline have made the token count lethal. He is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack with Zurgo plus any spare token. Sac the token → Soldiers + draw, or Soldiers + burn if a player is low.
- Default mode pair is **tokens + draw**. Switch to **tokens + burn** once the board is wide.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]] double the "whenever you attack" trigger, so you can sac twice and choose two modes each time.
- The new Soldiers are attacking tokens, so they trigger Zurgo's draw when they leave (end step, sac, or combat) and ping [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] on the way in.
- Do not tap out into Caesar with an empty board. He needs another creature to sacrifice.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Needs another creature to sacrifice. Do not tap out into Caesar with an empty board.
- The burn mode counts **creature tokens**, not all creatures. Non-token beaters do not increase the number.
- He is legendary — only one copy.
