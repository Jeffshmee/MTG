## Oracle Text

When this creature enters, create four 1/1 colorless Myr artifact [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]].

Whenever this creature attacks, you may tap X untapped Myr you control. If you do, this creature gets +X/+0 until end of turn and deals X damage to the player or planeswalker it's attacking.

## Deck Role & Rating

Myr Battlesphere is a seven-mana pile of bodies, not the plan. Four Myr enter into [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], then leave into [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] — draw if they attacked, drain if you clamp or sac them idle. The attack trigger (tap Myr, pump and ping) is optional reach when a player is dead to the X; the deck usually wants those Myr attacking or dying, not sitting tapped. Cast it when you can afford a turn to flood, not instead of Zurgo.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it with Tremors or Elas already out. Four ETBs are the reason to spend seven.
- Attack with the Myr alongside Zurgo so they draw when they leave. Tap them for the ping only when they would not connect anyway.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ogre Battledriver|Ogre Battledriver]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] first so the Myr haste in as 3/1s (or the team grows).
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Redoubled Stormsinger|Redoubled Stormsinger]] copies the four Myr if they entered this turn — a huge leave wave at end of step.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Seven mana. Dead in hand on a slow draw.
- Tapping Myr for the ping fights the "attack so Zurgo draws" plan.
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] makes Angels, not Myr — the tap-ping blanks.
- The Sphere itself is not a token. Zurgo does not pay you for it dying.
