## Oracle Text

Whenever you attack with one or more non-Gnome creatures, put a [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counter]] on Anim Pakal, then [[03 Effects/Create Token|create X 1/1 colorless Gnome artifact creature tokens]] that are tapped and attacking, where X is the number of [[03 Effects/Plus One Counters|+1/+1 counters]] on Anim Pakal.

## Deck Role & Rating

Anim Pakal is a snowballing attack-token engine: each time you attack with a non-Gnome, she gets a counter and then makes that many attacking Gnomes. Zurgo is a non-Gnome, so she triggers while sitting back; Caesar, Adeline, and Hero are the same. Those Gnomes are tokens, so they ping Tremors / Elas on the way in and draw off Zurgo when they leave. Isshin puts two counters and two waves in one combat; Aurelia’s second combat sees the larger count. She is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- She does **not** need to attack. Swing Zurgo (or Caesar, Hero, Adeline) and she still triggers. First attack is one Gnome; it gets bigger every combat she survives.
- Do not lead with only Gnomes. Keep at least one non-Gnome in the attack or the engine stalls.
- Isshin / Windcrag Siege: two counters and two waves in a single combat. Aurelia extra combat: the second swing sees the new counter count — this is one of the fastest go-wide kills in the pile.
- Caesar can sac a Gnome on a later attack. Clamp the 1/1s while they are still 1 toughness. Divine Visitation turns each Gnome into a 4/4 Angel.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Gnomes themselves do not trigger her. A board of only Gnomes stalls the engine.
- She is a 1/2. Any ping kills her and resets the counter count.
- She is legendary — only one copy.
