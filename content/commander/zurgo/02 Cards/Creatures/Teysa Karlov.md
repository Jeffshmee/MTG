## Oracle Text

If a creature dying causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability [[03 Effects/Double Trigger|triggers an additional time]].  
[[03 Effects/Create Token|Creature tokens]] you control have [[03 Effects/Vigilance|vigilance]] and [[03 Effects/Lifelink|lifelink]].

## Deck Role & Rating

Teysa is the death-trigger half of the doubler pair (Isshin is the attack half). She doubles Bastion, Elas, Garna, and Zurgo whenever a creature dies, and she gives the token army vigilance and lifelink so you can keep attacking. Teysa doubles **dies only**: Mobilize end-step sacrifice is a death, so Zurgo draws twice per token; exile or bounce is Zurgo once and Teysa does not apply. She is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play her before you start sacrificing. The anthem is live immediately; the doubling matters the first time a token dies.
- Attacking token **dies** (blocked, Skullclamp, Caesar / Seer / Yahenni sac, Mobilize end-step sacrifice) → Zurgo [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draws two]]. Non-attacking token **dies** → each opponent [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|loses 2 life]]. Same token **exiled or bounced** → Zurgo once, Teysa off.
- Best with [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] already out — each dying token drains twice *and* Zurgo fires twice. Garna draws twice on attacking deaths.
- Pair with Isshin: attack triggers fire twice, then death triggers fire twice. Vigilance lets Adeline / Hero tokens attack without tapping.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Does **not** apply if the token is exiled or bounced. Those leaves still trigger Zurgo once, but Teysa sees no death.
- Does not double ETB triggers ([[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], Elas lifegain).
- She is legendary — only one copy.
