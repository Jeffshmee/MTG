## Oracle Text

[[03 Effects/Vigilance|Vigilance]]

Whenever this creature enters or attacks, you may return target permanent card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard to the battlefield.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

Sun Titan returns a mana-value-3-or-less permanent on ETB and every attack. Priority is the cheap engines: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion]] — then [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Morbid Opportunist|Morbid]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Nightblade Brigade|Nightblade]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ophiomancer|Ophiomancer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Redoubled Stormsinger|Stormsinger]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]]. Do not grab a random land unless you are flooding. It makes no tokens of its own; the value is putting the loop back so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] keeps drawing and draining.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- First target: whatever closes the loop you already have (Tremors if you have tokens, Elas or Bastion if things are dying, Seer or clamp if you need an outlet, Greaves if Zurgo is naked).
- Attack every turn. Vigilance keeps it back as a blocker.
- Extra combat with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] or [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] is a second recursion.
- Empty yard: do not slam it. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Nightblade Brigade|Nightblade]] surveil or a cheap death first.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Six mana, and it makes no tokens.
- Misses Zurgo, Caesar, Solemn, Hero, Goldnight, Battledriver, Siege-Gang, and Divine Visitation (all mana value 4+).
- A yard of only lands and expensive cards is a 6/6 with vigilance. Do not take the land unless you are flooding.
