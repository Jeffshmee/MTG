## Deck Role & Rating

Loyal Apprentice is a two-mana lieutenant token engine. If [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] is in play, every combat you get a 1/1 Thopter with flying and haste — a cheap aerial attacker that pings [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]] on ETB and pays Zurgo on leave. Lieutenant checks that you control the commander, not that he attacks; without Zurgo it is a 1/1 haste. That is the whole card: cheap Mobilize-adjacent fodder that flies over.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Ideal: turn 2 Apprentice, turn 3 Zurgo, turn 4 attack. Beginning of combat Thopter, then Zurgo's Mobilize. The Thopter can fly over for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor|Gix]].
- You can keep Zurgo back and still get the Thopter, but this deck wants Zurgo attacking anyway.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]: second Thopter. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]]: Thopter ETB pumps the team before declare attackers.
- [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] the Thopter after combat (Zurgo drain, two cards) or sac it to [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aron, Benalia's Ruin|Aron]].
- If Zurgo is stolen, lieutenant is off and the opponent has your engine. Do not recast Apprentice until Zurgo is back.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Dead lieutenant if Zurgo is not in play. The body is a 1/1.
- The Thopter is not a Warrior — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mindblade Render|Mindblade Render]] does not count it unless a Warrior also connected.
- Divine Visitation 4/4s cannot be clamped.
