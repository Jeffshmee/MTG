## Deck Role & Rating

Packbeasts is a 0/4 vigilance Mobilize 3 — the biggest Mobilize number in the pile. One attack is three Zurgo draws, three Tremors pings, three Bastion / Elas drains, and a completed Belladonna ladder off a three-mana Ox. Vigilance means it still holds the ground after swinging; the 0 power does not matter, the tokens do the work. Isshin turns it into six Warriors; Bone-Cairn gives those tokens deathtouch. It is live in the 100.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Attack every turn it is safe. Zurgo plus Packbeasts is four attacking Warriors from one combat, all leaving at end of step.
- Isshin / Windcrag Siege: Mobilize 3 becomes six tokens. That is six Zurgo leaves and Belladonna’s ladder completed twice over. Aurelia is a second Mobilize 3 on the extra combat.
- Bone-Cairn Butcher gives those three (or six) tokens deathtouch. Send them into blockers they would never survive — they were dying at end of turn anyway.
- Divine Visitation replaces them with 4/4 Angels that still get sacrificed at end of turn — three 4/4 attackers out of a 3-mana Ox. Caesar can sac one of the Warriors on the attack for two modes and still leave the others to Zurgo.
