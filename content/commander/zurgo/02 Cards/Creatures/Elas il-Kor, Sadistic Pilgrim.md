## Deck Role & Rating

Elas is the two-mana drain engine that taxes both halves of the token loop. Another creature entering — Zurgo Mobilize, Adeline Humans, Hero Soldiers, Caesar’s pair, Thalisse Spirits — gains you 1 life; another creature dying — Mobilize end step, Skullclamp, Caesar sac, wipes — makes each opponent lose 1. He replaced Bone Devourer because he works every token with no setup turn. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] doubles the **dies** half, not the ETB; that is the half that wins games. Deathtouch lets him sit back as a blocker while the tokens attack.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast on turn 2, Zurgo on 3, attack. The first Mobilize token gains you 1 on ETB and drains the table when it is sacrificed at end of turn.
- Stacks with [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] — ETB damage + death drain + lifegain on the same loop. One Zurgo attack is already three tax events.
- Caesar or Clamp a token: Elas drains, Zurgo draws, Garna draws if it was attacking. Teysa makes that drain 2 and Zurgo draw two.
- Sit him back. Deathtouch trades with any attacker; you do not need him in the red zone.
