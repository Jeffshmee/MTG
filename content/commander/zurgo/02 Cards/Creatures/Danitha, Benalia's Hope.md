## Deck Role & Rating

Danitha is a five-mana combat body that cheats [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Stormbeacon Blade|Stormbeacon Blade]] or [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Infantry Shield|Infantry Shield]] (or a fallen aura) onto herself. Lifelink plus first strike is the Mardu combat package; vigilance keeps her as a blocker. Cut [[02 Cards/Creatures/Anafenza, Unyielding Lineage|Anafenza]] for her. Mark trade 03/09.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- ETB with Blade or Shield in hand or yard. Attack.
- Lifelink turns Elas / Bastion / Tremors into a race she can survive.
