## Oracle Text

At the beginning of combat on your turn, create a 1/1 red Goblin [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature token]]. That token gains [[03 Effects/Haste|haste]] until end of turn and attacks this combat if able.

## Deck Role & Rating

Legion Warboss is a core cheap combat engine: every combat a haste Goblin that must attack, and mentor if Warboss himself attacks. The Goblin is a token, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] draws if it dies attacking and drains if you [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] it later. It needs Zurgo or another attacker — a lone 1/1 forced to swing is not the card. Beginning-of-combat ETB also feeds [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]] before you even declare attackers.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Curve: Warboss on 3 with Zurgo already out, or Zurgo the next turn. Beginning of combat Goblin ETB, then declare Zurgo + Warboss + Goblin; Mobilize token ETBs attacking.
- Mentor the 1/1 Goblin (or a Mobilize token) when Warboss attacks. That body lives through more blocks; it also becomes a worse clamp target.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]]: second Goblin, second mentor, second Mobilize.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]] can sac a Goblin that survived. Clamp it after combat if it is still 1 toughness.
- If Zurgo is dead and you have no other attacker, the Goblin still has to attack. Hold Warboss a turn if that is a free chump for the table.

- **Must attack with**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ainok Strike Leader|Ainok Strike Leader]]
- **Same combat ETBs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]]
- **Leaves**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]]

- **Upgrade**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] (Goblin is a 4/4 Angel with haste that must attack)

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- The Goblin must attack. There is no "make a blocker" mode.
- Mentor on the Goblin turns off Skullclamp. Divine Visitation 4/4s also cannot be clamped.
- The Goblin is created at the beginning of combat on your turn only. Casting Warboss after combat makes nothing until next turn.
