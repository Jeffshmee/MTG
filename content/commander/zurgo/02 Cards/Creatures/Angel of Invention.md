## Deck Role & Rating

Angel of Invention is a go-wide closer: a static +1/+1 anthem plus fabricate 2. Take two Servos when [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo]] are in play; take counters when the board is empty and you need a 4/3 flyer that lives. The anthem is what [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]]'s until-end-of-turn pumps sit on top of for the kill. Servos enter unattacking, so Zurgo drains if they die the same turn and draws if they attack later and then leave.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast her onto an existing board, not as a 5-mana 2/1 into nothing. The anthem is the card; the Angel is a flying lifelink closer.
- Fabricate Servos if Tremors / Elas / Zurgo are out. Fabricate counters if you need her to survive a block or a ping.
- Goldnight plus this anthem is the one-shot: tokens ETB, Goldnight stacks +1/+1 until end of turn, Angel's static sits underneath.
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Aurelia, the Warleader|Aurelia]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/All-Out Assault|All-Out Assault]] lets the Servos (and the team) attack again under the anthem.
- Do not [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] the turn she is in play expecting 1/1s to die — they are 2/2s.

## Key Synergies

- **Anthem / go-wide**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldnight Commander|Goldnight Commander]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Adeline, Resplendent Cathar|Adeline]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hero of Bladehold|Hero of Bladehold]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Legion Warboss|Legion Warboss]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]]
- **Servo / token payoffs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas il-Kor]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Static +1/+1 turns Mobilize tokens, Goblins, Thopters, and Servos into 2/2s. Skullclamp no longer kills them while she is in play.
- Double white at five mana. A 2/1 if you take Servos dies to any ping.
- Fabricate Servos have no haste — they do not attack the turn they enter.
