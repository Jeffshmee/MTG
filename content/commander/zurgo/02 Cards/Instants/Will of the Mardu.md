## Oracle Text

Choose one. If you control a commander as you cast this spell, you may choose both instead.

• Create a number of 1/1 red Warrior [[03 Effects/Create Token|creature tokens]] equal to the number of creatures target player controls.

• Will of the Mardu [[03 Effects/Deal Damage|deals damage]] to target creature equal to the number of creatures you control.
