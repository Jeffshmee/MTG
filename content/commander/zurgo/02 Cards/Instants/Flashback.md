## Oracle Text

Target instant or sorcery card in your graveyard gains [[03 Effects/Flashback|flashback]] until end of turn. The flashback cost is equal to its mana cost. (You may cast that card from your graveyard for its flashback cost. Then [[03 Effects/Exile|exile]] it.)

## Deck Role & Rating

Flashback is a {R} instant that gives one yard instant or sorcery flashback this turn, cost equal to its printed mana cost. The 100 has almost no spell recursion — [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Lingering Souls|Lingering Souls]] already has its own cheaper flashback, and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Sun Titan|Sun Titan]] only returns permanents. The real job is recasting the win-makers: [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Tempt with Vengeance|Tempt with Vengeance]] (playtest overperformed), [[02 Cards/Instants/Grand Crescendo|Grand Crescendo]] (tokens plus indestructible), [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Heroic Reinforcements|Heroic Reinforcements]] (tokens, pump, haste), and [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Hour of Reckoning|Hour of Reckoning]] (convoke still works from the yard). It does not make tokens, does not Mobilize, and does not cover the Greaves/theft hole.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast a token spell, then {R} plus that spell’s cost again the same turn if you have the mana.
- Hold Flashback after a wipe to recast Grand Crescendo at instant speed (tokens plus indestructible).
- Recast Tempt when X is large and Divine Visitation or Goldnight is already out.
- Do not target Lingering Souls — its printed flashback {1}{B} is cheaper than {2}{W}.
- Hour of Reckoning from the yard still convokes; tap the tokens you just made.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Dead until an instant or sorcery is in the yard.
- Recast is full price — two spells in one turn.
- Does not protect Zurgo (playtest hole is theft; this is not a suit).
- Owned extra. Physical copy still Box until a named cut. Suggested: [[02 Cards/Instants/Abrade|Abrade]] (73). Do not cut Tempt, Goldnight, Divine Visitation, Swords, or Greaves. Mortify is already the Prismatic Ending cut.
