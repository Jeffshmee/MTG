As an additional cost to cast this spell, [[03 Effects/Sacrifice|sacrifice]] an artifact or creature.

[[03 Effects/Draw a Card|Draw two cards]] and create a [[03 Effects/Treasure|Treasure]] token. (It's an artifact with "{T}, Sacrifice this token: [[03 Effects/Add Mana|Add one mana of any color]].")

## Deck Role & Rating

Deadly Dispute is a main-deck instant.  

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Tokens ETB into [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]] and leave into [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]].
- Use spare Mobilize tokens as fodder. Deaths still pay [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zurgo Stormrender|Zurgo Stormrender]] and [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]].
- Hold it for a turn you are already making or sacrificing tokens so the draw stacks with Zurgo.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Divine Visitation|Divine Visitation]] upgrades the tokens; [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Skullclamp|Skullclamp]] then no longer kills 4/4s.
- One-shot. Sequence it around an attack or a wipe, then it is gone.
