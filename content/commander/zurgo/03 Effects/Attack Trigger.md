# Attack Trigger

A triggered ability that triggers because a creature is attacking (or because you attacked). The trigger goes on the stack after attackers are declared.

The core of this list. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Isshin, Two Heavens as One|Isshin]] and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Windcrag Siege|Windcrag Siege]] make these fire an extra time.
