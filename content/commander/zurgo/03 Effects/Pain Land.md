# Pain Land

A land that can tap for coloured mana by dealing 1 damage to you, or that asks you to pay 2 life to enter untapped.

[[02 Cards/Lands/Battlefield Forge|Battlefield Forge]] and [[02 Cards/Lands/Caves of Koilos|Caves of Koilos]] keep a turn-3 Zurgo realistic. The shocks ([[02 Cards/Lands/Godless Shrine|Godless Shrine]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Blood Crypt|Blood Crypt]]) do the same job by paying life on ETB.
