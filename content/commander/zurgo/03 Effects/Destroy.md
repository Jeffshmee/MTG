# Destroy

Move a permanent from the battlefield to its owner's graveyard. Indestructible permanents cannot be destroyed this way.

Spot removal and one-sided wipes. [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Hour of Reckoning|Hour of Reckoning]] destroys nontoken creatures only.
