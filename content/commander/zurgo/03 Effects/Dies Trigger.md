# Dies Trigger

A triggered ability that triggers when a creature dies (is put into a graveyard from the battlefield).

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa Karlov]] makes these trigger an additional time. See also [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|Opponent Loses Life]] and [[03 Effects/Sacrifice|Sacrifice]].
