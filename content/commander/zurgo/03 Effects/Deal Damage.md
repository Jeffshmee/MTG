# Deal Damage

Cause a player, planeswalker, battle, or creature to be dealt a stated amount of damage. Damage to a player reduces life; damage to a creature is marked on it.

Used as burn and as a finisher — [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Impact Tremors|Impact Tremors]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Garna, Bloodfist of Keld|Garna]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]].
