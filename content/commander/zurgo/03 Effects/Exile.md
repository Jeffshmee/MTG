# Exile

Put the object into the exile zone. Exiled cards are not in the graveyard and do not die.

[[02 Cards/Instants/Swords to Plowshares|Swords to Plowshares]] exiles a creature. Exile does **not** count as dying, so [[02 Cards/Creatures/Teysa Karlov|Teysa]] will not double Zurgo.
