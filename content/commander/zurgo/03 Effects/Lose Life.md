# Lose Life

Decrease a player's life total by the stated amount. Losing life is not damage and is not prevented by prevention effects that stop damage.

This deck sometimes pays life ([[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Thunder of Unity|Thunder of Unity]]). Distinct from [[03 Effects/Opponent Loses Life|Opponent Loses Life]] and from [[03 Effects/Deal Damage|Deal Damage]].
