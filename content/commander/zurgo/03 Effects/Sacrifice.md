# Sacrifice

To sacrifice a permanent, move it from the battlefield directly to its owner's graveyard. You can sacrifice only permanents you control. A sacrificed creature dies.

Mobilize tokens are sacrificed at end of turn. Outlets include [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Yahenni, Undying Partisan|Yahenni]], and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Caesar, Legion's Emperor|Caesar]].
