# Myriad

Whenever this creature attacks, for each opponent other than the defending player you may create a token copy that's tapped and attacking that player. Exile the tokens at end of combat.

On [[02 Cards/Creatures/Goldlust Triad|Goldlust Triad]]. The copies are tokens, so they still trigger Zurgo when they leave.
