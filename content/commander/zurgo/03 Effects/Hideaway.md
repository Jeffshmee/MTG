# Hideaway

When this permanent enters, look at the top N cards of your library, exile one face down, then put the rest on the bottom in a random order. A later ability lets you play the exiled card.

[[02 Cards/Lands/Windbrisk Heights|Windbrisk Heights]] unlocks after you attack with three or more creatures.
