# Play Patterns

How this **owned 54** is supposed to play. Commander is [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]. Every line is judged by whether it makes a disposable body, gains life toward +10, or pays off a death.

See also [[06 Strategy/Win Conditions|Win Conditions]], [[06 Strategy/Playtest Notes|Playtest Notes]], and [[01 Deck/Current Deck|Current Deck]].

**Status of this draft:** written against the **54** sleeved 2026-09-10. Target is 100. Soul Sisters and extra sac outlets besides Ayli / Bartolomé / Priest are still **🟢 owned extras** on [[01 Deck/Sideboard|Sideboard]]. Vito, Bastion, and Bloodwitch are live. Do not take a last copy out of Maralen, Zurgo, or Kykar.

## The engine

Ayli has two jobs, both `{1}` plus a body:

1. **Gain** — `{1}`, sacrifice another creature: gain life equal to its toughness.
2. **Exile** — `{1}{W}{B}`, sacrifice another creature: exile target nonland. Only if you have **10 or more life** than you started with.

Deathtouch on a 2/3 is real in combat, but she is not the attacker. This pile is trying to turn ETBs into +10 so the exile mode turns on.

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney]] is the piece that makes this pile a deck. Power 2 or less that would trigger on ETB or death triggers an extra time, and those bodies cannot be blocked by power 3 or greater. Live P≤2 ETBs: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Novice Inspector|Novice Inspector]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage|Infestation Sage]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Resolute Reinforcements|Resolute Reinforcements]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]] (2/2), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mentor of the Meek|Mentor of the Meek]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Vitality|Angel of Vitality]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]]. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Marauding Blight-Priest|Marauding Blight-Priest]] is 3/2 — Delney does not double it.

Ayli's **activated** abilities are not triggered. Delney does not double the sac-for-life or the exile.

## Opening and curve

**Keep** a hand that makes `{W}{B}` and casts Ayli on 2, with at least one cheap body or a life ETB.

- Untapped: [[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]], Plains, Swamp (five foils in the pile). [[02 Cards/Lands/Shadowy Backstreet|Shadowy Backstreet]] and [[02 Cards/Lands/Shattered Sanctum|Shattered Sanctum]] are the duals — Sanctum untaps if you already have two other lands.
- Tapped fillers ([[02 Cards/Lands/Orzhov Guildgate|Guildgate]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Temple of Silence|Temple]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Scoured Barrens|Scoured Barrens]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Adventurer's Inn|Inn]]) are a third land, not a second.
- Turn 1: Hinterland, Inspector, Infestation Sage, Sacred Cat is in Arahbo — do not look for it here.
- Turn 2: Ayli, or Mentor / Resolute / Pridemate / Bloodwitch if you need the body first.
- Turn 3: Delney if the board is P≤2 ETBs. Do not lead Delney into an empty board.

**Mulligan** hands with no black, or two tapped duals and no one-drop.

Ramp in this pile is **lands only**. [[02 Cards/Instants/Culling the Weak|Culling the Weak]] is the ritual off a fodder body — hold it to dump Delney + a spell, not to turn-1 nothing.

## Sequencing this pile

Default: put a 1/1 or 2/2 into play every turn, then Ayli, then Delney.

| Priority | Card | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier\|Hinterland Sanctifier]] | Life on every creature ETB. Delney doubles it. This is how +10 happens without Soul Warden. |
| 2 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout\|Delney]] | Doubles the rest of the table. Play it after one P≤2 body. |
| 3 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mentor of the Meek\|Mentor of the Meek]] | Draw on 2-power ETBs. Delney doubles the trigger. Pay `{1}` when you can. |
| 4 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate\|Pridemate]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Vitality\|Angel of Vitality]] | Life becomes a body. Angel also pads the +10. |
| 5 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Resolute Reinforcements\|Resolute]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Novice Inspector\|Inspector]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage\|Sage]] | Fodder. Sage leaves an Insect. Inspector leaves a Clue. |
| 6 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch\|Bloodwitch]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Marauding Blight-Priest\|Blight-Priest]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose\|Vito]] / [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance\|Bastion]] | Death drain and life-gain drain. Live plan 2. Zulaport still SB. |
| 7 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Emeritus of Woe\|Emeritus of Woe]] | Tutor face finds the missing engine (a Sister, a drain, a sac outlet). |
| 8 | [[02 Cards/Creatures/Massacre Wurm\|Massacre Wurm]] | Closer once they have a board. Not turn 4. |

[[02 Cards/Instants/Slice from the Shadows\|Slice from the Shadows]] is the cheap black removal. Culling is the ritual.

Ayli exile: only after Hinterland / Angel / Inn / Blight-Priest have put you +10. Sac a token or Inspector, not Delney.

## Common mistakes

- Casting Ayli with no second creature. She does nothing.
- Leading Delney into an empty board.
- Sacrificing Delney or Mentor to the first exile.
- Sleeving a last copy out of Maralen, Zurgo, or Kykar.
- Putting 🟡 Ordered into current-deck before it is in hand.
- Treating this pile as a Sister deck. Sisters are still on the sideboard. Vito and Bastion are live.
