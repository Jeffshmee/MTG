# Win Conditions

How this list closes a table. All three plans run through [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]] and a stream of cheap bodies.

Play order and sequencing live on [[06 Strategy/Play Patterns|Play Patterns]]. Table observations live on [[06 Strategy/Playtest Notes|Playtest Notes]]. Card scores live on the individual pages and in [[01 Deck/Current Deck|Current Deck]].

**Draft note:** written against the **54** sleeved 2026-09-10. Plan 1 is online. Plan 2 is live (Vito, Bastion, Bloodwitch). Plan 3 still wants Reservoir / Sisters.

---

## 1. Ayli exile lock (primary on this pile)

**How it wins:** get +10 life from ETBs, then `{1}{W}{B}` plus a fodder body exiles their nonland every turn. Repeat.

**Live in the 54**

- Commander: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli]]
- Life ETB: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]] (Delney doubles it), [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Vitality|Angel of Vitality]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Adventurer's Inn|Adventurer's Inn]]
- Bodies that become life: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Pridemate]]
- Fodder: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Resolute Reinforcements|Resolute Reinforcements]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Novice Inspector|Novice Inspector]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage|Infestation Sage]]
- Engine: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mentor of the Meek|Mentor of the Meek]]
- Find the missing piece: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Emeritus of Woe|Emeritus of Woe]] (tutor face)

**Still 🟢 on SB (sleeve to make this reliable):** [[02 Cards/Creatures/Soul Warden|Soul Warden]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Soul's Attendant|Soul's Attendant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Suture Priest|Suture Priest]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Serra Ascendant|Serra Ascendant]]

Without a Sister, Hinterland + Delney + two more ETBs is the +10 line. Do not exile until you are actually +10.

## 2. Drain — creatures die

**How it wins:** every death taxes the table. Ayli and the other sac outlets feed it. You do not need to connect.

**Live in the 54**

- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]] (death drain)
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Marauding Blight-Priest|Marauding Blight-Priest]] (life → they lose life)
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose|Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]] (life → that much loss)
- [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] (death drain + a Soldier)
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Massacre Wurm|Massacre Wurm]] (their creatures die → they lose life)
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage|Infestation Sage]] leaves a body when it dies
- [[02 Cards/Creatures/Bartolomé del Presidio|Bartolomé]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Priest of Forgotten Gods|Priest]] (outlets)

**Still 🟢 on SB:** [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zulaport Cutthroat|Zulaport Cutthroat]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Blood Artist|Blood Artist]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Cruel Celebrant|Cruel Celebrant]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim|Elas]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Enduring Tenacity|Enduring Tenacity]]

Plan 2 is online. Sleeve Zulaport or Elas if you want a third drain.

## 3. Life-to-damage — Reservoir / Vito

**How it wins:** the same +10 pile becomes a gun. Reservoir at 50. Vito (and Enduring Tenacity) turn each life gain into a ping.

**Live:** [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose|Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Well of Lost Dreams|Well of Lost Dreams]] (draws off the life, not a close).

**Still 🟢 on SB:** [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Aetherflux Reservoir|Aetherflux Reservoir]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Enduring Tenacity|Enduring Tenacity]]

Do not sleeve Reservoir until the life engine is on the table (Hinterland / Sisters / Angel). Vito is already the ping.

## How to choose a plan in-game

- **You have Ayli, Hinterland, and a second body** → plan 1. Get +10, start exiling their engine.
- **They are going wide** → Massacre Wurm, then sac the leftovers. Plan 2 is live (Vito / Bastion / Bloodwitch).
- **You are already +20 and Reservoir is in hand** → plan 3. Do not spin the Reservoir on 20 life.
