## Oracle Text

Until end of turn, target creature gets +1/+0 and gains lifelink and "When this creature dies, return it to the battlefield tapped under its owner's control."

---

## Deck Role & Rating

NEO 121 foil. One turn of lifelink and a death return. A sac outlet sends the creature back tapped. It is not a permanent engine. Reanimate is 77.

## Play Patterns & Lines

Cast it on a drain creature, sac that creature, and it comes back tapped.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]

- **Sac**: Ayli, Carrion Feeder, Viscera Seer if it arrives.

## Anti-synergies / Notes

The return lasts one death. The +1/+0 does not help Ayli's toughness sac.
