## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Instants/Despark|Despark]] is cheap exile for a permanent with mana value 4 or greater. Instant {W}{B}. The cheap answer for fat while Ayli's exile is still off.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold for the 4-drop engine, not a 3-drop dork. Massacre Wurm on their side is a legal target.
- Does not hit mana value 3 or less. Slice or Unmaking for those.
