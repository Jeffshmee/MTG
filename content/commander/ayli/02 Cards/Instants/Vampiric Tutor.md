## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Instants/Vampiric Tutor|Vampiric Tutor]] is {B} at instant speed: put any card on top, lose 2 life. That is the find for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Carrion Feeder|Carrion Feeder]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Ashnod's Altar|Ashnod's Altar]], or [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Toxic Deluge|Toxic Deluge]]. The life loss is a cost Ayli already wants. 🟡 Ordered (Axion_Now CMR 156) — not in hand. Do not sleeve a proxy.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- End of their turn: put the engine you need on top, draw it on yours.
- After a wipe: find [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sevinne's Reclamation|Sevinne's Reclamation]] or [[02 Cards/Sorceries/Reanimate|Reanimate]], not a 1/1.
- Do not fire it at 3 life if the only plan left is Ayli exile at 10.
