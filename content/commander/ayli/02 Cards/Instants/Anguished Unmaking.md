## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Instants/Anguished Unmaking|Anguished Unmaking]] is hard exile for a nonland at {1}{W}{B} and 3 life. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]'s exile needs +10 and a body; this does not. The 3 life paid still feeds [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose|Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]] if they are in play — you gained nothing, so they do not trigger. Point it at the permanent that stops +10.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Hold for the engine you cannot wait for +10 to answer — a restock, a stax piece, a planeswalker.
- Despark is cheaper on MV 4+. Unmaking hits anything nonland.
- Do not fire it at a 2/2 you could Slice.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes
