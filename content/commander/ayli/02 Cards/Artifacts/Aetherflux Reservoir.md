Whenever you cast a spell, you [[03 Effects/Gain Life|gain 1 life]] for each spell you've cast this turn.

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Artifacts/Aetherflux Reservoir|Aetherflux Reservoir]] is plan 3. Pay 50 life: deal 50. Each spell you cast also gains life. Do not sleeve it until the life engine is on the table (Hinterland / Sisters / Angel). 🟢 Owned extra on the sideboard — not sleeved.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sleeve after a Sister. Cast it when you are already high — the spell-gain stacks toward 50.
