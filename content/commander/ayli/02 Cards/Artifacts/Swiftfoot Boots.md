## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Artifacts/Swiftfoot Boots|Swiftfoot Boots]] is haste and hexproof on Ayli. You can still target her after equip. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Lightning Greaves|Lightning Greaves]] is still on the sideboard if you need shroud instead.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Equip Ayli the turn she enters if the table has bounce or theft.
- Hexproof lets you Slice / Gift around her. Shroud (Greaves) would not.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
