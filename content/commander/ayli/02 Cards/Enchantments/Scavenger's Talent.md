## Oracle Text

Whenever one or more creatures you control die, [[03 Effects/Create Token|create a Food token]]. This ability triggers only once each turn. Level 2: whenever you sacrifice a permanent, target player mills two. Level 3: sacrifice three other nonland permanents to return a creature card from your graveyard with a finality counter.
