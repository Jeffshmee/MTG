## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] ETB-makes a 1/1 Soldier and drains whenever a creature you control dies. Live enchantment drain next to [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]]. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zulaport Cutthroat|Zulaport Cutthroat]] is still on the sideboard — Bastion is the death tax that is actually sleeved. The Soldier is fodder for Ayli; Ayli exile is not a death and will not trigger Bastion.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play before you start sacrificing. The ETB Soldier is a body for Hinterland and for Ayli.
- Sac Sage / Inspector / the Soldier, not Delney. Each death is 1 life lost and 1 gained.
- Do not fire Ayli exile expecting Bastion to see it — exile is not a death.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
- **Deaths**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Zulaport Cutthroat|Zulaport Cutthroat]] *(owned extra)*, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]] *(owned extra)*

## Anti-synergies / Notes
