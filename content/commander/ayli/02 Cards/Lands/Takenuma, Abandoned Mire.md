## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Lands/Takenuma, Abandoned Mire|Takenuma, Abandoned Mire]] taps for {B} and channels: mill three, return a creature or planeswalker from the yard to hand. The channel costs {1} less for each legendary you control — Ayli, Delney, Vito, Karlov count. Recursion, not bounce.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it as a Swamp when you need {{B}} on 2. Channel later if the yard has Hinterland, Delney, or Vito.
- Do not channel for a 1/1. Priority: Delney → Hinterland → Vito / Bloodwitch.
