## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Lands/Westvale Abbey|Westvale Abbey]] is colourless mana plus a token factory: {5}, {T}, pay 1 life — a 1/1 Human Cleric. Those Clerics are Ayli fodder and Hinterland ETBs. Transform (five creatures, {5}, tap) is a late demon, not the plan. Do not count it as {W} or {B}.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Activate for a Cleric when you have the mana and need a body, not as land 2 for Ayli.
- The 1 life paid is real — this deck can spare it toward +10 math only if Hinterland recoups.
- Do not transform unless the game is stalled and five creatures are cheap.

## Key Synergies
