## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Lands/Command Tower|Command Tower]] is an untapped any-colour source for Ayli. Live with Plains and Swamp as the untapped core of this pile.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sequence so Ayli is {W}{B} on 2. Tower plus a basic is a keep.
- Tapped duals ([[02 Cards/Lands/Orzhov Guildgate|Orzhov Guildgate]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Temple of Silence|Temple of Silence]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Scoured Barrens|Scoured Barrens]], [[02 Cards/Lands/Adventurer's Inn|Adventurer's Inn]]) are a third land, not a second.
