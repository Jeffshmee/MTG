## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Lands/Vault of the Archangel|Vault of the Archangel]] is colourless mana plus a combat pump: {2}{W}{B}, {T} — creatures you control gain deathtouch and lifelink. Lifelink is a life-gain event for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose|Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]]. Ayli already has deathtouch.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Activate on an attack that will connect, or on a block. Not land 2 for Ayli.
- Vito plus this activation is a drain if damage happens. Empty-board Vault is a colourless land.
