## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Bartolomé del Presidio|Bartolomé del Presidio]] is a free sac outlet that grows: sacrifice another creature or artifact, put a +1/+1 counter on him. Ayli's sac costs {1}; Bartolomé does not. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Viscera Seer|Viscera Seer]] is still on the sideboard — this is the free outlet that is sleeved. The death feeds [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] and [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]].

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sac Sage, Inspector, or a token, not Delney or Hinterland.
- The artifact clause can eat a leftover Clue if you need the counter and not the draw.
- He cannot block well as a 2/1 — do not count him as a defender.
