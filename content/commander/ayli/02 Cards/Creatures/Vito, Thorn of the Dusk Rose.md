## Oracle Text

Whenever you gain life, target opponent loses that much life.
{3}{B}{B}: Creatures you control gain [[03 Effects/Lifelink|lifelink]] until end of turn.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose|Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose]] turns each life gain into that much loss for a target opponent. Live closer with [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]] and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]]. [[02 Cards/Artifacts/Aetherflux Reservoir|Aetherflux Reservoir]] is still on the sideboard — do not wait for it. The {3}{B}{B} lifelink dump is a combat trick, not the main line.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play after Hinterland or Angel so each later ETB is a ping. Delney-doubled Hinterland is two life, two loss.
- Sac-for-life on Ayli now also kills. Point the trigger at the player you can actually finish.
- Pay the lifelink only if the board can connect and you need a second drain this combat.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
- **Life**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Soul Warden|Soul Warden]] *(owned extra)*, [[02 Cards/Creatures/Marauding Blight-Priest|Marauding Blight-Priest]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Enduring Tenacity|Enduring Tenacity]] *(owned extra)*

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Targets one opponent. It does not drain the table. Empty-board Vito is a 1/3.
- Ayli exile is not life gain. Sac-for-life is.
