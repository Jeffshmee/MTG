## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]] is the engine and the win. Two jobs, both a mana plus a body: {1} sacrifice another creature to gain life equal to its toughness, and {1}{W}{B} sacrifice another creature to exile a nonland once you are **+10** life. Deathtouch on a 2/3 is real in combat, but she is not the attacker. Every other card is judged by whether it makes a disposable body, gains life toward +10, or pays off a death. Her activated abilities are not triggered — [[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney, Streetwise Lookout]] does not double them.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Keep a hand that makes {W}{B} and casts her on 2 with at least one cheap body or a life ETB.
- Turn 1: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Novice Inspector|Novice Inspector]], or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage|Infestation Sage]]. Turn 2: Ayli. Turn 3: Delney if a P≤2 body is already down.
- Exile only after Hinterland / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Vitality|Angel of Vitality]] / [[02 Cards/Lands/Adventurer's Inn|Adventurer's Inn]] / [[02 Cards/Creatures/Marauding Blight-Priest|Marauding Blight-Priest]] have actually put you +10. Sac a token or Inspector, not Delney.
- Casting her with no second creature does nothing. Do not lead Delney into an empty board.
