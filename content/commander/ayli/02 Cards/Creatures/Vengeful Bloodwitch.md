## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]] is the death-drain that is actually sleeved. When a creature you control dies, each opponent loses 1 and you gain 1. Delney doubles it (2/1). Start of plan 2 with no Zulaport yet.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play before you start sacrificing. Ayli sac-for-life then also drains.
- Pairs with Sage / Inspector / Resolute dying.
