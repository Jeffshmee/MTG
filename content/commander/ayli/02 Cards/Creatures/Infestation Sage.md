## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage|Infestation Sage]] is a 1/1 that leaves a 1/1 Insect when it dies. Fodder that replaces itself. Delney doubles its ETB if anything cares. Sac to Ayli, keep the Insect.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Turn-1 black. Sac it to Ayli or Culling; the Insect is the next body.
