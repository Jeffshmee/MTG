## Oracle Text

Whenever another creature you control with power 2 or less enters, you may pay {1}. If you do, [[03 Effects/Draw a Card|draw a card]].

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Mentor of the Meek|Mentor of the Meek]] draws whenever another 2-or-less-power creature enters, if you pay {1}. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney, Streetwise Lookout]] doubles the trigger. This is the card draw in the sleeved pile.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play it when you can pay {1} the same turn a 2-power body enters.
- Delney out: two draws per qualifying ETB. Skip the payment if you are holding Culling for a dump.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
- **2-power ETBs**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Resolute Reinforcements|Resolute Reinforcements]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney, Streetwise Lookout]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Novice Inspector|Novice Inspector]]
