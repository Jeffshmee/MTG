## Oracle Text

Creatures you control with power 2 or less can't be blocked by creatures with power 3 or greater.
If a triggered ability of a creature you control with power 2 or less triggers, that ability triggers an additional time.

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney, Streetwise Lookout]] is the piece that makes this pile a deck. Power 2 or less that would trigger on ETB or death triggers an extra time, and those bodies cannot be blocked by power 3 or greater. Live P≤2 ETBs: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Novice Inspector|Novice Inspector]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Infestation Sage|Infestation Sage]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Resolute Reinforcements|Resolute Reinforcements]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Mentor of the Meek|Mentor of the Meek]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Vitality|Angel of Vitality]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]]. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Marauding Blight-Priest|Marauding Blight-Priest]] is 3/2 — Delney does not double it. Ayli's activated abilities are not triggered.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play after one P≤2 body is already down. Leading Delney into an empty board is a miss.
- Pay the extra Hinterland / Mentor / Inspector triggers the turn it lands.
- Do not chump it. Do not sac it to Ayli while cheaper fodder is in play.
