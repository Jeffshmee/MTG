## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Emeritus of Woe|Emeritus of Woe]] is the tutor face. Cast it, search the library, find the missing engine — a Sister still on the sideboard once sleeved, a drain, or a sac outlet. Prepared copy is a second search if two creatures died. It is not a bounce spell.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Tutor for the hole: Soul Warden / Zulaport / Viscera Seer once those are in the 100. Today the pile is small — find Massacre Wurm or Delney if they are in the library.
- Do not treat 'put into your hand' as bounce. This searches your library.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
