## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Creatures/Karlov of the Ghost Council|Karlov of the Ghost Council]] is a 2/2 that puts two +1/+1 counters on himself whenever you gain life. [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]] and [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] feed him; [[02 Cards/Creatures/Delney, Streetwise Lookout|Delney, Streetwise Lookout]] doubles the trigger while he is still 2/2 (four counters per gain). Once he is 3+ power, Delney turns off. Remove six counters for {W}{B} to exile a creature — a second exile mode that does not need +10.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Play after Hinterland so the first ETB is two counters (four with Delney). Do not lead him into a dry board.
- Exile with the six-counter mode when Ayli's +10 is still off, or when you cannot spare a body.
- He is a body Ayli can sac — usually keep him as the clock instead.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
- **Life**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Angel of Vitality|Angel of Vitality]], [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ajani's Pridemate|Ajani's Pridemate]]
- **Exile**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Delney only doubles him while he is power 2 or less. After one gain he is usually 4/4 and Delney is off.
