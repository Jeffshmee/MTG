As long as you've lost life this turn, this creature has [[03 Effects/Flying|flying]] and [[03 Effects/Vigilance|vigilance]].
Whenever you [[03 Effects/Gain Life|gain life]], put a +1/+1 counter on this creature.
When this creature dies, put its counters on target creature you control.
