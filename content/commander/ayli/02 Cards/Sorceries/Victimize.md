## Oracle Text

Choose two target creature cards in your graveyard. Sacrifice a creature. If you do, return the chosen cards to the battlefield tapped.

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Sorceries/Victimize|Victimize]] sacs a creature to return two from the yard to the battlefield. Aristocrat recursion. 🟢 SB. 🟢 Owned extra on the sideboard — not sleeved.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Sac fodder, return Delney + Hinterland, or a drain + an outlet.
