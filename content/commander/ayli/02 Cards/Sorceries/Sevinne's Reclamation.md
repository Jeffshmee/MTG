## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Sorceries/Sevinne's Reclamation|Sevinne's Reclamation]] puts a MV≤3 engine back after a sac. Flashback is a second trip. Better here than a Kykar 100-cut. Sleeved 12/09/2026.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Default: Seer, Feeder, Artist, a sister, or Bastion.
- Flashback after the first resolve if you have two bodies in the yard.
