## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Sorceries/Lingering Souls|Lingering Souls]] at {2}{W}: create two 1/1 white Spirit creature tokens with [[03 Effects/Flying. The body is fodder for Ayli's sac and an ETB for [[02 Cards/Creatures/Hinterland Sanctifier|Hinterland Sanctifier]]. 🟢 Owned extra on the sideboard — not sleeved.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Cast it when it makes a body, gains life, pays off a death, or answers the thing that stops Ayli.
- Do not sac Delney, Hinterland, or Vito to the first outlet.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
