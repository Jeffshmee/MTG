## Oracle Text

As an additional cost to cast this spell, pay X life.
All creatures get -X/-X until end of turn.

---

## Deck Role & Rating

[[02 Cards/Sorceries/Toxic Deluge|Toxic Deluge]] is the wrath: pay X life, all creatures get -X/-X. Your tokens die — that is on-plan if [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]] or [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]] is in play. Life paid moves you off +10; recoup with Hinterland ETBs after.

## Play Patterns & Lines

- Pay X to kill their board, not yours only. Sac your 1/1s to Ayli first if you need the life.
- Do not Deluge into an empty table. Massacre Wurm is the other wipe — Deluge hits indestructible-by-damage less often; -X/-X does.
- If Vito is in play, the life you lose does not ping. Only life gained does.

## Key Synergies

- **Commander**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
- **Deaths**: [[02 Cards/Enchantments/Bastion of Remembrance|Bastion of Remembrance]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Vengeful Bloodwitch|Vengeful Bloodwitch]], [[02 Cards/Creatures/Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim|Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim]]
- **Other wipe**: [[02 Cards/Creatures/Massacre Wurm|Massacre Wurm]]

## Anti-synergies / Notes

- Hits your team. Delney and Hinterland die to a high X. Pay the smallest X that clears them.
